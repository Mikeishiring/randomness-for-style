CRITIQUE OF 3-5-3.html

Clarity
1. The page contradicts its own colour grammar. The chart establishes ink hatching for
   visits and red hatching for signups — then every source card fills its
   share-of-visits bar with red stripes. A reader who learned the system from the
   chart is told, in the cards, that these bars are about signups. They are not.
   This is the worst defect left on the page, and it was introduced in the first
   version and preserved twice without being noticed.
2. The clerk's red line-work now carries two meanings (annotation mark, stamp) and the
   red hatch a third (signups). Red-as-annotation and red-as-signups can coexist —
   ledger clerks used red ink for both marks and contra entries, and the mark is an
   outline while the data is a fill — but only if no red fill ever sits on a
   visits figure. Point 1 must be fixed for this to hold.

Originality
3. The inspector is inconsistent. In the daily record the clerk circles the peak
   (Sunday's 2,100); in Sources, where the page holds its most decision-relevant
   spread — Direct converts at 6.0% against Social's 3.1%, nearly double — no figure
   is marked at all. The one bureaucratic act that earned its place is applied to one
   section and forgotten in the other.
4. Considered and rejected: more stamps, a "carried forward" footer line, Latin
   binomials under the insect names, a third section. All are identity inflation —
   decoration or copy without new information. The remaining surfaces (masthead,
   summary, case, cards) are each doing a job; at this point most available changes
   would subtract clarity, and the harshest true criticism is that the previous pass
   fixed pin placement while missing the colour-grammar error in the same cards.

Changes applied in 3-5-4.html
- Share-of-visits bars now fill with ink stripes (and an ink end-cap), matching the
  visits hatching in the daily record. Red fill is once again exclusively signups.
- The clerk's ellipse is applied consistently: Direct's 6.0% signup rate — the best
  of the three sources — is circled with the same red mark that circles Sunday's
  peak in the daily record.
- Everything else is unchanged: data, purpose, layout, chart geometry, values, rate
  row, stamp, pins, cards, responsive behaviour. The page remains fully
  self-contained.
