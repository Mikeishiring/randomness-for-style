Critique of 1-3-2.html (harsh, originality and clarity), followed by the changes applied in 1-3-3.html.

ORIGINALITY

1. The curtain is still the giveaway that this identity was assembled, not drawn. Twelve
   identical quadratic arcs, metronome-regular, ending in nothing at both edges — real
   theatre valances gather, vary, and terminate in hanging ends. The doubled trim line
   added in the last pass is cosmetic polish on cloned geometry. For a page whose opera
   ingredient leads with this element, it is the least convincing thing on the screen.

2. The columns stop being a temple at their feet. Each column got its own plinth, but
   they stand on a bare axis line: Greek columns share a stylobate — the stepped platform
   is half of what makes a colonnade read as architecture — and an opera stage has a
   floor. The one horizontal that could have fused both ingredients (platform = stage)
   was left as a generic gold rule.

3. The crest, stems, and night numerals from the last pass hold up; the header and table
   otherwise remain typography-plus-palette. That is now a defensible stopping point:
   further identity in the table (string motifs, mask icons, epithets) was weighed and
   would tax reading for garnish. The remaining originality debt is in the drawn
   elements, not in missing ones.

CLARITY

4. Gold still means "peak/best" by inference alone. The Sunday tooltip says "peak day",
   but Direct's gold 6.0% in the table has no equivalent: a screen-reader user or a
   hovering reader gets no explanation for the accent. One title attribute fixes the
   asymmetry without adding visible copy.

5. The track hairline added last pass behaves differently than specified: an inset
   box-shadow is painted under child elements, so on Direct's full-width bar it is
   invisible, and on Search/Social it outlines only the empty remainder of the track.
   Examined rather than assumed, the accident is useful — the faint empty box extends
   each bar to the common scale, showing what the bar is out of — and is kept
   deliberately. Noted here so it is a decision, not an artifact.

6. The uppercase title is set with .2em letter-spacing, which adds a trailing space
   after the final character; centered, "WEEK 01" therefore sits a half-character left
   of optical center at display size. Sloppy for a page this symmetric.

7. Everything load-bearing survives scrutiny: zero-based column scale with labeled
   gridlines, non-zero signup staff clearly labeled and drawn with points not areas,
   per-day conversion strip, same-scale table segments with the shared-scale legend,
   totals row, tooltips. No change found that would make the data easier to read;
   this pass is therefore confined to drawing quality and the two nits above.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 1-3-3.html (data, scales, figures, labels, both charts' geometry,
table values: all unchanged)

a. Curtain redrawn as fabric rather than pattern: swag depths alternate, the two ends
   gather and fall lower at the page edges, and each gather stud now carries a small
   tassel drop. Same palette, same height, still one inline SVG.
b. A stylobate: a shallow shared marble step now runs under all seven plinths, fusing
   temple platform and stage floor; the gold baseline remains as the axis edge on top
   of it, so the zero line is exactly where it was.
c. Direct's highlighted 6.0% cell gains title="Highest signups per visit", matching the
   Sunday tooltip's explanation of the gold accent.
d. The h1 compensates for its trailing letter-space (padding-left: .2em) so the title
   is optically centered.

Nothing else was touched. The report reads exactly as before; only the drawing earns
its ingredients better.
