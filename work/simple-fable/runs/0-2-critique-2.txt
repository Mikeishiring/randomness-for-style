Critique of runs/0-2-2.html (second harsh pass on originality and clarity)

ORIGINALITY

1. The fusion now reaches the daily chart (wave, sucker-textured bars, deep
   zone) but stops at its edges. The corner tentacle art is still a clip-art
   watermark: it touches nothing, frames nothing, and would survive a palette
   swap to any other theme unchanged.

2. The wave is a mathematically uniform sine - fourteen identical Q/T
   segments. Nothing eldritch or liquid moves that regularly; it reads as a
   zigzag border from a style kit.

3. The "menu board" KPI panel is three centered stats with dashed dividers;
   the menu exists in the divider alone. The sources rows open as menu items
   (name, dot leader, price-position chip) and then collapse back into
   admin-template pill meters on the next two lines.

4. Typographic voice lives entirely in the logotype. Every label below the
   title is stock system small-caps; a drive-thru board would carry more
   weight in its signage lettering. (Within a no-external-fonts constraint
   the available range is limited; noted, not fully fixable.)

CLARITY

5. Color semantics break on the rate. The KPI "Visits -> signups 5.0%" is
   kelp green; the identical concept in sources wears ketchup-red chips -
   while red everywhere else means signup counts. Same meaning, two hues;
   one hue, two meanings. This is the worst clarity defect on the page.

6. "Visits -> signups" is notation where plain words exist. The owner should
   not decode an arrow; "signup rate" says it.

7. The week's most meaningful derived fact is missing: the signup rate climbs
   steadily from 4.0% (Mon) to 6.0% (Sun). Sources get rates; days do not.
   The page shows visits and signups both rising but hides that signups rose
   faster - precisely the kind of useful detail the owner reads a report for.

8. Accessibility bug: aria-label="Visits to signups" on the rate chips
   replaces the visible "6.0%" wherever the label is honored, so assistive
   tech hears the explanation and never the number. Both earlier versions
   share this defect.

9. Minor: zone scale notes ("Visits - 0-2,100") are terse but adjacent and
   unambiguous enough; the sucker texture stays below the alpha where it
   could be mistaken for plotted marks. Both judged acceptable.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 0-2-3.html

- Rate becomes one color everywhere: a single mint-green token for the KPI
  value, the source chips, and the new per-day rates. Red now exclusively
  means signup counts, mustard exclusively visits. Fixes 5.
- "Visits -> signups" and "visits -> signups" replaced with "Signup rate" /
  "signup rate" in plain words. Fixes 6.
- Per-day signup rate row added beneath the signup counts in the deep
  (4.0, 4.5, 5.0, 4.5, 5.0, 5.4, 6.0), in mint, anchored by a right-aligned
  "signup rate" zone label; the deep zone grew 14px to hold it. The rising
  conversion story is now visible day by day. Fixes 7.
- aria-labels removed from the chips; the visible "signup rate" column
  header now serves all readers, and the numbers are read as written.
  Fixes 8.
- Wave redrawn with varied amplitude and wavelength - an irregular,
  hand-shaped waterline instead of a repeated sine segment. Fixes 2.

DELIBERATELY KEPT (change only what improves)

- The ambient corner tentacles: still garnish (1), but harmless set-dressing;
  removal would thin the identity without aiding clarity, and entangling
  them with content risks covering data.
- The plain pill meters (3): the menu metaphor ends where measurement
  begins, by choice - clarity over novelty at the data surface.
- All figures, scales, zero baselines, bar geometry, layout and reading
  order are unchanged. Purpose and data unchanged.
