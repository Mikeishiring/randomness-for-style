CRITIQUE OF 3-1-3.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh

1. This version invents nothing; it lives off devices built in the first two
   passes. The clearest cheap omission: the identity stops dead at the
   sheet's edge. The desk behind the form is flat studio purple — on a wide
   screen that is the largest surface on the page, and it says nothing. The
   form sits in an office, not in a club.
2. The mirror ball remains ornamental. Standing decision: it is the page's
   one instant genre cue and costs ~40 lines of inline SVG; it stays.
3. The obvious next disco move — animating the dance-floor strip so the
   tiles pulse — was considered and rejected. Motion on a static weekly
   report pulls the eye away from figures permanently, must be gated behind
   prefers-reduced-motion, and would be the first element to compete with
   the information. The identity brief forbids exactly that.

CLARITY — harsh

4. The chrome title is a single point of failure. It relies on
   background-clip:text with color:transparent and no fallback: in any
   renderer without that property the page's own title is invisible. The
   showiest element is also the most fragile — a defect, not a style choice.
5. Measurable mobile defect in §3: at phone widths the header cells are
   nowrap with a swatch prepended, and "SIGNUPS" plus its swatch (~55px)
   overflows its 46px column, colliding with RATE. The binding device added
   in the last pass broke the narrow layout.
6. The mirrored §2 chart keeps different scales above and below one shared
   axis (max 2,100 up, max 127 down), which could invite a false
   cross-metric length comparison. Mitigations already in place: different
   hues, every bar carries its printed value, and the axis band separates
   the halves. Explicit "max 2,100 / max 127" scale notes were considered
   and rejected as copy noise that restates what the printed values show.
7. "% = signups ÷ visits" sits in the §2 strip while §3's Rate column is
   the same ratio per source. Duplicating the note in §3 would be clutter;
   a first-occurrence definition is the normal register of a form. No
   change.

REVISION APPLIED

- Title fallback: the h1 now has solid pink ink by default, and the chrome
  gradient with transparent fill applies only inside an @supports gate for
  background-clip:text. Worst case is a legible pink title, never an
  invisible one.
- §3 mobile retune: at phone widths the numeric columns are rebalanced
  (50/50/36px), header type drops to 9px with tighter tracking, and
  swatches shrink to 6px, so "SIGNUPS" and its swatch fit their column with
  no collision.
- The desk is now a dim dance floor: a faint violet tile grid (12% alpha,
  64px cells) over the same dark ground, entirely outside the sheet, so the
  identity continues past the paper without touching any data surface or
  costing contrast.

PRESERVED

Everything else, deliberately: chrome-horizon title treatment, dance-floor
divider, faceted bars, §1 entry rules, the shared-axis diverging chart with
rates filed in the axis band, dual-series source bars with header swatches,
stamp, holes, margin rule, § numbering, and every supplied and derived
figure. The pass is small because the design has converged; the prompt's
"change only what improves it" bars churn for its own sake. Purpose and
data unchanged; page remains fully self-contained.
