CRITIQUE OF 1-1-1.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh
- "Greek myths" arrived as Greek architecture clip-art: a pediment, two columns,
  a meander strip. There is no myth in it — no laurel, no oracle, no victory, no
  narrative. The header mark is what any brief containing the word "Greece"
  produces on the first try.
- Disco is a checklist: one ball, two sparkles, an animated rainbow shimmer on
  the title. The shimmer is the stock move for "disco" and the conic laser rays
  are set so faint (0.05-0.07 alpha) they are effectively absent — dead code
  posing as atmosphere.
- The two ingredients never touch the data. Strip the palette and this is a
  generic dark dashboard: KPI cards, grouped bars, a table. The identity lives
  in the margins (header, border strips) instead of in how the numbers are
  drawn. The two meander strips are identical bookends — ornament filler.
- Gold/magenta/cyan tips toward synthwave rather than disco, and nothing reads
  as marble or inscription beyond some letterspacing.

CLARITY — harsh
- The daily chart's two self-scaled tracks share one card, equal bar widths and
  no stated scales: Sunday's 127 signups draws a bar more than half the height
  of 2,100 visits. It is honest only if the reader finds the 10px row labels
  and then guesses that the scales differ. The scales must be stated.
- Conversion — the page's only real story (a 4.0% to 6.0% climb; Social at
  roughly half the site rate) — is the least visible element on the page: bare
  12px numbers with no encoding and no visible trend.
- "Mon → Sun +48%" implies a clean climb and hides the Thursday dip, which the
  bars show but nothing asserts.
- The table's conversion mini-bars are scaled to Direct = 100%, an arbitrary
  ceiling. Social merely looks "shortest" instead of measurably below the 5.0%
  site average.
- The facet grid (both axes) on ~40px bars is texture noise, and 9.5px value
  labels on mobile sit at the legibility floor.
- The peak day is marked with an unexplained glow — decoration standing in for
  a statement.

KEPT — strong decisions preserved
- Every value is printed; no unlabeled encodings. Visits and signups stay on
  separate tracks instead of a dual-axis lie. Sources stay a real table. The
  synthetic-data badge stays beside the title. Minimal copy, dark-only
  identity, no filler prose. The Share column stays: the visits bars show size
  relative to the largest source, the share figures show share of the total —
  different facts.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 1-1-2.html
1. The bars are now the identity: each bar is a fluted column with a capital,
   so the daily chart reads as a mirrored colonnade — Greek structure, disco
   surface. The capital caps at the exact data height; the datum edge is
   unchanged and every value stays printed.
2. Myth with meaning: a gold laurel sprig crowns the peak day (Sun) in place
   of the unexplained glow — laurel as the mark of victory.
3. Scales stated: each track label now carries its range (0–2,100 / 0–127 /
   0–6%), and both tracks sit on their own baseline line (a stylobate).
4. Conversion encoded: a small bar on a shared 0–6% scale under each day's
   rate makes the week-long climb visible at a glance.
5. The table's conversion bars are redrawn on a true 0–6% axis with a tick at
   the 5.0% site average, captioned once below the table.
6. The Thursday dip is named in the visits KPI sub-line next to the +48%.
7. The title's shimmer animation is removed in favor of a static gold-foil
   gradient; the laser rays are raised to visible strength; the horizontal
   facet stripes are dropped from the daily bars (vertical flutes only), and
   the smallest mobile labels are raised off the legibility floor.

Purpose, figures, layout order and all data values are unchanged.
