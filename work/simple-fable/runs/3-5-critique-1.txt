CRITIQUE OF 3-5-1.html

Originality
1. The identity is unevenly applied. Victorian insect collecting lives only in the
   three source cards; the largest element on the page — the daily chart — is a
   generic hatched bar chart that any "antique" theme could own. The 3px circles on
   the bar tops were meant as specimen pins but are far too small to read as anything.
2. Much of the surface is stock antiquarianism rather than the two named ingredients:
   parchment, diagonal hatching, and a printer's fleuron (❦). The fleuron is borrowed
   decoration doing no work; it belongs to neither bureaucracy nor entomology.
3. Bureaucracy is present only as nouns (FORM W-01, a stamp, numbered sections) but it
   never acts. Clerks mark records — yet nothing on the page is checked, circled, or
   approved. The summary names Sunday as the peak day while the record itself carries
   no such mark.
4. The collection has no case. Victorian specimens sit pinned inside bordered,
   labelled drawers; here the source cards get borders and pins while the chart floats
   unframed on the sheet.

Clarity
5. The muted label ink (#6f6148) runs light at 10–11px on parchment; marginal contrast
   on the row labels, day labels, and summary captions.
6. Nothing ties the summary's "Peak day — Sunday" back to the chart; the reader must
   re-derive it from seven value labels.
7. Sound and kept: two separate panels instead of a dual-axis chart, a value on every
   bar, the labelled signup-rate row, dotted ledger leaders, the SYNTHETIC DATA stamp
   as a working caveat, and the beetle/moth/ant mapping to Direct/Search/Social.

Changes applied in 3-5-2.html
- Removed the fleuron divider; spacing alone separates masthead from summary.
- The daily record now sits inside a bordered specimen case on the same ground as the
  source cards — the drawer the week is pinned into.
- The visits bars are properly pinned: a shaft passes through each bar top to a head
  above it, with the value label above the pin. The identity now sits on the data
  instead of beside it. Signup bars stay plain to avoid doubling the noise.
- A red clerk's ellipse circles Sunday's 2,100 — the inspector's mark that connects
  the record to the "Peak day" summary entry without adding a word.
- Darkened the muted label ink (#6f6148 → #5e5137) for small-size contrast.
- Everything else is unchanged: data, scales, per-bar values, rate row, stamp, cards,
  share bars, responsive behaviour.
