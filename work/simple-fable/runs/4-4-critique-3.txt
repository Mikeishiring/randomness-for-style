Critique of 4-4-3.html

ORIGINALITY — coherent now, and a little complacent. The timing-sheet-as-folio
grammar finally reads on the surface (fastest-lap gold, flag defined by reuse,
finish line closing the page), but the composition is static: three equal boxes
stacked, two sections in the identical register, nothing expressing pace except
bar length. Motorsport still contributes symbols more than structure. The KPI
band is the most generic zone on the page — four striped tiles that could front
any dashboard; it earns its place by working, not by belonging. The plaque's
double border is near-invisible at its size. The Didot/condensed-italic pairing
— the identity's best fused device — is used correctly but timidly.

CLARITY — three findings, one of them real.
1. Real: the bar fills end against a track that is only slightly darker than
   they are dim; the reading edge — the one pixel that carries the value — is
   the least defined part of the mark. On a timing sheet the mark's edge IS the
   datum.
2. Semantic: day and source names are plain data cells; they are row headers
   and screen readers should announce them as such.
3. Named, not fixed: bars are scaled per table, so Sunday's full-track 2,100
   and Direct's full-track 5,240 are the same length one section apart. This
   cross-section echo of the defect fixed in v3 is accepted deliberately:
   days and week-totals are different units of analysis, each bar sits in a
   visible full-length track that declares its own scale, and forcing a shared
   scale would crush the daily resolution the owner reads most. The exact
   figures sit beside every bar. A harsher fix would cost more clarity than it
   buys.

Also weighed and rejected: a share-of-week column for sources (derivable,
adds a fourth number per row for little gain); totals rows with double rules
(would duplicate the KPI band); any further emphasis on Sunday (tint + flag +
gold figure is already the ceiling).

STRONG DECISIONS PRESERVED: everything structural from v3 — the ledger
grammar, color roles (green bar = visits, gold bar = signups, gold figure =
best/peak), fastest-lap convention, flag tied to the Peak day KPI, finish-line
close, plaque, livery stripes, palette, minimal copy, no JavaScript, system
fonts.

CHANGES MADE (surgical; no restructure is justified this round):
- Reading edge: every bar fill gets a 2px ivory tip at its end, so the value
  position snaps against the track in both bar colors. Serves legibility
  first; the streamlined edge is the motorsport note, earned by function.
- Track contrast nudged up one step so the 0-to-max range each bar lives in
  is unambiguous — this is also what licenses the per-table scaling above.
- Day and source names are now th scope="row" (announced as row headers);
  visual styling unchanged.
- Plaque inner border strengthened from soft gold to gold so the double ring
  survives at rendered size.
- Nothing else touched. Same purpose, same supplied figures, still fully
  self-contained: no external requests, no scripts, no linked assets.
