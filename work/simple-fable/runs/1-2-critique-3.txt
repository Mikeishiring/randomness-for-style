CRITIQUE — run 1-2-3 (Agora Grill, Week 01), third pass

THE MISS ALL PREVIOUS PASSES SHOULD HAVE CAUGHT

1. The stylesheet declares a global rule: svg{display:block;width:100%;height:auto}.
   It was written for the chart but it also matches the 64px header logo, whose
   width="64" attribute loses to CSS width:100%. The logo therefore does not
   render at its designed size: as a flex item with a percentage width, an
   aspect-ratio'd viewBox and no intrinsic size, it inflates against the brand
   container instead of sitting at 64px beside the wordmark. Two "harsh" passes
   critiqued epithet phrasing while a sizing defect sat in the header. That is
   the clarity failure of this iteration history: critique aimed at taste, not
   at verification.

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

2. The two-voice system (Impact = menu board, Georgia italic = myth) now runs
   through sources and chart, but the KPI strip still speaks only one voice.
   Acceptable: tiles are the densest surface and extra voice there would mean
   extra words, which the brief's clarity constraint outweighs.
3. The peak star appears once, in the chart, while "Best day — Sunday" states the
   same fact in the KPI strip with no shared mark. Same fact, two unlinked
   presentations — a missed chance for the identity to do connective work.
4. Otherwise the identity has reached its sensible depth for this format: frieze,
   logo, epithets, summit, leaders, double rule. Pushing further (patterned
   fills, illustrated gods, ticket die-cuts) would start taxing the report.

CLARITY — harsh read

5. "the summit" is blue while its star is gold — one annotation, two hues. Gold
   text would fail contrast on cream (~1.9:1), so blue (the myth-voice color) is
   the correct, if slightly impure, choice. No change.
6. Dotted leaders, thin signup bars, share suffixes, labelled best-day numbers:
   all landed; no regressions found on re-reading the markup.

PRESERVED (strong decisions, kept deliberately)

- All figures, chart geometry, same-scale signups segments, numeric table,
  section header system, epithets, summit annotation, leaders, double rule,
  palette, responsive rules.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 1-2-4 (one pass, each justified)

a. Scoped the chart's sizing rule to .chart-card svg so the header logo (and any
   future inline icon) renders at its attribute size. Defect fix; no visual
   intent changed anywhere else. (1)
b. The gold star now also precedes "Sunday" in the best-day tile: one mark, one
   meaning, in both places the peak is stated. Links summary to evidence without
   a word of copy. (3)

CHANGES CONSIDERED AND REJECTED

- Recoloring KPI emphasis (+48%, +123%) away from red: red is the page accent,
  not a conversion-only code; churn without gain — rejected.
- Shortening the signups legend: the long phrase is what makes a subtle encoding
  legible — rejected.
- Any further identity layering: diminishing returns reached; the remaining
  risk is decoration without information — rejected.

If a further pass is requested, the correct result is likely an identical file:
the report now says everything the data supports, and both ingredients work in
every section where they can earn their place.
