CRITIQUE OF 5-1-3.html

Originality — the ingredients still stop short of the lettering.
- Three passes in, every disco signal remains non-typographic: a color pair,
  a ball, some glints. Disco's most recognizable print signature — echoed,
  offset display type trailing behind the headline — is absent. The H1 is
  still a gradient-clip, the single most common neon-page trick, exactly as
  it was in version one.
- The composition is the stock single-column stack. Assessed deliberately:
  totals → days → sources is the correct reading order for a weekly report,
  and a more exotic composition would trade comprehension for novelty, which
  the brief forbids. Kept — but it caps how original the page can claim to
  be, and that cap is acknowledged rather than disguised.
- The depth story is asymmetric. The page darkens downward and the lower
  card sits deeper, yet the mirror-light flecks sparkle at full strength at
  every depth. Light that should fade with depth doesn't.
- The Sunday emphasis stops at the bars: the day row still renders "Sun" in
  the same dim grey as a Tuesday nobody cares about. The emphasized column
  isn't yet a column.

Clarity — no new defects introduced by v3; residuals named.
- The nested pink source bar reads correctly under both plausible
  interpretations: as signups on the shared absolute scale, and — relative
  to its own cyan bar — as the conversion fraction. The two coincide by
  construction (s/v), so there is no wrong reading to fall into.
- Residual and accepted: chart labels bottom out at 10.5px on narrow
  phones; "Conv" is terse but matches the KPI's "Visit → signup"; lanes are
  max-scaled with the absolute numbers adjacent.
- Considered and rejected on clarity grounds: glossy-floor bar reflections
  (the mirror-floor disco trope). A faded mirrored bar below a baseline
  invites a negative-value misread; declined.

Kept — unchanged and still right.
- The aligned day panels with direct labels; peak-day glow; KPI trio;
  nested-signups source lanes in a semantic table; flecks, faceted ball,
  dance-floor baselines; synthetic badge; sr-only daily table; all figures.

Changes applied in 5-1-4.html — three small, each closing a named gap.
1. Disco echo type: the title now trails two offset echoes behind it, pink
   then cyan — the era's display-type signature, done with aria-hidden
   copies so assistive tech still reads "Week 01" once.
2. The peak column completes: "Sun" in the day row brightens to match its
   emphasized bars.
3. Light fades with depth: the two flecks lowest on the page blink dimmer
   than those near the surface.
Data, figures, purpose and all other structure unchanged; the page remains
fully self-contained.
