Critique of runs/0-2-3.html (third harsh pass on originality and clarity)

ORIGINALITY

1. The corner tentacle graphic has now survived two critiques as admitted
   garnish. That is no longer restraint; it is avoidance. A pasted corner
   sticker at opacity .32 is the weakest element on the page: it belongs to
   no edge of the composition, overlaps nothing, means nothing. Meanwhile the
   page's own best idea - things reaching up from below the waterline - is
   begging to become the page frame itself. The chart already floats its data
   above a deep; the page does not.

2. The pill meters remain admin-template, for the third version running. The
   defense (clarity over novelty at the data surface) is accepted once more,
   and for the same reason the deep zone stays free of decorative menace:
   silhouettes behind bars would be the first step toward unreadable. The
   identity's remaining development must happen outside the data fields.

3. The wave, suckers, menu board, dot leaders and checker all hold up. The
   fusion is no longer garnish in the charts; it is garnish only in the
   ambient art - which is exactly what this pass fixes.

CLARITY

4. The worst remaining defect is invisible to sighted readers: the daily
   chart exposes twenty-one bare numbers (seven visits, seven counts, seven
   rates) in three separate grids, with the day names in a fourth. A screen
   reader hears "1,420 1,600 1,780 ... Mon Tue ... 57 4.0% 72 4.5% ..." with
   no association between day and value. The visual design communicates
   structure that the markup withholds.

5. The per-day rates are set at 9.5px - the smallest text on the page, below
   a comfortable floor even at strong contrast, and they carry the week's
   most interesting fact.

6. Everything else re-checked and standing: one hue per meaning (mustard
   visits, ketchup signup counts, mint rates), plain-word labels, in-place
   zone scales, value-labeled zero-baseline bars, visible peak-day emphasis,
   the thin/thick meter distinction. No further clarity defects found that a
   change would not worsen.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 0-2-4.html

- The corner sticker is gone. In its place, a fixed horizon strip spans the
  bottom of the viewport: five tentacle silhouettes of varied height, curl
  and weight rising from below the page, dim kelp on abyss, behind all
  content. The page now does what its own chart does - the report floats
  above the deep - and the identity frames the composition instead of
  decorating one corner. Addresses 1 while respecting 2: no ink enters any
  data field.
- The visits grid and the deep grid each become a labeled image for
  assistive tech (role="img" with the full day-by-day figures in the label:
  "Mon 1,420 ... Sun 2,100" and "Mon 57, 4.0% ... Sun 127, 6.0%"), with
  their painted children hidden from the tree; the now-redundant day-name
  row is hidden too. Non-visual readers get the same associations sighted
  readers get. Fixes 4.
- Per-day rates raised from 9.5px to 10px. Fixes 5.

DELIBERATELY KEPT

- Pill meters, clean deep zone, all figures, scales, bar geometry, layout,
  copy and reading order. Purpose and data unchanged.
