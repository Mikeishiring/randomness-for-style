Critique of 0-5-3.html

ORIGINALITY — the vocabulary is complete; the temptation now is excess.

1. The collection is finally plural and the fusion reaches every surface:
   pinned specimens on the cards, seals on the peaks, pins ending the
   share bars, curls on the rules. The honest harsh reading is that the
   identity is now pastiche executed well rather than invention — default
   Georgia with tracked uppercase is the most generic possible "Victorian"
   typography. The authentic next move (italic Latin binomials under each
   specimen, a collector's key) was considered and rejected: fake taxonomy
   is decorative filler text that adds zero information and would start
   competing with the figures. Restraint is the correct reading of the
   brief here, so the typography stands.
2. The Search emblem is compositionally a shrunken sibling of the header
   moth. Examined and kept: the repetition now reads as family resemblance
   within one engraver's plate style, and a more exotic third wing-form
   would buy novelty at the cost of coherence.
3. The rate percentages are set in the teal annotation color beneath a
   brass chart. Examined and kept: teal-deep is consistently the page's
   annotation voice (section heads, curls), which correctly frames rate as
   commentary spanning both metrics rather than visits data.

CLARITY — three real faults remain, two of them mine from v2/v3.

4. Phone width is the genuine failure. The 700-unit chart viewBox scales
   to ~49% at 375px, rendering value labels at ~6px and axis labels at
   ~5px — unreadable. Every version so far has shipped this.
5. The wax seal ring introduced in v3 marks the peak in both charts, but
   nothing on the page says what a red ring means. An unexplained symbol
   is a clarity debt; a legend line would be more copy than the fix needs.
6. The chart aria-labels say "rising to a sealed peak" — in-joke flavor in
   an accessibility string is noise to a screen-reader user.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 0-5-4.html (purpose, data, scales, layout unchanged):
- Chart cases scroll horizontally on narrow screens: the SVG keeps a
  560px minimum width (labels stay ~10px+ rendered) and the case gets
  overflow-x auto. The drawer slides instead of the figures shrinking to
  dust; days are sequential, so the scroll direction is natural.
- The peak tile's label now opens with the same wax seal ring glyph used
  in the charts, closing the symbol loop without a legend: the tile names
  the peak, the ring shows where it sits.
- Both aria-labels reworded to plain statements of the data ("Monday
  1,420 rising to Sunday's peak of 2,100").
Nothing else changed: the remaining critiques above are judged better
served by restraint than by another layer of ornament.
