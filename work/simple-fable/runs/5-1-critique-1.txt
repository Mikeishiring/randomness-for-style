CRITIQUE OF 5-1-1.html

Originality — weak.
- It is a stock neon dashboard. Dark background, cyan/magenta accents, glassy
  rounded cards: remove the 46px ball and nothing on the page says either
  deep sea or disco. The identity is a palette, not a design.
- The two ingredients never combine. Cyan = ocean, magenta = disco is a color
  split; no single element belongs to both worlds, so the brief's "combine"
  is answered with "place side by side".
- The disco ball is literal and inert: a CSS gradient circle with grid lines
  reads as a generic sphere, and its "spin" is only a box-shadow crossfade.
  Disco's actual signature — a faceted mirror scattering spots of light —
  is absent.
- Bioluminescence is reduced to the laziest trope available: five blurred
  dots drifting in the background, unconnected to the data, with no depth
  gradient and no luminous behavior in the content itself.
- The bar glow via feGaussianBlur is the cheapest "make it glow" move, and
  it costs clarity (below).

Clarity — one real bug, several soft spots.
- Critical: all chart text lives in a 700-unit SVG viewBox, so it scales
  with the container. At a 375px phone width the 13px labels render at
  roughly 7px — illegible. The chart fails on mobile.
- feGaussianBlur fuzzes the ends of the bars. Bar-length comparison is the
  entire job of the chart; softened ends undercut it.
- The mobile sources grid hides the signups column — useful detail deleted
  instead of reflowed.
- The shared day labels float between the two panels and belong to neither
  at a glance; the signups panel has no adjacent day reference.
- Minor: source lanes are scaled to the max (Direct = 100%), which could be
  misread as share of total; the adjacent absolute numbers mitigate this.

Kept — these were right.
- Two aligned panels instead of a dual-axis chart; direct value labels with
  no gridline clutter; the KPI trio including derived conversion; per-source
  conversion; the synthetic-data badge; minimal copy; the sr-only data table.
- All figures, structure and purpose are unchanged.

Changes applied in 5-1-2.html.
1. Daily chart rebuilt in HTML/CSS: labels are real pixels and stay legible
   at every width; bar edges are crisp (halo via box-shadow, no blur); each
   day label sits in its own column between the two bars it names.
2. A fusion motif replaces the particle trope: mirror-ball flecks — small
   angled glints of cyan and pink — drift and blink through the water column,
   one element carrying both ingredients at once. The header ball is now a
   faceted SVG mirror ball with lit facets.
3. Dance-floor baselines: each chart panel stands on a glowing cyan-to-
   magenta strip instead of a grey hairline.
4. Depth gradient: the page shades from a faintly sunlit top to abyss at
   the bottom.
5. The sources grid keeps every column on mobile; it shrinks instead of
   hiding data.
6. The title gradient gets a slow shimmer sweep; all motion is disabled
   under prefers-reduced-motion.
