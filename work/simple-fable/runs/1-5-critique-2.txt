CRITIQUE OF 1-5-2.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh reading

1. The amphora ghost was the right instinct, executed timidly. It is stamped
   identically on all three plates, and at 13% opacity behind a large insect it
   risks reading as a stain rather than a vessel — the beetle occludes almost
   the whole vase, leaving only the lip and handles. Considered for this pass:
   a different Greek vessel per source (kylix for Athena, krater for Echo —
   both apt: the owl kylix, the communal mixing bowl). Rejected: at this size
   the short, wide kylix and krater bowls sit entirely behind the spread wings,
   so the variation would be invisible, and uniform mounts legitimately read as
   a museum series. The judgment stands, but it should be on record that the
   motif is near the floor of legibility.

2. The Greek ingredient still never touches the data marks. The meander now
   recurs as section rules, the vases sit behind specimens, but the daily chart
   itself remains generic lollipops — the identity lives at the edges of the
   figure, not in it. Considered: amphora-shaped pin heads, insect-silhouette
   marks. Rejected: novelty marks at 9–10px would cost instant readability for
   a conceit nobody could decode. Neutral marks are the deliberate price of a
   report that is read daily; the identity is allowed to frame, not encode.

3. Title block and KPI row remain plain letterspaced Georgia. Restraint, but
   the least thematic zone of the page. No change found that adds identity
   there without adding decoration that competes with three numbers whose whole
   job is to be read in one glance.

CLARITY — harsh reading

4. v2 fixed the conversion orphan and created a mirrored, lesser version of
   the same bug: the day labels moved to the foot, so the VISITS band — the
   first and largest thing on the page — now sits ~160px above its day labels
   with a whole second band in between. A first-time reader must either assume
   Monday-first ordering or trace a column across the signups band. The fix
   traded one long-distance lookup for another.

5. The "signups ÷ visits" definition renders at 9px in the SVG — about 7px
   after the chart's minimum-width scaling on a phone. Tertiary text, but
   needlessly at the edge of legibility.

6. Otherwise sound: both bands separately and honestly scaled, every value
   labeled, bar-scale note consolidated under Sources, synthetic tag present.

CHANGES APPLIED

- Sandwich layout for the daily chart: day labels and the conversion row now
  sit together BETWEEN the visits and signups bands. Visits reads down ~28px
  to its day; conversion sits directly under the days with its definition at
  the row head; signups reads up ~32px to the same labels. No element is more
  than ~60px from its day label, and no labels are duplicated. (Fixes 4,
  completes the repair begun in v2.)
- Definition text raised from 9px to 9.5px — the largest size that clears the
  Monday column. (Fixes 5.)

PRESERVED DELIBERATELY

- Uniform amphora mounts, meander head band and section rules, specimen
  plates, binomials, owl eyespots (see 1–2 for the rejected alternatives).
- Neutral data marks, two separate scales, labeled values, bar-scale note,
  synthetic-data tag, palette and dark theme.
- All figures unchanged and drawn only from the supplied data.
