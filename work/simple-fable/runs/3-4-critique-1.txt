Critique of 3-4-1.html (harsh, originality and clarity)

ORIGINALITY
1. The blend is mostly surface dressing. Cream paper, gold double border, a
   crest, a stamp: that is the generic "vintage official document" look, not a
   developed Bureaucracy x Luxury-hotel identity. Nothing on the page is
   specifically *hotel* — no concierge cue, no registry-desk object — and the
   bureaucracy is garnish: "Form WPR-7" appears twice and does no work the
   second time.
2. The crest is clip-art heraldry: two circles and "W·01". It commits to
   neither a hotel monogram nor an official seal.
3. The stamp is the right instinct but visually timid — a neat styled div at
   78% opacity, nothing rubber about it, and it sits politely clear of
   everything instead of overlapping the document like a real stamp.
4. The charts carry none of the identity. They are default bar charts on blank
   paper; a hotel register would be ruled, a bureau chart would sit on a grid.
5. One typographic voice everywhere: letterspaced small caps for headers,
   tiles, table heads, captions and footer alike. A luxury surface
   differentiates display from rubric; this one whispers at a single volume.

CLARITY
6. Real defect: at phone width the sources grid hides the sign-ups and
   conversion numbers (hide-s). That deletes useful detail rather than
   reflowing it.
7. The gold conversion strip is overlaid on the visits bar at a different
   scale, and the page has to confess it in a legend caption ("scaled to 6.0%
   max") — the one place it explains itself, which means the encoding is not
   self-evident. At 5px tall it is also nearly invisible.
8. The two daily charts rhyme visually but use different scales; Sunday's
   sign-ups bar is almost as tall as Sunday's visits bar. Value labels rescue
   it, but the form invites misreading.
9. The most decision-useful fact — daily conversion rising steadily from 4.0%
   to 6.0% — is buried as the last column of the table. Nothing visual
   carries it.
10. Repetition without purpose: "Form WPR-7" twice, the registry name twice.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 3-4-2.html
- Crest redrawn as a concierge bell inside the double ring: the one object
  that says hotel front desk, kept as plain inline SVG.
- Faint ledger ruling added behind both charts: register texture that also
  gives rough relative reading, no fake shared scale implied.
- Conversion made first-class: its own small gold bar beside the printed % in
  the sources grid, and inline gold bars in the ledger's conversion column so
  the 4.0% -> 6.0% weekly climb is visible. The overlay strip and its
  explanatory legend are deleted.
- Consistent colour grammar: green = visits, gold = sign-ups/conversion,
  applied to charts, tiles, bars. Gold darkened for contrast on paper.
- Mobile no longer hides data: the sources grid compresses (smaller type and
  gaps) instead of dropping columns; every number stays visible.
- Stamp inked harder: stronger colour, double impression, rotated and
  overlapping the header rule.
- Repetition cut: form number once in the header; the footer becomes a
  bureaucratic close ("End of return") instead of repeating it.
- Display hierarchy: larger title, demoted rubric line.

PRESERVED (strong decisions)
- Purpose, all supplied figures and derivations (12,480 / 624 / 5.0%).
- Paper-on-felt palette, double-rule borders, section rules with gold
  "Section A/B/C" markers.
- Totals tiles, the full ledger table with double-rule total row, the
  proportional source bars, the synthetic-data stamp as provenance.
- Two separate daily charts (different colours signal different measures).
- System fonts only; fully self-contained; minimal copy.
