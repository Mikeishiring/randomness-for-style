Critique of 0-1-2.html (originality, then clarity), followed by the changes applied in 0-1-3.html.

ORIGINALITY — harsh

1. Pass 1 decorated the template better; it did not deepen the fusion. Outside the emblem, Cthulhu and Disco still alternate instead of combining: green bars here, magenta bars there, purple tentacles in the margins. The two ingredients share a page, not a design.
2. Disco is still frozen. A mirror ball, chromatic echoes, a tiled dance floor — and not one thing moves or glints. Disco without light in motion is a costume, not an identity. The medium supports animation at zero data cost and the page leaves it on the table.
3. There is still no light source. The emblem's ball hangs there emitting nothing; the glows in the page background are generic radial washes unconnected to it. The strongest available composition — the ball actually casting light across the report — was not attempted.
4. The dance floor reads as a strip of colored tiles, which is the fix pass 1 promised, but it is inert: floors at a disco move under light. Static, it is one notch above the 2px line it replaced.

CLARITY — harsh

5. The ✦ glyph now means two things on the same page: pure decoration in every section heading (h2::before) and "best" in the Daily legend, Sunday's visits value, and Direct's conversion. Defining a semantic marker and simultaneously using it as ornament is a self-inflicted ambiguity — worse than pass 1's undefined star, because now the false definition is printed.
6. The "best" convention is applied inconsistently even on its own terms: Sunday's visits value gets a star but its conversion gets gold without a star; Direct's conversion gets both; the bar cap gets gold alone. Three renderings of one idea.
7. The peak bar's gold cap is a 3px border-top added outside the bar's height, so Sunday's bar is drawn 3px taller than its track allows — a small but real distortion of the tallest, most-looked-at bar, and it overflows its container.
8. Social's 3.1% conversion is set in dim text. Dim reads as "unimportant", yet Social converting at half the Direct rate is the most actionable number in the report. The styling actively de-emphasizes the finding a reader should act on.
9. The legend has grown to four items to explain one chart; the fourth ("✦ best") exists only to patch the convention problem in point 5. A cleaner encoding would need less legend.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 0-1-3.html

- One "best" encoding: gold color marks the week's best values, everywhere — Sunday's visits value, Sunday's conversion, Direct's conversion, and the peak bar's cap. The semantic ✦ is removed from values and legend; the legend's fourth item becomes a gold swatch labeled "best". The ✦ in section headings is now unambiguously decorative. (Fixes points 5, 6, 9.)
- Peak cap redrawn as the top 4px of the bar's own gradient instead of an added border, so Sunday's bar height is exact and nothing overflows. (Fixes point 7.)
- Social's conversion restored to full-strength ink; only "best" carries color. (Fixes point 8.)
- The mirror ball now casts light: three faint conic beams angle down from the emblem across the header. (Addresses point 3.)
- Motion, guarded by prefers-reduced-motion: the emblem's two sparkles twinkle on offset cycles, and the dance-floor tiles drift slowly as if lit by the turning ball. Data marks do not move. (Addresses points 2 and 4.)

PRESERVED (strong decisions)

- The overall architecture (KPIs, mirrored daily chart on the dance floor, sources grid) is retained deliberately: the brief rewards identity that does not compete with the information, and the mirrored-floor composition is the page's one structural fusion of both ingredients — it is developed, not replaced. The emblem, chromatic-echo h1, per-bar value labels, all figures and derivations (12,480 / 624 / 5.0%), the tapered tentacles, and the always-visible synthetic-data badge carry over unchanged. Point 1 is accepted as a real limit of this design rather than patched with a gimmick: forcing tentacles into the data marks would trade clarity for novelty, which the brief forbids.
