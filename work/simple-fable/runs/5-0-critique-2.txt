Critique of 5-0-2.html (originality, then clarity), followed by the changes applied in 5-0-3.html.

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

1. The signature idea contradicts itself. 5-0-2 claimed "luminance carries data", then applied
   it only to the new elements. In the daily chart the brightest objects are the visits bars
   (full cyan gradient, glow filter); in the sources chart visits are explicitly the dark
   body and signups the lit organ. One page, two opposite light metaphors. A reader who
   learns "light = conversion activity" from Sources is told "light = raw traffic" one panel
   up. That is decoration wearing the costume of a system.
2. The page frame is still a stock dashboard: chip header, three identical KPI cards, two
   rounded bordered cards. The ingredients live inside the charts, not in the bones of the
   page. The speck background remains random confetti any template ships with.
3. The conversion dot strip was the one data-driven luminous element, and it was tuned by
   eye, not by rule — see clarity point 5.

CLARITY — harsh read

4. Mobile failure. The daily SVG is a 720-unit viewBox scaled to ~330px on a phone, so the
   12px value labels render at roughly 5.5px. Every number in the chart becomes decoration
   exactly where screens are most common. The previous "clarity" pass never checked the
   smallest viewport.
5. The conversion dots are dishonest. Radius was mapped linearly (2.5 to 5), so dot AREA
   grows ~4x between Monday (4.0%) and Sunday (6.0%) — a 1.5x change in the rate shown as
   4x of glowing ink. The brightening is mood; the sizing is exaggeration.
6. Accessibility regression: aria-hidden on the sources header row removes the only visible
   column labels from screen readers, while the per-value unit words are display:none on
   desktop. Assistive tech hears "Direct, 5,240, 314, 6.0%" with no idea what the numbers are.
7. The lit segments are 1.6–6% of the track. Comparing a 3.1% sliver to a 6.0% sliver is
   below what length perception can do; the numeric Conv column does the real work. The
   segment's 6px inner radius against the 7px body also leaves a misshapen nub at small sizes.

WHAT HOLDS UP (kept unchanged)

- Separate daily panels with declared scale ticks; every value labeled; no dual axis.
- Lit-fraction sources bar: the absolute lit lengths (314 vs 224 vs 86 on one scale) do
  compare honestly, and the numbers sit beside them.
- KPI totals, synthetic-data chip, minimal copy, self-containment, dark-only scheme.
- The stock frame and speck field stay: replacing the skeleton would trade comprehension
  for novelty, which the brief forbids.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 5-0-3.html

a. One luminance rule everywhere — the scarcer the signal, the brighter it glows. Visits
   glow dimmed (SVG filter and KPI), signups glow raised, conversion stays brightest. The
   daily and sources charts now speak the same light grammar instead of opposite ones.
b. Mobile legibility: the daily chart sits in a horizontal-scroll guard with a 600px floor,
   so labels stay readable on phones instead of scaling into noise.
c. Honest dots: radius now proportional to the square root of the rate (area tracks the
   value); brightness still rises with the rate. Sunday reads larger and brighter, no
   longer inflated.
d. aria-hidden removed from the sources header row; screen readers get the column names.
e. Lit segment radius matched to its body.
f. Everything else — data, layout, copy, palette, structure — unchanged.
