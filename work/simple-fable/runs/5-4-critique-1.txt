Critique of runs/5-4-1.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

1. The composition is the default analytics template with a palette swap: three KPI
   boxes, a bar chart, a source table. Neither ingredient changed a single structural
   decision. "Deep-sea bioluminescence" is five background specks nobody will notice
   and a cyan gradient; "luxury hotel" is letterspaced small caps and one diamond —
   the most cliched possible luxury marker, doing no work.
2. The abyss never appears. The background depth gradient is so faint and so uniform
   behind opaque panels that the page reads "dark theme", not "light in deep water".
   Bioluminescence means points of light against graded darkness; here glow is applied
   evenly as box-shadow on every cyan element, which flattens it into a theme color
   instead of an emphasis system.
3. The luxury craft is skin-deep. The double hairline frame on panels is a good
   instinct, but there is no system behind the gold: it frames everything and
   highlights nothing.

CLARITY — harsh read

4. Worst risk on the page: the daily chart's two bands share a day axis but use
   different scales (max 2,100 vs max 127), and only a detached legend names the
   series. A fast reader can compare gold bar heights against cyan ones. Per-bar
   value labels mitigate; the legend-at-a-distance does not.
5. Series color coding is inconsistent across the page: signups are gold in the chart
   and table but cream in the KPI row; the derived rate glows in the KPI row but is
   plain cream in the table. The reader gets no reusable rule for what color means.
6. CSS defect: in the sources header row, the data color classes (.c-visits etc.)
   override the intended gold small-caps color, so column headers render in data
   colors. Header and data lose their visual distinction.
7. "Rate" as a column header is ambiguous alone (rate of what?); the KPI row says
   "Signup rate" and the table should echo it.

STRONG DECISIONS — preserved

- Only supplied figures plus their sums/rates; synthetic-data caveat kept visible.
- Two separated bands on one day axis (no dual-scale overlay lie), every bar labeled
  with its exact value.
- Minimal copy: labels, numbers, one caveat; no helper prose.
- Serif/gold frame vs sans/tabular data split; dark ground; panel double hairline.

CHANGES APPLIED (one pass, data and purpose unchanged)

a. Direct labeling replaces the legend: small-caps VISITS / SIGNUPS captions sit
   inside the chart at the top-left of each band. Fixes 4 with fewer words.
b. One color rule page-wide: visits pale cyan, signups gold, derived rates luminous
   cyan with glow — in the KPIs, the chart, and the table. Glow is now reserved for
   bar top-caps and rates (the light = conversion motif); source-bar glow reduced.
   Fixes 2 (partly), 5.
c. Sources header colors scoped so headers stay gold small caps; "Rate" renamed
   "Signup rate"; rate column widened. Fixes 6, 7.
d. Abyss developed: a vertical depth gradient (faint teal light above, near-black
   below), slightly translucent panels so the depth reads through, two more faint
   specks near the header. Ornament refined (wider rules, smaller dim diamond).
   Addresses 1-3 without adding any copy or new chart elements.

Not changed: layout structure (KPIs / Daily / Sources) — it serves the owner's
question directly; restructuring for novelty's own sake would cost clarity. The
sources rows remain divs rather than a semantic table; converting would complicate
the narrow-screen reflow for little gain on a single-page report — accepted and
noted.
