Critique of 2-1-2.html (second pass)

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

1. The masthead is still rented. Uppercase gradient caps over a dark field is
   the single most common neon-template move on the web; the lockup contains
   nothing of this page's own system. The mirror ball — the one mark that is
   actually ours — never appears at masthead scale, only as a 40px data icon.
2. The disco has the lights off. Disco's defining ingredient is moving light,
   and the page is entirely static: the "sparkle" layer is frozen confetti,
   the gradient never sweeps, nothing glints. A still photograph of a disco
   is a storage room.
3. The condition system (Overcast / Partly lit / Full disco) is now the
   strongest original element, but it carries the whole merge alone; the
   typography and bars contribute generic synthwave, not disco-weather.

CLARITY — harsh read

4. The day bars float. Seven separate wells imply a shared scale but no
   baseline is drawn; the eye re-derives the floor from bar bottoms. A
   weather strip (and a dance floor) both have a ground line — this page has
   neither.
5. A weather report leads with the high. The week's high rate (Sun, 6.0%) is
   buried inside the seventh card; the stats row restates three totals and no
   extreme, so the one number a "weather report" promises is missing from the
   summary.
6. Condition captions are set at 9px — below comfortable legibility on a
   dark ground. The words were added to decode the icons; at this size they
   barely do their job.
7. Minor, accepted: first-entry-only units depend on Mon staying in view in
   the horizontal scroll on phones. Noted, not changed — the alternative
   (repeating units seven times) was the prior version's noise.

WHAT STAYS (strong decisions preserved)

- Forecast cards with rate-as-temperature and named conditions.
- The legend's single job and its position between the two sections it keys.
- Sources grouped by measure: volume (bar + counts) left, rate (icon + %)
  right.
- First-entry units, totals row, synthetic badge, all figures and chart
  geometry unchanged.

REFINEMENTS APPLIED (one pass)

a. Masthead mark: the mirror ball now hangs beside the title at masthead
   scale — the page's own mark instead of type-only template styling (1).
b. Lights on, politely: the title gradient sweeps slowly and the ambient
   glow layer breathes, both only under prefers-reduced-motion:
   no-preference, both far from the data, nothing moves inside charts (2).
c. Dance-floor baseline: each day card draws the bar well's floor line at
   the shared zero, anchoring the common scale and giving the strip its
   ground (4).
d. Week-high tile added to the summary row — "6.0% · week high · Sun" —
   next to the signup-rate tile it qualifies (5).
e. Condition captions raised to 10px (6).

REJECTED (restraint)

- Mirrored reflections under the bars: would read as negative values.
- Themed section titles ("Floor", "Fronts"): ambiguity for one joke.
- Animating rays or bars: motion adjacent to data competes with reading it.
- No palette, layout, data or copy changes beyond the above.
