Critique of 0-5-2.html

ORIGINALITY — better bones, still a one-specimen collection.

1. The page calls itself a collection and owns exactly one specimen: the
   header moth plate. Victorian entomology is plural by definition —
   plates of catalogued creatures — yet the three source cards, the most
   natural display cases on the page, hold only a dot-with-a-needle. The
   catalog marks (No. I–III) promise taxonomy the cards never deliver.
2. The fusion is still additive rather than structural. Victorian owns the
   layout, Cthulhu owns the margins, and they only meet in the header.
   The ornament system also repeats one idea at one scale — curl, pinhead,
   curl, pinhead — instead of developing it.
3. The chart where the owner actually looks treats the week's prize
   specimen like any other rectangle. The peak tile says "Sun", but
   Sunday's bar and pin are visually identical to Monday's. A collector
   marks the type specimen.
4. The share bar is the last surface untouched by either ingredient — a
   plain fill in a track, straight out of any admin template.
5. The "bioluminescent" spores are nine uniform specks at 30% opacity.
   They read as dust on the monitor, not deep-sea light. Commit or cut.

CLARITY — v2 fixed the real problems; what remains is small.

6. Round scales, the labeled rate row, and consistent "signup rate"
   wording all landed. Separate charts per metric, value labels on every
   bar, and shares summing to 100% remain correct and stay.
7. The card needle pierces nothing — it hangs over blank parchment, the
   same complaint v1's chart pins earned.
8. Peak information lives only in a tile; marking it in the charts would
   let the two surfaces confirm each other at a glance.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 0-5-3.html (purpose, data, layout, scales unchanged):
- The collection becomes plural: each source card gets its own small
  engraved eldritch specimen, pinned like the header plate — a tentacled
  beetle (Direct), a narrow-winged moth (Search), a winged cephalopod
  (Social). Stroke-only ink on parchment, 44px, decorative emblems that
  replace the floating card needle rather than adding clutter.
- Sunday's pin in both charts gains a wax-red seal ring, marking the peak
  the summary tile already names — truthful emphasis, no new numbers.
- The share fill ends in a pinhead marker, tying the last generic surface
  into the pin system without changing what the bar measures.
- Spores reworked: six glow points (soft halo + bright core) instead of
  nine flat specks.
- Dead CSS for the removed card pinhead deleted.
Everything else is deliberately untouched: more ornament inside the chart
cases would start competing with the figures.
