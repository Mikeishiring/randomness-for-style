CRITIQUE OF 5-5-3.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh
- The identity has stopped being paint and now lives in the encodings (lit bar
  caps, radiance rings, compartment dividers, the conversion-as-light-position
  bars in Plate II). The harshest true criticism left is that any further
  thematic development — anglerfish lures on the Sunday peak, light rays, depth
  parallax, descent markers — would be vandalism: more theatre, zero information,
  and a clarity tax. Convergence reached on structure.
- The header is the most "modern-generic" surface remaining: eyebrow + big
  numeral reads contemporary-minimal rather than Victorian label. Left alone
  deliberately — the identity is established elsewhere, the header is clean, and
  restyling it risks regression for taste points.
- Craft sloppiness, pass two running: dead custom properties (--glow, --glow-dim,
  --amber-line) shipped unused. Harmless to render, embarrassing to keep.
- The ambient light contradicts its own legend in one place: the Signups total on
  the catalog label glows cyan like its neighbors, though everywhere else amber
  means signups. The system should hold even in the halos.

CLARITY — harsh
- No color-scheme is declared, so the Plate I horizontal-scroll scrollbar (and
  any UA chrome) renders light-on-dark on Windows and some mobile browsers — a
  bright slab across the abyss. A real, visible defect on exactly the device
  class the inner scroll exists for.
- Social's amber bar at 1.6% of track is a sliver. Accepted: it is the honest
  size, the glowing end-marker keeps it findable, and the precise 86 sits two
  columns away. Inflating it would reintroduce the pass-2 lie.
- The empty bar-column header in Plate II announces as blank to screen readers.
  Accepted: the bars are aria-hidden duplicates of adjacent printed values.
- No further defects found: values all printed, scales zero-based and shared
  where comparison is invited, conversion defined once, columns tied vertically,
  table semantics real, copy minimal, synthetic caveat visible.

REFINEMENT APPLIED (one finishing pass, in 5-5-4.html)
1. Declared dark color-scheme (meta + :root), so scrollbars and UA chrome render
   dark and the inner chart scroll no longer breaks the deep-sea ground.
2. Removed the three unused custom properties.
3. The Signups total now glows amber, matching the legend the rest of the page
   keeps.
Nothing else changed: markup order, all values, scales, geometry and copy are
identical to 5-5-3.html. A further pass would need to invent problems; this one
is finish work, and the design should now be left alone.
