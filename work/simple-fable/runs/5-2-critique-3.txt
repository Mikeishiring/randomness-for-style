Critique of 5-2-3.html (harsh, originality and clarity), then changes applied in 5-2-4.html.

ORIGINALITY

1. Three passes in, every identity move has been perimeter work: awning strips, dotted
   rules, background speckles, twinkle timing. The chart — the centerpiece and the reason
   the page exists — is still the most generic artifact on it: uniformly filled rounded
   bars that would survive any rebrand untouched. Neither ingredient has yet been allowed
   to shape a data mark.
2. The glow is sourceless. Bioluminescent animals carry photophores — the light has an
   anatomical origin at an edge or point. These bars glow from everywhere and nowhere,
   which is exactly how every generic neon dashboard glows. The sea ingredient deserves
   light with a location.
3. The awning strip now repeats on five surfaces; it verges on wallpaper. Accepted as
   system consistency rather than changed — varying it per surface would read as error.

CLARITY

4. A real craft defect: both speckle layers are position:fixed pseudo-elements, and
   unpositioned content paints below positioned boxes in the same stacking context — so
   the "background" plankton actually floats in front of the cards. Marine snow can land
   on a KPI numeral. The dots are 2px at low alpha, so the harm is small, but the layering
   is simply wrong: a background that is not behind.
5. The feDropShadow glow softens every bar's top edge — the one pixel row that encodes the
   value. The current design trades the most information-bearing edge for mood.
6. Still strong and untouched: the per-band daily small multiples with exact labels, share
   bars that sum to the week's total, conversion defined once as signups / visits, the
   semantic palette (sea-cyan traffic, food-mustard signups, ketchup rate), reduced-motion
   guards, and the menu grammar.
7. Remaining accepted trade-offs, unchanged from previous rounds: unsignalled horizontal
   chart scroll on phones; no numeric share labels on the share bars.

CHANGES APPLIED (two, each fixing a named defect; everything else preserved)

- Speckle layers sent behind the content with z-index:-1 (they still sit above the page
  background). The plankton is now actually background. Fixes 4.
- Each bar gains a crisp photophore cap: a 4px bright rim inset at the exact bar top
  (bright cyan on visits, bright mustard on signups), drawn outside the blur filter so it
  stays sharp. One mark fixes 2 and 5 together: the glow gets a source at the creature's
  edge, and the eye gets back a hard, precise line at the exact encoded value — the caps
  read like a dot plot laid over the bars, making day-to-day height comparison easier,
  not harder. Fixes 2 and 5, and finally puts an ingredient inside the data marks (1).
- Nothing else changed: data, geometry, copy, layout, animations, and all accepted
  trade-offs stand as decided in earlier rounds.
