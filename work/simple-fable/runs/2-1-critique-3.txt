Critique of 2-1-3.html (third pass)

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

1. The merge now holds — mirror-ball mark, named conditions, scattered-light
   rays, floor line, gated motion — but the furniture around it is still
   catalog stock: glassy rounded cards, pill badge, stat tiles. Nothing about
   a tile says weather or disco. That said, this furniture is doing clarity
   work (grouping, alignment, hierarchy); re-theming it would be novelty
   bought with legibility, so it is criticized here and deliberately left.
2. Craft slip in the new masthead: the mirror ball hangs from nothing. Its
   stem stops mid-air three units above the ball — at 34px masthead scale
   the missing ceiling is visible and undercuts the one mark the page owns.
   At 40px data-icon scale the same slip is below the threshold of notice.
3. The legend icons at 20px blur the facet detail into noise, but they only
   need to be recognizable silhouettes next to their thresholds, which they
   are. Noted, not changed.

CLARITY — harsh read

4. The new summary tile reads "6.0% / WEEK HIGH · SUN". High of what? The
   reader must borrow context from the neighboring tile. A summary number
   that needs its neighbor to be parsed is mislabeled.
5. The last pass raised condition captions to 10px citing a legibility floor,
   then left the unit labels ("visits", "signups" on Mon) at 9px. The page
   now contradicts its own stated floor; the units are the only words at 9px.
6. Residual, accepted risks, unchanged with reasons:
   - A reader may take day-bar height for the rate printed above it. The bar
     is labeled by the visits figure directly beneath, Mon carries the unit,
     and the legend keys the rate to the ball — three bindings are enough.
   - First-entry-only units still assume Mon is in view on phones.
   - The static fallback (reduced motion) loses the light sweep; the gradient
     remains, so identity degrades gracefully. Correct behavior, not a flaw.

WHAT STAYS (strong decisions preserved)

- All of pass 1 and 2: forecast cards, condition system, legend's single job,
  measure-grouped sources, first-entry units, floor baseline, gated motion,
  masthead mark, week-high summary, all figures and geometry unchanged.

REFINEMENTS APPLIED (one pass — deliberately minimal)

a. Masthead ball now hangs from the frame: stem extended to the top edge of
   its own viewport (2).
b. "week high · Sun" relabeled "rate high · Sun" so the tile names its own
   measure (4).
c. Unit labels raised 9px → 10px, honoring the established floor (5).

REJECTED (restraint)

- Re-theming cards/tiles with facet or cloud texture: decoration over data.
- Animating anything else: current motion is ambient and far from the data;
  more would compete with reading.
- Legend redesign or condition words on source rows: the rows already pair
  icon with bold %, the same lesson the cards teach.
- At this point every remaining originality critique costs more clarity than
  it buys; absent new data or purpose, further passes should converge, not
  churn.
