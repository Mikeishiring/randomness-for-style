Critique of 2-5-2.html (harsh, originality and clarity)

ORIGINALITY
1. The weather glyphs are still stock app iconography - the same Feather-style
   cloud path stamped seven times, a generic emoji-grade sun - sitting inside a
   hand-ruled Victorian cabinet next to hand-engraved beetles. The incongruity
   is the tell: every other mark on the page is drawn, these are installed.
   The weather ingredient remains the shallowest element of the identity.
2. The ingredients are adjacent, not fused. The sky strip now sits over the
   chart, which is better, but the metaphor still only decorates; it never
   explains anything. A Victorian field log would close the loop: clearing
   weather, better collecting. The page has that exact story in its numbers
   and never tells it.

CLARITY
3. The report states no finding. The reader is handed bars, glyphs and tiles
   and must do the synthesis alone. For a page whose brief is "help its owner
   understand this week's performance", the single most useful sentence -
   the week improved, sharply, and signups improved faster than visits -
   is missing.
4. The outcome metric is cramped. Signups, the number the owner presumably
   cares most about, lives in a 48px strip under a 140px visits chart, flat-
   tening a 2.2x Monday-to-Sunday climb into a few millimetres.
5. The week's clearest quality signal is absent: daily signup rate rose
   every step of the trend, 4.0% -> 6.0%. It is derivable from what is shown,
   but a weekly report should not ask the owner to divide seven pairs of
   numbers in their head.
6. Defended, not changed: the register gridlines stay unlabeled because every
   bar prints its exact value; adding an axis would duplicate them. The
   repeated "signup rate" phrasing in the three cards is deliberate parallel
   labeling, not filler.

CHANGES APPLIED (one pass)
- Engraved the weather glyphs: fine diagonal hatching strokes inside every
  cloud so the sky is drawn in the same hand as the specimens and bars.
- Added one finding line under the tiles: "The week cleared - visits up 48%
  from Monday, signups more than doubled." It uses the weather frame to state
  the result, fusing the two ingredients into the report's actual message
  (1,420 -> 2,100 visits = +47.9%; 57 -> 127 signups = 2.2x).
- Enlarged the signups band: bar scale raised from 48px to 60px max with the
  band deepened to match, giving the outcome metric legible resolution.
- Added a "Rate" row under the signups band with each day's signup rate
  (4.0, 4.5, 5.0, 4.5, 5.0, 5.4, 6.0 percent), making the traffic-quality
  trend visible without arithmetic.

PRESERVED (strong decisions)
Sky strip above the bars with the rain -> clear legend formula; register
gridlines per 500 visits; printed values at every bar; day labels adjacent to
the bars; data-ordered specimen sizes (116/108/84); disambiguated card lines
("6.0% signup rate" vs "42% of the week's visits"); museum-tag tiles; the
parchment cabinet frame; all supplied figures and derived totals unchanged.
