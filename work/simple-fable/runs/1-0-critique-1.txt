CRITIQUE — Week 01, first pass (1-0-1.html)

ORIGINALITY
1. The brief says Greek *myths*; the page delivers Greek *architecture*. Meander, columns,
   pediment — all temple, no story. The only mythic object is a lightning bolt hidden in a
   KPI tile. As shipped, the identity could be any generic "antiquity" template.
2. The Tech half is nearly absent. Dark panels and hairlines are a default dashboard look,
   not a statement. Nothing fuses the two ingredients; they are layered, not combined —
   and the fusion is the brief.
3. Columns-as-bars is the one genuinely original move, and it is underbuilt: capitals are
   two flat rects, flutes are three faint strokes, shafts have no taper. At a glance they
   read as bars wearing hats.
4. The pediment mark is clip-art grade: a triangle over three sticks.
5. The meander appears twice at the page edges and nowhere else — a sticker, not a system.
6. Georgia has no tabular figures; font-variant-numeric quietly does nothing, so any
   number alignment was luck, not design.

CLARITY / HONESTY
7. The conversion mini-bars sit in a visible track, so Direct's 6.0% fills 100% of its
   container — the strongest "full tank" signal on the page attached to the smallest
   number on the page. Misleading. The visits tracks whisper the same lie.
8. The "1k / 2k" dashed gridlines are redundant (every column carries its value) and
   cryptically abbreviated besides.
9. The sources block is a div grid with no table semantics — a screen reader gets word soup.
10. Kept honest and good: zero-based bars, the per-day funnel alignment
    (visits -> signups -> conversion), the synthetic-data chip, labeled values everywhere,
    minimal copy.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 1-0-2.html
- Type system now carries the fusion: every word is serif (inscription), every figure is
  monospace (telemetry). Replaces the non-functioning tabular-nums.
- Myth made explicit at minimal cost: each traffic source carries its patron epithet —
  Direct/Hermes, Search/Athena, Social/Dionysus — one muted word each, no explanatory prose.
- The meander gains terminal pads at its hook ends so the fret reads as circuit trace: one
  mark that is both ingredients at once.
- Columns rebuilt: tapered shafts (entasis), trapezoid echinus under the abacus, four
  flutes. They now read as columns at thumbnail size.
- Conversion and visits bars lose their container tracks: pure zero-based length, no false
  share-of-max signal.
- Redundant gridlines and "1k/2k" labels removed.
- Sources block is now a real <table> with scoped headers.
- Pediment mark gets the same two-step stylobate as the chart.

PRESERVED
Data, figures and derived rates unchanged. Layout order, KPI row with trend and peak
sublines, aligned daily funnel, stylobate zero line, gold-capital Sunday highlight, dark
marble/gold palette, synthetic-data chip, and the no-filler copy policy all stay.
