CRITIQUE OF 3-5-2.html

Originality
1. The pins are in the wrong place. The daily bars are now properly pinned, but each
   source card carries its pin on the top edge of the card — pinning the label, not
   the specimen. A Victorian collector drives the pin through the insect's thorax.
   With the chart pins and the card pins drawn differently, the page also has two pin
   vocabularies where one would do.
2. The insects remain inert illustration: they encode nothing and connect to nothing.
   Scaling them by visits was considered and rejected — it would read as a quantitative
   encoding and lie about magnitude. The honest connection available is the pin itself:
   the same pin that fixes each day's bar should fix each specimen.
3. Bureaucracy still acts only once (the clerk's ellipse on Sunday). The summary strip
   presents four totals yet ignores the oldest clerical convention for exactly this:
   the accountant's double rule beneath a totals row. The single bottom border marks
   nothing.
4. Considered and rejected: wrapping the three source cards in a second bordered case
   to match the daily record. Cards inside a case doubles every border and adds
   structure without information; the shared card ground already links the sections.

Clarity
5. The two pin styles make the reader resolve one metaphor twice; unifying them is a
   small clarity gain as well as an identity one.
6. No other clarity regressions found: both panels keep per-bar values, the labelled
   rate row, separated scales, and the peak mark; contrast was fixed in the previous
   pass. The remaining cross-panel caveat (visits and signups bar heights are not
   comparable between panels) is already mitigated by the dividing axis, separate
   row labels, distinct colours, and a value on every bar — adding scale apparatus
   for it would cost more than it returns.

Changes applied in 3-5-3.html
- Each insect is now pinned through the thorax with the same pin used on the daily
  bars (ink head, light ring); the decorative card-edge pin is removed. One pin
  vocabulary across the whole collection.
- The summary strip closes with an accounting double rule, marking the four figures
  as the week's totals — a bureaucratic mark that does semantic work instead of
  decorating.
- Everything else is unchanged: data, purpose, layout, both chart panels, values,
  rate row, peak ellipse, stamp, cards, share bars, responsive behaviour. The page
  remains fully self-contained.
