Critique of 4-5-1.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

1. The motorsport half is the laziest possible version of itself. Checkered
   strips top and bottom, a roundel, "racing green" bars with a single pale
   stripe: these are the first three images anyone reaches for. Nothing of
   motorsport's actual visual culture — pit boards, timing columns, sector
   logic — made it in. The checker chips next to "Best day" are decoration
   wearing the costume of meaning.
2. The two ingredients never fuse. Green bars = motorsport, brass pins =
   entomology, each kept in its own panel. The design is a split cabinet,
   not a hybrid identity. The one surface where they could have met — the
   sources table — got a generic progress bar whose faint 20px stripes align
   to nothing and mean nothing.
3. The entomology half stops early. The "pins" are plain lollipop circles;
   the specimen idea ends at the pin with nothing mounted on it. Plates are
   numbered but not treated as plates. The fake Latin binomials are whimsy
   at half commitment.
4. The roundel reads "01" directly beside the heading "Week 01". As a race
   number plate the echo is defensible, but it is also the single most
   clichéd borrow on the page.

CLARITY — harsh read

5. The strongest fact in the data is invisible: conversion climbs through
   the week (4.0% Mon to 6.0% Sun; signups grew ~123% while visits grew
   ~48%). A page whose purpose is "understand this week" buried its best
   insight. Per-day conversion appears nowhere.
6. The tiny checker marks under Sun in both plates are unexplained; a reader
   must guess they mean "best day". Implied meaning without declaration.
7. The "Share of visits" header cell carries a class collision (.track on a
   th) patched over with inline style — fragile, misleading markup.
8. The five-column table overflows on narrow screens with no escape hatch.
9. Minor: conversion numbers are brass-colored, borrowing the signup-pin
   color for a different (if related) quantity.

WHAT WAS STRONG AND IS PRESERVED

- Two separate direct-labelled charts instead of a dual-axis mess.
- Specimen-case frame, pinned "Synthetic data" label, KPI plaques,
  plate numbering, palette (paper / ink / racing green / brass).
- The sources table's columns and numbers: nothing useful removed.
- The roundel stays, accepted as a deliberate number-plate echo.

CHANGES MADE IN 4-5-2.html

A. Added a pit board (the genuine motorsport artifact the page lacked):
   a dark ink board between the plates and the sources table showing
   conversion for each day — Mon 4.0, Tue 4.5, Wed 5.0, Thu 4.5, Fri 5.0,
   Sat 5.4, Sun 6.0 — with Sunday highlighted in brass with the same
   checker chip used by the Best-day plaque. This surfaces the missing
   insight and fuses the two identities on one surface (race telemetry,
   mounted in the cabinet).
B. Signup markers are now mounted specimens: each brass pin passes through
   a small uniform beetle body (ink ellipse, split elytra) — the collection
   metaphor completed without encoding anything in the glyph's size. Stems
   rescaled slightly so the tallest label still clears the frame.
C. Share-of-visits track now carries ticks at every 10%, so 42.0 / 35.9 /
   22.1 can actually be read against the ruler instead of guessed.
D. The checker chip now appears in three consistent places (Best-day
   plaque, plate markers, pit-board Sunday), so its meaning is established
   by the labelled plaque rather than left to guesswork.
E. Fixed the th markup hack; the table now sits in a horizontal-scroll
   wrapper with a minimum width for narrow screens.

Not changed: purpose, data, totals, layout order, plate structure, palette,
typography, header, footer. All figures remain exactly the supplied ones
plus the same derived totals and conversions.
