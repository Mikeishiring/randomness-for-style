CRITIQUE OF 3-3-3 (harsh, originality and clarity)

ORIGINALITY

1. The design has settled into its identity; the danger now is ornament for
   ornament's sake. Pass-3 candidates were tested against that:
   - Stamp overlapping the flourish (stamps never sit in reserved
     whitespace): REJECTED. Centered layout means the overlap would land on
     the "WEEKLY RETURN" text itself; off-center absolute placement collides
     with the title at phone widths. Authenticity is not worth illegibility.
   - Gold mini-meters for the "Per visit" column: REJECTED. The page has an
     implicit grammar — counts are drawn, rates are printed — and it holds
     everywhere (score registers 1–2 drawn, register 3 printed; ledger
     visits drawn, rates printed). Meters would break the grammar to restate
     three two-digit numbers that compare instantly as figures.
   - Tassels, perforation edges, "E. & O.E.": REJECTED as cliché, joke, and
     obscurity respectively.
2. What still rings generic: the overture's four identical boxes remain the
   least characterful section, and "FIN" closes the opera but nothing closes
   the bureaucracy at the foot. Both are noted and left: every concrete
   remedy tried on paper (a signature line, a date field, differentiated
   box sizes) added fake data or false hierarchy to a page of real figures.

CLARITY

1. Field No. 04 is an incomplete summary. "Sun · 2,100 visits · 127 signups"
   omits that Sunday is also the conversion peak (6.0%) — the page's own
   headline vocabulary, established by field No. 03 and the score's third
   register, is missing from the one field that summarizes the best day.
   FIXED: sub now reads "2,100 visits · 127 signups · 6.0%", completing the
   triple that Act I corroborates column by column.
2. The cast ledger is built from divs, so a screen reader hears an unlabeled
   soup where a sighted reader sees a table. The bars are also announced as
   nothing while being the row's most prominent element. FIXED: table/row/
   columnheader/cell roles added, the drawn-bar column header given an
   accessible name ("Visits, drawn to scale"), and the decorative tracks
   marked aria-hidden so the printed figures remain the single source.
3. Day labels sit between the second and third registers, so scanning
   register 1 requires dropping past register 2 to name a column. Measured
   against the fix (duplicating MON–SUN above register 1), the duplication
   costs more than the trace saves — the measure lines exist precisely to
   carry the eye. ACCEPTED as is.
4. "The daily procession" drifts liturgical rather than operatic. It is
   established identity, instantly intelligible, and renaming it now would
   churn an already-strong decision. ACCEPTED as is.

CHANGES APPLIED (one pass)

- Field No. 04 completed with Sunday's 6.0% signups-per-visit.
- Table semantics on the cast ledger (role=table/row/columnheader/cell),
  an accessible name for the drawn-bar column, aria-hidden on the
  decorative bar tracks.

Nothing else met the bar. Both changes are additive detail and semantics;
no figure, layout, or identity decision from 3-3-3 was altered.
