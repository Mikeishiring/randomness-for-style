Critique of 2-4-2.html (originality, then clarity), followed by the changes applied in 2-4-3.html.

ORIGINALITY — harsh

1. v2's "engraving pass" was cosmetic: washes and hairlines, not invention.
   The masthead crest is still a generic ring-and-sun asserting luxury while
   carrying nothing — ornament with no duty. Worse, the page defines its own
   language (sky = conversion) and the crest ignores it: the week's overall
   conversion is 5.0%, which the page's own key names "partly sunny", yet the
   emblem says "clear". By the report's own grammar the seal is wrong — or at
   best empty.
2. The weekend band and tariff rules move in the right direction but are
   conservative; the bars remain anonymous dashboard rectangles. Considered
   and declined: weathering the bars (gradients, cloud silhouettes, skyline
   profiles) would put atmosphere inside the data marks themselves and tax
   the two encodings that already work.

CLARITY

3. v2 introduced a regression: renaming the Sources header to "Conversion"
   overflows its fixed 70px mobile column — 10 letterspaced capitals at 9px
   with .24em tracking run roughly 80px — so the header can collide with
   "Visits" below 620px. A rename without re-measuring its column was
   careless.
4. The week board is a CSS grid, not a semantic table; a screen reader hears
   all skies, then all days, then all values, row-major. Noted and kept: a
   table rebuild is a structural rewrite out of proportion to this pass, and
   every value remains reachable as text with labeled glyphs.
5. Everything else holds up: the separate-scale bar rows self-label through
   their printed maxima, the legend bands round honestly (5.4% reads as the
   5½% sky), and the weekend band states the week's shape without words.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 2-4-3.html (and only these)

- The crest now bears the week's own sky: partly sunny, the key's name for
  the overall 5.0% conversion shown directly beneath it in the stat strip.
  The emblem stops being generic ornament and becomes the report's seal,
  correct by the page's own legend. No words added. (Fixes 1.)
- The mobile Sources grid lets the Conversion column size to its header,
  with slightly tighter tracking under 620px. (Fixes the v2 regression, 3.)

DECLINED, DELIBERATELY

- No texturing of bars (2): atmosphere stays in the sky row where it informs,
  not in the marks that measure.
- No table rebuild (4), no fleurons, no added prose.
- Purpose, data, figures, layout and section order are unchanged.
