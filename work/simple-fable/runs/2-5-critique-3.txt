Critique of 2-5-3.html (harsh, originality and clarity)

ORIGINALITY
1. The engraving pass of v3 stopped halfway: the clouds now carry fine
   hatching, but the three suns are still flat app-icon discs with ruler
   rays. One sky, two hands. The remaining stock-icon flavour in the page
   is concentrated in exactly those three glyphs.
2. Beyond that, the identity holds together: the finding line makes the
   weather frame carry the actual result, the specimens are data-ordered,
   and the register ruling keeps Plate I from reading as a generic chart.
   Pushing the theme further (faux taxonomy names, barometer dials,
   collection-ledger borders) would now cost clarity for charm - the wrong
   trade under this brief.

CLARITY
3. Accessibility parity gap: both bar bands expose their figures through a
   role="img" label with the day names attached, but the new Rate row is
   bare positional text - a screen reader hears "Rate 4.0% 4.5% ..." with
   no days. The one row added for insight is the one row a non-visual
   reader cannot map.
4. Considered and rejected, to avoid churn:
   - Highlighting Sunday's 6.0% rate: the tile already names the best day,
     and singling out one value in a monotone trend would imply the others
     matter less.
   - Extending the finding sentence to mention the rate climb: the rate row
     now shows it directly beneath the signups; a third clause would turn a
     finding into a paragraph.
   - Axis labels on the register lines: every bar prints its exact value;
     an axis would state each number a second time.
   - Renaming headings or adding theme copy anywhere: the information
     surfaces are already at minimum viable words.

CHANGES APPLIED (one pass)
- Engraved the suns to match the clouds: two or three fine hatching strokes
  inside each disc (clearing, fair, clear), same ink, same weight family as
  the cloud shading, so every drawn mark on the page shares one hand.
- Gave the Rate row the same accessibility treatment as the two bands:
  role="img" with "Signup rate by day: Monday 4.0% ... Sunday 6.0%", visual
  cells hidden from the accessibility tree.

PRESERVED (strong decisions)
Everything else is unchanged: the finding line with its 48% / more-than-
doubled figures, the sky-over-register layout and rain -> clear legend
formula, 500-visit ruling, printed bar values, the enlarged signups band,
the daily rate row, data-ordered pinned specimens, disambiguated card
labels, museum-tag tiles, parchment cabinet frame, and all supplied figures
and derived totals.
