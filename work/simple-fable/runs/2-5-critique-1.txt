Critique of 2-5-1.html (harsh, originality and clarity)

ORIGINALITY
1. The weather ingredient is the least original move available: a row of stock
   weather-app glyphs (a Feather-style cloud, a generic sun) pasted under an
   otherwise anonymous bar chart. Nothing about the chart itself is Victorian
   or meteorological - no register ruling, no instrument framing. Any dashboard
   could ship Plate I minus the strip.
2. The two ingredients never actually meet. Weather lives in Plate I, insects
   live in Plate II, and the page is two separate decorations wearing one
   parchment. A collector's field register recorded sky conditions above the
   day's catch; v1 instead wedges the "sky" strip BELOW the bars, between the
   bars and their day labels, where it reads as a third data row rather than
   weather over the week.
3. The insects hint at an encoding they then contradict: widths were 108 / 128
   / 96, so the Search moth is drawn widest even though Direct leads on every
   figure. Decorative sizing that half-implies meaning and gets the order wrong
   is worse than either honest sizing or uniform sizing.

CLARITY
4. The legend "Conditions follow visits - rain to clear" is cryptic. It neither
   states the direction of the mapping nor rules out the reading that real
   weather data was involved.
5. The weather strip separates the bars from their day labels, adding reading
   friction exactly where the eye travels; on mobile the condition words are
   hidden, leaving unexplained glyphs mid-chart.
6. "rate 6.0%" in the source cards is ambiguous (rate of what?), and sits one
   line above "42% of visits", inviting the reader to confuse per-source signup
   rate with share of all visits.
7. Bug: the mobile stylesheet shrinks the visits band to 130px while the inline
   bar heights run to 140px, so the tallest bars and their values overflow the
   band on phones.
8. Bars float against blank paper with no scale reference beyond the printed
   values; fine lines (and the register look) are free clarity.

CHANGES APPLIED (one pass)
- Moved the conditions strip ABOVE the bars: the sky now sits over the week,
  day labels sit directly under their bars, and the metaphor becomes literal
  instead of a bolted-on icon row.
- Rewrote the legend as the mapping itself: "rain -> clear = fewer -> more
  visits".
- Added faint ruled register lines behind the bars (one per 500 visits),
  giving the chart a meteorological-ledger texture that doubles as a reading
  aid; printed values kept.
- Resized the specimens to follow the data order: Direct beetle 116, Search
  moth 108, Social butterfly 84.
- Disambiguated the card figures: "314 signups - 6.0% signup rate" and
  "42% of the week's visits".
- Fixed the mobile overflow by keeping the full-height band at all widths.
- Retitled Plate II to plain "Sources"; the pinned specimens carry the theme,
  the heading carries the meaning.

PRESERVED (strong decisions)
Parchment cabinet frame and double rule; museum-tag stat tiles with punched
holes; plate numbering; the dual-scale visits/signups bands with values printed
at the bars; exact share bars with numeric labels; the three hand-drawn pinned
specimens; all supplied figures and derived totals (12,480 visits, 624 signups,
5.0% rate) unchanged.
