Critique of 5-0-1.html (originality, then clarity), followed by the changes applied in 5-0-2.html.

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

1. The identity is a palette, not an idea. Dark navy + cyan glow + monospace numerals is the
   default costume of every crypto/tech dashboard. Nothing on the page could only come from
   the deep sea: no depth, no darkness doing work, no sense that light is scarce and
   therefore meaningful.
2. The bioluminescence is purely decorative. Static specks sprinkled with radial-gradients
   and an identical drop-shadow on every bar. When everything glows equally, glow is a
   Photoshop filter, not bioluminescence. Real bioluminescence flares where something
   happens; here the data's one story (conversion rising all week, Sunday peak) gets no
   luminous emphasis at all.
3. The composition is stock: chip row, three KPI cards, two bordered section cards. The
   layout owes nothing to either ingredient. The "tech" half amounts to letterspaced caps
   and a mono stack — table stakes, not an instrument.

CLARITY — harsh read

4. The daily chart's two bar panels use different scales (max 2,100 vs max 127) and never
   say so. A tall Sunday signups bar sits under a short Monday visits bar; only bar width
   hints that they are not comparable. No ticks, no axis, nothing declares the scales.
5. The most useful number on the page — daily conversion, the week's actual story — is the
   faintest text on the page: 11px #8aa3c0 on dark, cryptic "CONV" label, below comfortable
   contrast. The insight is typeset as a footnote.
6. Sources: the visits bar and signups bar in the same row are each scaled to their own
   column maximum. Honest per column, but stacking them per row invites reading the two
   lengths against each other, which encodes nothing.
7. #5e7693 labels fail contrast; empty header spacer spans are markup noise; on mobile the
   column headers vanish and are replaced by 9px uppercase labels.

WHAT WAS WORTH KEEPING (and is kept)

- Separate visits/signups panels instead of a lying dual axis.
- Every bar directly labeled with its value; no tooltips, no JS, no copy.
- The per-day conversion row existing at all, and conversion as a first-class column
  for sources.
- KPI totals, synthetic-data chip, self-containment, system fonts, dark-only scheme.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 5-0-2.html

a. Luminance now carries data (develops bioluminescence as information, not decoration):
   the daily conversion row becomes a strip of glowing dots whose radius and brightness
   scale with the rate — the week literally brightens from Monday 4.0% to Sunday 6.0%.
   Values beneath are larger and brighter; they are the story, no longer a footnote.
b. Each source is now one bar: a dim, dark body sized by visits with a luminous violet
   segment lit from the left sized by signups — the lit fraction IS the conversion rate,
   like a glowing organ on a dark animal. One scale per chart, cross-readable lengths,
   and the misreadable second bar column is gone. Numeric visits / signups / conv columns
   keep every figure.
c. Scale ticks added (develops the tech-instrument half and fixes the undeclared scales):
   faint soundings at 1k/2k on the visits panel and 50/100 on the signups panel.
d. Contrast repairs: conversion values and tick labels lifted; cryptic label kept short
   but legible; empty spacer markup reduced; mobile layout gives each value its unit
   word instead of 9px floating labels.
e. Everything else — layout, KPI row, header, palette, copy level, data — unchanged.
