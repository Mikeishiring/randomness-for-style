Critique of 5-0-3.html (originality, then clarity), followed by the changes applied in 5-0-4.html.

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

1. The protagonist still whispers. Conversion is the page's declared story, yet it renders
   as seven nearly identical dots. The honesty fix in the last pass (area proportional to
   rate) shrank the radii to 4–4.9px — differences no eye can rank — and opacity steps of
   ~0.11 on dots that small are scarcely better. "Bioluminescence as data" survives only as
   a caption claim. The fix proved the point: size and brightness are the wrong channels
   for a 4.0–6.0% range. The one channel that is both accurate and native to the identity —
   a luminous chain rising through the water column, i.e. POSITION — sits unused.
2. The sources table abandons the system halfway: the daily chart colors its value labels
   by series (cyan-white visits, violet-white signups), while sources sets every numeral in
   the same flat ink. A visual identity that applies to one chart and not its neighbor is a
   style, not a system.
3. The frame remains a template dashboard (chips, three KPI cards, two rounded panels) and
   the speck field remains generic. Third pass, same verdict — and the same defense: the
   skeleton is doing clarity work, and the brief forbids trading comprehension for novelty.
   Kept, again, deliberately.

CLARITY — harsh read

4. The week's most interesting shape — conversion climbing Mon→Wed, dipping Thursday,
   then climbing to a Sunday peak — is invisible as a shape. A reader must read seven
   percentages and diff them mentally. Every other series on the page gets a geometric
   trend; the headline series gets arithmetic homework.
5. Numeral alignment relies entirely on the monospace stack; if a fallback serves
   proportional figures, the table columns drift. Minor, costless to fix.
6. The mobile scroll guard has no affordance that the chart pans; the clipped edge is the
   only cue. Noted and accepted: any added hint is chrome the chart does not need, and the
   clip itself is a conventional cue.

WHAT HOLDS UP (kept unchanged)

- Declared scales, labeled bars, no dual axis, one luminance grammar across charts.
- Lit-fraction sources bars with full numeric columns.
- KPI totals, synthetic chip, minimal copy, self-containment, dark-only scheme.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 5-0-4.html

a. The conversion strip becomes a luminous chain: each dot now sits at a height mapped to
   its rate (24 SVG units per percentage point), linked by a faint filament. The rise and
   the Thursday dip are now visible as shape — position carries the value; area and
   brightness remain as reinforcement; every percentage stays printed beneath. This is the
   deep-sea image the identity wanted (a glowing siphonophore chain) doing honest work.
b. Sources numerals now wear their series colors, matching the daily chart's labels.
c. font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums added to all numeric text as fallback insurance.
d. The chart's aria-label now names the Thursday dip.
e. Everything else — data, layout, copy, palette, structure — unchanged.
