CRITIQUE OF 4-3-3.html

ORIGINALITY
1. After three passes the header is still a straight playbill. The page's
   opening statement - its biggest typographic moment - contains no motorsport
   beyond a thin red rule. Every fused element the design has earned (finish
   line, fastest-lap red, finishing order) lives below the fold, so the page
   introduces itself as one genre and only later admits the other.
2. The racing grammar is inconsistent, which is worse than absent. Sources
   bars race rightward into a checkered finish line; the daily chart - the
   largest display on the page, and one that literally depicts a left-to-right
   run through the week ending at the week's peak - has no finish. A device
   used once is an ornament; used everywhere it becomes the page's grammar.
3. The checker ribbons remain pure trim at top and bottom. The earlier defense
   (frame, bookends) stands, but the motif still does no data work outside the
   sources chart.
4. The "WEEK 01" date line is the one remaining element that could carry both
   voices for free - racing prints round numbers on number boards, opera
   programmes print performance numbers - and it is currently set as plain
   tracked caps, i.e., the default.

CLARITY
5. Craft risk: the "Finishing order - By visits" heading is set nowrap; at
   320px-wide screens the tracked caps plus flanking rules sit at the exact
   limit of the content box and can overflow by a pixel or two.
6. The red system now reads (peak values are printed red at their caps); the
   two daily scales are separate but every bar carries its printed value, so
   no misreading path remains. No other clarity defects found: labels state
   what bars and numerals mean, headers carry units, data is unchanged and
   traceable.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 4-3-4.html
- The week now finishes at a flag: a narrow checkered strip closes the right
  edge of both daily tracks, directly beside Sunday's red-capped peaks. With
  the sources chart this completes a single page-wide grammar - every display
  runs toward a checkered finish - turning the motif from ornament into
  system, and giving the top and bottom ribbons a mid-page echo that earns
  them.
- "01" in the date line is set on a small gold-bordered number plate in the
  condensed italic of the figures: the header's first element that reads as
  race number board and programme number at once, at zero added copy.
- Mobile heading size and tracking reduced so the sources heading can no
  longer overflow at 320px.

REJECTED
- Podium brightness on numeral I (dimming II/III): ranking is already carried
  by order and the bars; grading the numerals would break their second reading
  as opera act numbers, which is the fusion.
- Reshaping or slanting bars, curtain fringes on the ribbons: theme at the
  cost of the data-bearing shapes or of taste.

KEPT
Structure, palette, playbill title, stripe, stats row, roman numerals, finish
line in sources, conversion row, red best-of-week system, and every supplied
figure unchanged.
