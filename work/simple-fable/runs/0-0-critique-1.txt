Critique of runs/0-0-1.html

ORIGINALITY — weak. The page is a stock dark dashboard wearing a thin costume.
1. The "Cthulhu" half is decoration, not design: a small eye sigil and two corner
   tentacles at opacity .07-.10 that barely register on most screens. Remove them
   and nothing else changes — the identity is detachable, which means it was never
   an identity. The prompt asked to COMBINE the two ingredients; here they are
   merely co-located (abyss palette + sticker).
2. The "Tech" half is the default move: monospace numerals, dashed gridlines,
   glowing gradient bars with rounded tops. That is the 2015 crypto-dashboard look,
   indistinguishable from a thousand dark-theme templates.
3. The one structural idea the theme offers — depth, a surface with something
   vast beneath it — was considered during the first pass and dropped. The charts
   are ordinary upward bars; the abyss never organizes anything.
4. Palatino small-caps at 11-20px reads as generic tracking, not eldritch. The
   serif earns nothing at these sizes.

CLARITY — mostly sound, with three real faults.
1. Visits and signups are split into two charts with duplicated day axes. The
   reader must correlate Sun 2,100 with Sun 127 across separate blocks, and the
   duplication buys nothing.
2. Per-day conversion is omitted entirely. It climbs 4.0% -> 6.0% across the week
   — the most interesting fact in the data — and the flat 5.0% stat tile hides it.
   That is useful detail removed, exactly what the brief forbade.
3. In Sources, only visits get a bar. Signups (and therefore Social's weak 3.1%)
   must be computed by reading numbers; no visual comparison exists.
Minor: Sun's value label crowds the top tick; violet 13px numerals are low
contrast; the sources header row carries two empty grid cells.

WHAT SURVIVES — the stat row, the synthetic chip, exact figures with no
explanatory filler, honest separate scales, and the restrained copy. Kept.

REVISION (one pass)
- Merged the two day charts into a single waterline chart: visits rise above a
  glowing surface line, signups hang below it as tentacle-tip bars on their own
  labeled downward axis. The abyss now does structural work — the strongest
  motif the theme offers — while each series keeps its own honest scale, and the
  duplicated axis is gone. Small "Visits ^ / Signups v" axis labels prevent the
  down-means-more misread.
- Added the missing per-day conversion row (4.0 -> 6.0%) under the day labels.
- Sources rows now carry a second thin violet bar for signups (scaled to the max
  source), so Social's weakness is visible at a glance; numbers unchanged.
- Raised tentacle/sucker ambience opacity slightly so the identity registers
  without touching the content layer; lightened the dim label color and the
  violet numerals for contrast; gave value labels more headroom.
- Left alone: data, stat tiles, header, chip, palette, copy level.
