Critique of runs/2-3-2.html (Weather + Opera, Week 01, revision 2)

ORIGINALITY
1. The opera-house cross-section exists mostly in the design notes, not on
   the page. The "stage" is a 1px gold rule; nothing renders as proscenium,
   footlights, or house. A reader sees a bar chart with gold stubs under it.
   The strongest idea in the design is effectively invisible.
2. The curtain valance hangs at the top of the web page, hundreds of pixels
   from the theatre it belongs to. Detached from the chart, it decorates
   nothing in particular and reads as a generic fancy page border - the
   exact "applied as skin" failure the last critique named.
3. The sky is seven icons floating in an empty 72px band: no backdrop, no
   night, no depth. Weather still never touches the header, totals, or
   sources; below the fold the identity remains close to zero (recycled bar
   colors only).
4. Sunday's "stars" are drawn as plus signs; at 42px they read as
   mathematical crosses, not a clear sparkling night.

CLARITY
5. The stage line is drawn as border-bottom on each padded day column, so
   the single most important structural line is actually dashed with 12px
   holes. Accidental, and it undermines both the chart and the metaphor.
6. Cascade bug: `.bandlabels .zone` wins over `.zone.pit` for align-items
   and justify-content but not flex-direction, so "Signups" renders
   bottom-centered in a column flex while "Visits" and "Conv." sit
   right-aligned and vertically centered. Inconsistent geometry on the one
   axis readers use to decode the chart.
7. The honest-scale pit strips (6-13px) sit inside a 48px band - roughly
   70% dead space below them. The strips look vestigial rather than
   deliberate, which invites reading them as a rendering error.
8. In the sky key, the drizzle strokes shrink to under 1px at 20px glyph
   size and effectively vanish, so the key's low end is just a cloud: the
   key under-describes the very encoding it exists to declare.

REFINEMENT APPLIED
- Built the theatre around the chart instead of asserting it: the crimson
  valance now hangs directly over the daily chart; thin curtain legs frame
  its sides; one continuous stage line spans the day columns with a soft
  footlight glow rising off it; faint ivory stars dot the sky band behind
  the glyphs. All decoration, all anchored to the data it frames, none of
  it encoding anything.
- Fixed the dashed stage line: a single rule drawn once across the chart
  (pseudo-element spanning the day columns); per-column borders removed.
- Fixed the Signups band-label alignment (row direction, right-aligned and
  vertically centered like Visits and Conv.).
- Tightened the pit band from 48px to 36px (mobile proportional) so the
  honest strips occupy it purposefully; the pixels-per-visit scale is
  preserved exactly (strip percentages recomputed for the new band).
- Redrew Sunday's sparkles as four-point stars; thickened the key's drizzle
  strokes so the low end of the scale is legible at 20px.

KEPT (strong decisions): the stage/pit architecture, single honest scale,
printed exact values, night-sky encoding with declared key, band labels,
playbill header, totals, sources table, synthetic flag, Mon-Sun order.
REJECTED as clutter or integrity risks: act numbering on section heads,
per-day sky tints proportional to visits (double-encoding), door icons for
sources.
