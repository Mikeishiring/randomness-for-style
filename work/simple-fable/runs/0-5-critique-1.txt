Critique of 0-5-1.html

ORIGINALITY — the fusion is thinner than it pretends to be.

1. The Cthulhu ingredient is two corner flourishes, and they are the same
   flourish: one path copy-pasted and rotated 180 degrees. At 22% opacity
   behind a dominant parchment panel, the page reads as 90% Victorian
   ledger, 10% vaguely green margin. The only genuine fusion moment is the
   tentacled-moth specimen plate in the header; it is small and never
   echoed again.
2. The insect-collecting ingredient is surface decoration, not structure.
   The "pins" are gray dots floating above plain rectangles; the needle has
   no point and does not visibly pierce anything, so it reads as an
   unexplained vertical line. Nothing uses the conventions of a real
   collection — no catalog numbering, no drawer furniture beyond a border.
3. The two ingredients never touch below the header. Section rules,
   share bars, and gridlines are stock dashboard furniture with a sepia
   coat of paint (dashed gridlines, a plain fill bar).

CLARITY — mostly sound, with avoidable friction.

4. The visits scale ticks are 0 / 700 / 1400 / 2100 — thirds of the max
   value, not round numbers. Nobody reasons in units of 700. Signups ticks
   (0/60/120) are better but the top specimen (127) floats above the last
   tick.
5. The per-day rate row under the signups chart is labeled only by a 10px
   "rate" placed at x=676 — outside the chart's own baseline (ends at 652)
   and easy to miss entirely. The percentages risk being read as mystery
   numbers.
6. Terminology drifts: "signups ÷ visits" on the tile, "rate" in the
   chart, "6.0% rate" on cards. One concept, three names.
7. Minor hygiene: a redundant style="text-transform:uppercase" on already
   uppercase SVG labels; value labels sit tight against pinheads.

WHAT IS STRONG AND STAYS: separate charts per metric instead of a lying
dual axis; a value label on every bar; shares that sum to 100%; per-source
signup rates; the synthetic-data tag as a specimen label; the moth plate;
restrained copy throughout.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 0-5-2.html (all data and purpose unchanged):
- Round scales: visits gridlines at 500/1000/1500/2000 (bars rescaled to a
  2,200 max), signups at 40/80/120 (135 max); all bar geometry recomputed.
- Rate row gets a left-margin "rate" label aligned with the axis labels,
  inside the chart; the stray floating label is removed.
- One term everywhere: "signup rate" on the summary tile, "rate" as its
  short form in chart and cards.
- Needles now pierce: each pin gains a pointed tip entering the bar, and
  card pinheads gain a short needle shaft.
- The two corner tentacles are now two different tentacles, with faint
  bioluminescent specks added in the abyss for depth.
- Section rules end in small tentacle curls — the fusion reaches the
  chrome, not just the header.
- Source cards get Victorian catalog marks (No. I–III, ordered by visits).
- Hygiene: redundant style attribute removed; value labels nudged clear of
  pinheads.
