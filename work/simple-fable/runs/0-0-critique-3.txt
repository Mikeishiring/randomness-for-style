Critique of runs/0-0-3.html

ORIGINALITY — the system is forming, but unevenly.
1. The palette is quietly semantic — teal = visits/above the surface, violet =
   signups/below — and that is the page's best unification. Yet the Conversion
   stat tile glows teal, claiming conversion for the visits world and breaking
   the one system that works. Conversion bridges both series; everywhere else
   (chart row, table column) it is set in pale ink. The tile is the outlier.
2. The waterline — the identity's centerpiece — is a dead-straight 1.5px line.
   A surface over an abyss with no life on it. The inter-bar gaps sit empty on
   exactly that line: free space the motif never uses.
3. The stat tiles remain four stock KPI rectangles. Considered dressing them
   (glowing rules, motif corners) and rejected it: four repeated flourishes is
   wallpaper, and the tiles' plainness lets the chart carry. Accepted, not fixed.
4. The craft slipped in places: a stray span with an undefined visually-hidden
   class in the table head, an aria-hidden th. Sloppiness is the opposite of
   identity.

CLARITY — one genuine defect, two paper cuts.
1. DEFECT: the day chart is a fixed 740-unit SVG scaled to container width. At
   phone width (~343px of content) its 13px labels render at roughly 6px —
   unreadable. The page silently destroys its own detail on small screens.
2. The sources table's first column (source names) has no column header, so
   assistive tech announces "Direct" with no context; the decorative second
   column's th carried aria-hidden, which is invalid on a column header.
3. The paired visits bar and signups dot still show no scale; within-column
   comparison works, cross-encoding comparison is unstated. Acceptable — the
   exact numbers sit beside each graphic — noted, not changed.

REVISION (one pass)
- Mobile fix: the chart now lives in an overflow-x container with a 620px
  minimum width, so small screens pan a legible chart instead of shrinking it
  into noise. Desktop is unchanged.
- Conversion stat tile recolored to pale ink, completing the semantic palette:
  teal = visits, violet = signups, pale = conversion, everywhere.
- Small bioluminescent ripples added along the waterline in the empty gaps
  between columns — the surface gets life exactly where no data lives, at low
  opacity, touching nothing legible.
- Sources table head repaired: visible "Source" column header, stray undefined
  class removed, no aria-hidden on headers.
- Left alone: all figures and scales, the waterline chart's geometry, the
  conversion row, the sucker-dot encoding, tiles, header, chip, tentacles, and
  the copy level. No change for its own sake.
