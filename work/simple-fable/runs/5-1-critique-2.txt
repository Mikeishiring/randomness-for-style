CRITIQUE OF 5-1-2.html

Originality — better dressed, same body.
- The skeleton is still the default dashboard template: header, three KPI
  tiles, two glass cards in one column. The fusion motifs (flecks, faceted
  ball, floor strips) are appliqué on a layout any generator produces.
- Disco stays decoration-deep: pink, a ball, some glints. Nothing of disco
  reaches the typography or the composition; every label is plain system
  sans, and the H1 gradient-clip is the one trick every neon page uses.
- Bioluminescence still has no behavior. An organism lights up where it is
  stimulated; this chart glows evenly everywhere, like a backlit sign. The
  glow is ambience, not response.
- The page recites numbers but develops no emphasis. The week's two actual
  findings — the Sunday peak and Social's weak conversion — carry no visual
  weight anywhere. A report that highlights nothing is a table with mood
  lighting.

Clarity — one real gap, one hack, two soft spots.
- Sources: signups and conversion exist only as numerals. The lane draws
  visits alone, so the comparison that matters most — how little of
  Social's traffic converts — is invisible until the reader divides in
  their head.
- The sources header row is positioned with empty aria-hidden spacer spans:
  fragile markup doing layout's job, and the grid carries no table
  semantics for what is plainly a table.
- Lanes scaled to the max (Direct = 100%) can still be misread as share of
  total; adjacent absolute numbers mitigate but don't remove it.
- Chart labels bottom out at 10.5px on narrow phones — legible as real
  pixels, but at the floor.

Kept — these were right and stay.
- Two aligned day panels with direct labels and no axis clutter; the KPI
  trio with derived conversion; the fleck motif and faceted ball; the
  dance-floor baselines; the synthetic badge; minimal copy; the sr-only
  daily table; every figure.

Changes applied in 5-1-3.html.
1. Signups are now drawn inside each source lane as a nested magenta bar on
   the same absolute scale as visits, so conversion is visible as how far
   the pink reaches along the cyan. Social's gap becomes a picture instead
   of an arithmetic exercise. Numbers all remain.
2. Bioluminescent response where the data peaks: Sunday's bars — the
   week's maximum in both series — glow brighter than the rest, and their
   labels brighten with them. Height still carries the value; glow only
   points at the peak.
3. The sources grid is rebuilt as a real table: header cells with
   semantics, no spacer-span hack, and the lane column gets a screen-reader
   label. The separate sr-only duplication is no longer needed for sources.
4. Depth develops down the page: the lower card sits visibly deeper
   (darker surface, dimmer edge) and the page floor fades further toward
   black — surface light at the top, abyss at the bottom.
Data, figures, purpose and all other structure unchanged; the page remains
fully self-contained.
