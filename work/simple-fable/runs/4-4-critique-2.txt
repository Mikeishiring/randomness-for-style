Critique of 4-4-2.html

ORIGINALITY — the fusion got smarter and the page got duller. The "timing
sheet as hotel folio" idea mostly lives in the designer's head: on the surface
v2 is two near-identical admin tables with a 9px mystery dot. A real timing
sheet carries rank, pace and a fastest-lap convention; none of that made it in.
The strongest visual asset of this dataset — a clean rise from Monday to a
Sunday peak with a Thursday dip — was loud in v1's bar chart and is now
whispered by 7px slivers interleaved with text. The finish-line idea was never
developed: the checker band at the top is still a disconnected sticker, while a
report that literally runs Mon → Sun toward its peak ends with... nothing. The
header shrank into genericism: "Website Performance" in a smaller Didot could
be any brandless tool, and the plaque "01" sits next to an eyebrow that says
"Week 01" — the duplication was moved, not removed. Luxury is claimed but not
felt: row spacing is tight, the ledger's conventions (generous ruling,
restrained emphasis) are underplayed, and gold is scattered thin.

CLARITY — one real defect and two irritants.
1. Per-column max scaling puts three incomparable metrics side by side at
   identical lengths: Sunday and Direct peg 100% on all three bars, and Mon's
   visits bar (67.6%) sits next to Mon's conversion bar (66.3%) looking
   "equal". Same-looking bars invite exactly the cross-metric comparison the
   scales forbid. This is worse than v1.
2. The champagne conversion bar and gold signups bar are too close in hue at
   7px tall; signups and conversion read as one metric family. The conversion
   bars also encode almost nothing: a 4.0–6.0 range on a zero-based track is
   a few pixels of difference — ink without information.
3. The checkered flag on Sunday is defined nowhere visible; it leans on an
   aria-label and the reader's goodwill.

STRONG DECISIONS PRESERVED: the fused ledger/timing-sheet grammar, one shared
row system for both sections, zero-based bars with exact figures, the
racing-green/champagne palette, plaque monogram, livery stripes, KPI tiles,
"Synthetic data" badge, no JavaScript, system fonts, minimal copy.

CHANGES MADE:
- Removed the conversion bars (the false-comparison engine and near-zero-ink
  encoding). Conversion is now a set figure, timing-sheet style: the rate is
  the lap time, read as a number. The two remaining bar columns (visits green,
  signups gold) get more width and 9px height, so the week's rise and the
  Thursday dip read at a glance again.
- Adopted the fastest-lap convention: gold text now means best/peak only —
  Sunday's row and 6.0%, Direct's 6.0%. Values are otherwise ivory, so color
  roles are: green bar = visits, gold bar = signups, gold figure = best.
- Defined the flag by reuse: the same checkered glyph appears on the Peak day
  KPI tile and on Sunday's row, tying symbol to meaning without a legend.
- Finish line developed: a contained checkered rule now closes the report
  above the footer — the week runs Mon → Sun into the flag. The top band
  stays as the identity cue.
- Header: h1 restored to full size; the plaque is now explicitly decorative
  (aria-hidden) with "Week 01" carried once as text, removing the duplicate
  announcement.
- Luxury in the ledger: slightly more generous row spacing and section
  padding; everything else unchanged.
- Same purpose, same data, all supplied figures intact; still fully
  self-contained, no external requests, no scripts.
