CRITIQUE OF 0-3-1.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh

1. Decoration, not identity. Cthulhu lives entirely at the margins: an emblem, a faint
   corner tentacle, two ornament dividers. Remove those three ornaments and this is any
   dark-gold dashboard. The one structural idea the page owns — visits above a waterline,
   signups hanging below it in the deep — is undercommitted: the "water" is two straight
   hairlines indistinguishable from table rules, so the page's best invention reads as a
   header row.

2. Opera is costume-shop playbill. Small caps, letterspacing, gilt tile frames: period
   poster pastiche, not opera. Nothing of the theatre's own machinery is used. The curtain
   is a 30px striped awning that appears once at the top and never falls; "in two acts"
   promises a dramaturgy the page performs only in its headings.

3. The two ingredients alternate instead of combining. Gold things are opera, teal things
   are sea, and only the emblem fuses them. Swap the emblem out and the page could be
   pitched as art-deco hotel branding.

4. Gradient bars with a soft glow are stock dark-dashboard styling.

CLARITY — harsh

5. Act I's series labels are 10px rotated letterspaced captions — the weakest possible
   labeling for the chart's least conventional feature. A reader must find them to learn
   that gold-below-the-line is not a negative value.

6. Act I and Act II label the same two series with different systems (rotated captions vs
   legend chips). One vocabulary, two grammars.

7. Act II's signups bars use their own scale: Direct's 314 signups is drawn as long as its
   5,240 visits. Bar thickness is the only cue that the scales differ; a reader can
   misread gold lengths as comparable to teal ones.

8. Conversion — the page's most actionable comparison (Direct 6.0% vs Social 3.1%) — has
   the least visual presence: a small right-aligned label, with the word "conversion"
   repeated three times.

9. The daily conversion trend (4.0% Monday rising to 6.0% Sunday) is absent entirely,
   though it is the week's cleanest story and derivable row by row from the supplied data.

PRESERVED DELIBERATELY

The waterline chart concept, the playbill structure (overture + two acts), the stat tiles,
the palette, printed values on every mark, the emblem, the synthetic-data label, and the
minimal copy are strong decisions and stay.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 0-3-2.html

A. The waterline is made literal and put to work (addresses 1, 5, 9): the day band is now
   bounded by waved gold lines (inline SVG pattern, no external request), and the band
   carries each day's conversion percentage at the waterline — seven useful data points
   added exactly where the metaphor meets the axis.

B. One labeling system (addresses 5, 6, 8): both acts use the same three-item legend
   (Visits / Signups / % conversion). The rotated captions are removed; per-source
   percentages drop the repeated word "conversion".

C. Honest shared scale in Act II (addresses 7, 8): signups bars now use the same scale as
   visits (Direct's gold bar is 6.0% of the track), so each source's conversion is visible
   as the ratio of gold to teal length — Social's sliver against its long teal bar is the
   point. Gold bars are thickened so the smallest (86) stays visible; every value remains
   printed.

D. The curtain falls (addresses 2, 3): a mirrored velvet band with upward scallops closes
   the page, and both curtains get soft gradient folds instead of flat stripes. The opera
   frame now opens and closes the report instead of decorating its top edge.

E. Everything else is left alone (addresses "change only what improves it"). The glow and
   gradients (4) stay: muted, consistent, and not worth a churn that risks the page's
   restraint.
