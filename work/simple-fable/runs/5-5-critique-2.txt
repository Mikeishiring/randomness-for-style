CRITIQUE OF 5-5-2.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh
- Pass 2's one genuinely new visual — the stacked bars in Plate II — is also its
  worst idea. The cyan bar is normalized to 5,240 and the amber bar to 314, yet
  they share a left origin in one column with no axis. Direct shows two bars at
  full width, inviting the read that signups rival visits. Novelty was bought at
  the price of graphical integrity.
- The KPI row is still three identically stamped mini-plates, each with its own
  pin. The "template repetition" criticized in pass 1 was patched elsewhere but
  survives here. A Victorian collector writes ONE label card with ruled fields,
  not three tiny plates for three numbers; three pins for three figures is
  ornament inflation.
- The deep-sea ingredient has stopped developing at glow effects. Noted, but the
  obvious next moves (light rays, depth parallax, animated drift) would tax
  clarity and add no information. Restraint is the right call; this is a
  boundary, not a defect to fix.
- The radiance rings are uniform regardless of magnitude — decorative, not
  data-bearing. Left alone deliberately: sizing them to value would merely
  double-encode the y-position and add noise.

CLARITY — harsh
- The Plate II cross-color comparison is actively misleading (above). This is
  the only material clarity defect found.
- The per-day conversion row is the dimmest text on the page, yet it carries the
  week's most interesting trend: 4.0% rising to 6.0%. Underweighted.
- Minor: three separate KPI plates fragment what is really a single reading —
  "12,480 visits became 624 signups at 5.0%."

KEPT (strong decisions preserved unchanged)
- Compartment dividers tying each day's visits, signups and conversion together;
  lit bar caps and dot glow attached to the marks; panel labels and table headers
  carrying the color legend; moth wing spots echoing the encoding; real table
  semantics; every value printed; minimal copy; the synthetic-data tag; honest
  zero-based Plate I scales.

REFINEMENT APPLIED (one pass, in 5-5-3.html)
1. Plate II bars now share one scale (max 5,240 visits). The amber signups bar is
   6.0% / 4.3% / 1.6% of the track with a glowing end-marker, so within a row the
   amber-to-cyan length ratio IS the conversion rate, and across rows the amber
   bars still compare 314 : 224 : 86 truthfully. Small but honest beats big but
   false; the adjacent numbers carry the precision.
2. The three KPI plates merge into a single catalog-label plate with ruled
   internal dividers and one pin — one label card, three fields.
3. The daily conversion row is lightened one step (#9db4bd in chart and table)
   to match its interest.
Purpose, data, Plate I geometry and the overall layout order are unchanged.
