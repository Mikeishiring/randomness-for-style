Critique of runs/0-2-1.html (harsh pass on originality and clarity)

ORIGINALITY

1. The fusion is garnish, not structure. Strip the header (name, burger logo,
   checker strip, corner tentacles) and what remains is a generic dark admin
   dashboard with a mustard/red palette. The two ingredients decorate the page;
   they barely touch the information surfaces, which is where identity would
   actually earn its keep.

2. The one structural idea with real promise - visits above a waterline,
   signups hanging into "the deep" - is underdeveloped. The waterline is a flat
   2px border that says nothing about water, the deep gradient is nearly
   invisible, and the hanging bars read as an upside-down bar chart before they
   read as anything eldritch.

3. Fast food contributes only a palette and a checker strip that fades out with
   an arbitrary mask, committing to nothing. No menu-board structure, no menu
   typography; the drive-thru exists in the name alone.

4. The sources card is template-grade: pill meters that could ship in any admin
   kit. Neither ingredient reaches it at all.

5. The red offset text-shadow on the title is the single most cliched
   "fast food" move available, applied at full strength.

CLARITY

6. The "-> 6.0%" rate chips lean on an aria-label; a sighted reader must leap
   to the KPI tile on another card to learn what the arrow means.

7. The daily chart uses two scales (0-2,100 above, 0-127 below) and never says
   so. Every bar is value-labeled, so it is honest, but adjacent bar heights
   across the waterline invite a false comparison.

8. The legend dots sit far from the bars and duplicate what zone labels could
   say in place; the zones themselves are unlabeled.

9. The "best day" emphasis on Sunday (slightly brighter gradient, bold value)
   is too subtle to perceive. Emphasis nobody can see is dead code.

10. In sources, both meters per row are drawn at equal full width for Direct
    (5,240 visits vs 314 signups) because each metric is scaled to its own max.
    Same-width adjacent bars for different magnitudes invite misreading, and
    nothing visually distinguishes the two tracks beyond hue.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 0-2-2.html

- Waterline is now an actual wave (inline SVG strip); the deep zone is darker
  and holds its own label. Signup bars gain a faint sucker texture so they read
  as tentacles reaching down from the surface - identity fused into the chart
  without changing any bar height.
- Zones are labeled in place with their scales ("Visits - 0-2,100",
  "Signups - 0-127"), fixing 7 and 8; the detached legend is removed.
- Sunday peak gets a visible column tint across both zones plus the mustard
  day label, fixing 9.
- KPI row becomes one menu-board panel with dashed dividers instead of three
  floating cards; sources become menu items with dotted leader lines running
  from name to rate chip (the rate as the "price"), giving fast food a
  structural role, fixing 3 and 4.
- Rate chips drop the arrow; a muted "visits -> signups" header sits directly
  above the chip column, fixing 6. Signup meters are thinner than visit
  meters so the two tracks cannot be read as one scale, fixing 10.
- Title shadow darkened to deep ketchup; checker strip becomes a confident
  two-tone tile band instead of a fading mask, addressing 5 within the
  diner-signage register rather than abandoning it.

Kept: all figures and derived totals, value-labeled bars, zero-baseline
scaling, the burger-with-eyes logo, corner tentacles, card structure and
reading order. Purpose and data unchanged.
