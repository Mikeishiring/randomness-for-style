CRITIQUE OF 3-0-3.html

ORIGINALITY
- The continuous-form move finally fused the two ingredients, but the page
  lost its nerve about the consequences. Real green-bar stock is a
  two-voice object: preprinted chrome in a pale second ink, data struck
  over it in black. This page prints labels, rules and figures all in the
  same voice, so the medium is quoted rather than inhabited.
- The Consolas "machine layer" was indicted in the last round as an
  imperceptible private joke -- and it survived anyway, on two box numbers
  and a footer line. Keeping a device you cannot defend is worse than
  choosing it the first time.
- The ink story is incoherent: red means "signups series" in the bars and
  "worst value" in the conversion column, two unrelated semantics sharing
  one hue on one page. Blue likewise moonlights as both the visits series
  and the peak marker (defensible -- the peak IS a visits peak -- but
  nobody decided that; it happened).

CLARITY
- Red's double duty is the page's one real misreading risk: Social's red
  bar and Social's red "worst" flag look like the same statement, and
  Monday's signup bar looks pre-flagged as a problem.
- Sunday prints both bars at 100% because each column normalizes to its
  own maximum; adjacent full bars imply the two series peaked in equal
  proportion. The printed values rescue it, but the coincidence was never
  examined. (Accepted here: within-column scaling with printed values is
  the standard contract, and an axis note would add copy that the numbers
  already provide.)
- The part bands look like table captions but are not programmatically
  associated with their tables; assistive tech hears two anonymous tables.
- A document doing continuous-form cosplay has no print stylesheet -- the
  desk-blotter background and double outline would print as-is. Irony.

KEPT (strong decisions)
- Sprocket strips and green-bar striping, the form frame, numbered boxes,
  black part bands, merged labeled bar columns with printed values,
  segmented fills, glyph high/low markers, the stamp, totals alignment,
  scope attributes, legend wording, all figures.

CHANGES APPLIED
1. Ink discipline. Blue = visits series and the peak marker. Black = data,
   including the signups bars (impact-ink, printout-true). Red appears
   only as exception ink: the stamp and the flagged lowest conversions.
   The highest conversions keep their glyph and bold weight without a
   color; the glyphs were already carrying the meaning.
2. Preprint voice. Secondary chrome -- column headers, box labels and
   numbers, the footer line -- shifts to a preprint blue-grey, separating
   the preprinted form from the machine-struck figures. Rules and bands
   stay ink; contrast stays comfortably above AA.
3. The vestigial Consolas layer is deleted. One face, as one printer
   would have had.
4. Part headings get ids and each table an aria-labelledby; the legend's
   glyphs now render in the same styles they annotate.
5. A minimal print stylesheet: white ground, no desk padding or double
   outline, full-opacity stamp.
6. Revision counter -> 4.
Purpose, figures, tables, derived values and structure unchanged.
