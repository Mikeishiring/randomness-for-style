CRITIQUE OF 0-3-3.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh

1. The emblem has shipped unchanged through three versions and is now the weakest thing on
   the page: an eye ringed by tentacles — pure Cthulhu, zero opera. The single mark whose
   job is to fuse the two ingredients carries only one of them. Everything below the
   masthead evolved; the figurehead never did.

2. Identity still rides on furniture, not on the marks. Curtains, waves, ornaments and an
   emblem do the theming while every data mark remains a plain rectangle. In a page whose
   best idea is "signups hang in the deep", the things hanging in the deep are generic
   boxes. The one place identity cannot be dismissed as decoration — the encoding itself —
   is the one place it never reached.

3. The corner background tentacle is the stock "faint oversized motif" move every dark
   themed page makes. Tolerable, but nobody would call it an idea.

CLARITY — harsh

4. The page's real clarity failure is invisible: to a screen reader, Act I is three
   disconnected runs of numbers — all seven visit counts, then seven day names with
   percentages, then seven signup counts — with no programmatic association between a day
   and its values. The chart that sighted readers praise is noise to assistive technology.

5. Act II's stacked values are bare numbers ("5,240", "314") whose units exist only in a
   color legend; the conversion percentages are bare too. Color-keyed association is fine
   for the eye and meaningless to AT.

6. Beyond that, no visual change is justified: scales are honest, every mark is labeled,
   the legend is consistent across acts, the dividers now vary, copy is minimal. Further
   visual churn would be redesign for its own sake.

PRESERVED DELIBERATELY

The waterline chart, daily conversion at the surface, nested subset bars in Act II, the
shared legend, italic-rate convention, tiles, curtains, dividers, palette, printed values,
and all copy. The teal visits towers stay plain rectangles on purpose — the orderly
surface against the tentacled deep is the contrast that tells the story.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 0-3-4.html

A. The emblem is redrawn as a proscenium (addresses 1): tentacle curtains hang inside a
   stage arch, framing the watching eye, with a row of footlights at the stage floor. The
   one identity mark now performs the fusion the page is named for.

B. Tentacle tips on the deep (addresses 2): each hanging signups bar is rectangular for
   most of its length and tapers to a tip at its true end. Length — the encoding — is
   unchanged and still ends exactly at the value; only the final width tapers. Identity
   enters the marks themselves, and only in the deep, where the metaphor lives.

C. A screen-reader layer (addresses 4, 5): the Act I grid is marked decorative and a
   visually-hidden table carries day, visits, signups and conversion as associated rows;
   Act II's values and percentages gain hidden unit words (visits, signups, conversion).
   Nothing visible changes; nothing is removed.

D. Nothing else is touched, including the background tentacle (3): replacing ambient
   decoration is churn, not improvement, and the fusion now has two load-bearing homes —
   the emblem and the marks.
