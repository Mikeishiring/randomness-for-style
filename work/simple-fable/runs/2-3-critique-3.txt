Critique of runs/2-3-3.html (Weather + Opera, Week 01, revision 3)

ORIGINALITY
1. The theatre finally renders - and then the identity stops dead at the
   "By source" rule. The lower half of the page belongs to a different,
   plainer document: no weather, no theatre, just recycled bar colors.
   Half the report is still unthemed after three passes.
2. Weather is confined to the daily sky band. An identity ingredient that
   appears on exactly one surface is a feature, not an identity.
3. Sunday's sparkle stars are an undeclared fourth sky level: the key
   promises drizzle-to-moon and the page quietly adds "moon with stars".
   Harmless as an ordinal flourish on the maximum, but it is ornament
   drifting ahead of the declared encoding.
4. The valance scallops stretch with the viewport (preserveAspectRatio
   "none"): at sheet width they are ~23% wider than drawn, on a phone ~50%
   narrower. Assessed: within these ranges the shape still reads as a
   curtain edge; redrawing with slice-cropping would clip the scallop tips,
   so the distortion is the better trade and is left alone.

CLARITY
5. The same metric is named three ways: "Conversion" (totals tile),
   "Conversion" (sources header), "Conv." (daily band label). The
   abbreviation exists only because the 72px label gutter was sized before
   the word; the gutter should serve the label, not the reverse.
6. Mobile band labels render at 7.5px - below comfortable legibility for
   the row that tells a reader what the gold strips are.
7. The source signup bars are honest but unanchored: at 1.6% width,
   Social's gold bar is a floating dot with no visible origin, so its
   length can't be judged. The rows lack the one cue the daily chart has -
   a drawn baseline.
8. The source names sit in a bare text column while the daily chart gives
   every category a sky; the asymmetry reads as an unfinished section
   rather than a quieter one.

REFINEMENT APPLIED
- Extended the night sky below the fold: each source carries a small sky
  glyph on the declared encoding (visits, low to high, relative within the
  section) - Social drizzle, Search moon behind cloud, Direct clear moon.
  Same ingredient, same key, second surface; glyphs are decorative to
  assistive tech since the exact values are printed.
- Anchored the source bars: a gold hairline rail now marks the shared
  origin of both series in each row, echoing the stage line rotated
  upright, so even the 1.6% Social sliver has a visible baseline.
- Renamed "Conv." to "Conversion" on the desktop band label (label gutter
  widened to fit the word); mobile keeps the abbreviation where the full
  word cannot fit, via a breakpoint swap.
- Raised mobile band labels from 7.5px to 8px with tightened tracking and
  a slightly wider mobile gutter.

KEPT: stage/pit architecture, single pixels-per-visit scale, printed
values, sky key, valance/legs/stars/footlights, playbill header, totals,
synthetic flag, Mon-Sun order, Sunday's stars (documented above as an
accepted flourish). REJECTED: an operatic seating-map arc for sources
(harder to compare than bars), mapping source glyphs to conversion instead
of visits (would contradict the declared key), renaming metrics to theatre
vocabulary like "attendance" (purpose must stay legible).
