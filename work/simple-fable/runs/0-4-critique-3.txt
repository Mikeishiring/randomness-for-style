CRITIQUE OF 0-4-3.html

Originality
- Third look: ornament, watermark, plaques, register — the identity now frames
  the data but still never touches the data marks where it matters. The one
  place the two ingredients genuinely meet (gold as the house's take:
  conversion) was realized in the register and then abandoned in the by-day
  panel, where conversion — the week's actual story, 4.0% rising to 6.0% — is
  a row of plain text.
- The page never says what it reports. "Week 01" of what? The fiction (the
  hotel marque) crowds out the fact (a website's week). An identity that
  omits its subject is vanity.

Clarity
- The conversion trend is the most actionable fact on the page and it is
  invisible as shape. Sources got comparison meters; days did not. One
  quantity, two treatments, and the treatment is missing exactly where the
  movement is.
- 9px type (the depth soundings, the register headers) sits below a
  comfortable legibility floor for the least-skimmable content on the page.
- Register semantics are sloppy at the edges: an unnamed empty header cell
  exposed to assistive tech, a stray aria-hidden="false", a meter marked up
  as <i>.

Kept (strong decisions)
Everything structural: lanes with baselines and labeled gridlines, the
register with its single header row and ruled entries, share-of-week bars,
from-zero conversion meters, accessible per-day labels, the restrained
identity load. No new ornament is added this pass — v3 already carries as
much identity as the data can bear, and the remaining gains are in making
the data itself speak.

Changes applied in 0-4-4.html
1. The tag line now carries the subject with the caveat: "Site performance ·
   Synthetic data". Two necessary facts, one quiet line.
2. By-day conversion values gain the same from-zero underline meters as the
   register (6.0% = full length), making the weekly rise visible. Widths use
   min(percent, px) pairs sharing one ratio, so proportions stay truthful at
   every viewport width instead of clamping.
3. Small type raised from 9px to 10px (gridline soundings, register headers,
   mobile figure labels).
4. Semantics cleanup: the empty track header cell is hidden from assistive
   tech, the stray aria-hidden="false" is removed, meters are <span>s.
No other changes; data, scales, structure and identity are unchanged.
