Critique of 5-2-2.html (harsh, originality and clarity), then changes applied in 5-2-3.html.

ORIGINALITY

1. The identity is still trim-deep. The awning is a 4px sticker on the same stock glass
   cards; strip the palette and this is any dashboard. No component's *form* is shaped by
   either ingredient — the chart would survive a rebrand untouched.
2. The plankton pulse animates every speckle in unison. Real bioluminescence twinkles
   asynchronously; a whole-sky fade in lockstep reads as a screen glitch, not living light.
   This is the weakest rendering of the sea ingredient on the page.
3. The header is the largest element and carries the least identity per pixel. It floats
   unanchored while everything below it now speaks a menu-board grammar (awning strips,
   dotted leaders); the page's two visual languages split at the fold line.
4. A strength the previous critique never named, and which must be protected from any
   decorative recoloring: the palette is semantic, not cosmetic. Sea-cyan measures traffic,
   food-mustard measures signups (the orders), ketchup-coral the rate. That mapping is the
   actual fusion of the two ingredients and is worth more than any added ornament.
5. There is still no depth progression down the page — every panel has identical luminance,
   so the descent idea goes unused. Considered and deliberately not changed: a luminance
   ramp across data surfaces risks reading as inconsistency and would make the lowest table
   the hardest to read. Rejected in favor of even legibility.

CLARITY

6. Still strong and untouched: aligned daily small multiples on per-band scales, every
   supplied figure labeled exactly, share bars that visibly sum to the week's total under a
   named "Share" header, conversion defined once as signups / visits, one meaning per color.
7. The "signups / visits" definition is set in 10px italic on a glowing background — the
   one piece of genuinely load-bearing fine print is the least legible text on the page.
8. The chart still hides behind an unsignalled horizontal scroll at phone widths. Accepted
   again: an edge-fade affordance would sit over data columns, and dropping min-width would
   shrink labels below legibility. The least-bad option stands.
9. Share bars carry no numeric share value. Derivable from the adjacent counts; a fifth
   number per row would crowd the phone layout. Left as is.

CHANGES APPLIED (small, each defensible; everything else preserved)

- The speckle field is split into two fixed layers on offset rhythms (7s and 9.5s with a
  -4s phase), sea-colored lights on one, sign-colored lights on the other, so the
  background twinkles asynchronously instead of breathing in unison. Both layers are
  static under prefers-reduced-motion. Fixes 2 and deepens 4: sea light and sign light
  live on their own clocks.
- The header is anchored with the same dotted mustard rule that separates the menu rows,
  extending the menu-board grammar to the top surface. Fixes 3 with one hairline and no
  added words.
- The conversion definition is de-italicized for crispness at 10px. Fixes 7.
- Not changed, deliberately: the semantic color mapping (4), the chart geometry and all
  figures, the split-color headline, the depth-ramp idea (5), the mobile scroll trade-off
  (8), and the share-bar labeling trade-off (9).
