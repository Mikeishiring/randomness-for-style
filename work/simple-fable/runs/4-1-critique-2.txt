Critique of runs/4-1-2.html

ORIGINALITY

1. The identity still lives at the page's edges. The daily display is two
   generic bar charts; the one fused gesture inside the data — the finish-line
   cap — is a six-pixel garnish. Nothing about how the data itself is drawn
   says speed or light.
2. The chequered mirror ball is a real fusion, but it is a passive masthead
   badge, and the gradient-sheen title remains the stock dark-dashboard move
   it was in round one.
3. The checker strips top and bottom are page trim any template could wear.
4. The KPI row is four identical rounded cards with a gradient hairline —
   dark-theme component-library defaults, with no compositional idea.
5. The week's own story — traffic building all week to a weekend peak — is a
   disco arc the charts show but the design never claims.

CLARITY

6. Day labels sit at the very bottom of a ~320px stack. Matching a visits bar
   to its day means scanning down across the entire signups chart; the shared
   x-axis is farthest from the chart that needs it most.
7. Color coding leaks: the sources track fill ends in gold, but gold
   everywhere else means signups, and the track encodes visits only. The
   strongest color on each source row points at the wrong metric.
8. That track also spans under the signups and conversion columns, inviting a
   read of "overall row performance" when it is a visits bar.
9. Gold is additionally spent on the h2 flourish, so accent gold and
   data-coding gold compete on the same screen.
10. "Conv." is the table's only naming of conversion and leans on the KPI tile
    to be understood. Tolerable — the tile establishes the term and the values
    carry their own % sign — but it is a lean.

CHANGES IN 4-1-3

- Mirrored the day-by-day chart around a shared day axis: visits rise above
  it, signups hang below it like a reflection. Each day now reads as one
  vertical slice — label, both metrics — with direction, not distance,
  separating the two scales (fixes 6, develops 1). The silhouette is an
  equalizer: rhythm as the disco gesture inside the data at last, and the
  finish-line caps now mark Sunday's tips on both sides of the axis. Metric
  labels sit at the outer edges, Visits top and Signups bottom, symmetric
  with the mirror rather than inconsistently stacked.
- Retuned the sources track fill to the visits coding (pink to orange),
  removing gold from a bar that does not encode signups (fixes 7).
- Quieted the h2 flourish from gold to silver so gold spends only on signups,
  the podium chip, and the synthetic-data badge (fixes 9).

PRESERVED, DELIBERATELY

The KPI cards stay plain (4): the identity now lives inside the charts, and
dressing every tile would compete with the information the brief says must
win. The checker trim (3), the mirror-ball masthead (2), and the restrained
title sheen stay as framing — the margins are the correct place for the
loudest identity. The aligned sources leaderboard, direct value labels,
P1/P2/P3 podium, track width under the full row (8 — acceptable once its
gradient matches visits), and the "Conv." header (10) are kept as working
decisions. Purpose, figures, and data are unchanged.
