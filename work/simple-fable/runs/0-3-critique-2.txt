CRITIQUE OF 0-3-2.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh

1. The fusion now lives in exactly one place. Act I earned its identity (waved waterline,
   conversion at the surface, signups in the deep), but Act II abandons the sea entirely:
   it is a plain horizontal bar list wearing opera typography. The page's two ingredients
   combine in one chart and merely coexist everywhere else.

2. The same ornament divider is stamped twice, identically. A playbill varies its
   ornaments; mechanical repetition reads as a rubber stamp, not engraving.

3. The masthead is still stock playbill: emblem, small caps, italic note. Defensible — it
   is clear and quiet — but nothing in it could not have shipped with v1.

CLARITY — harsh

4. The honest shared scale introduced in v2 made Act II's gold bars vestigial: Social's
   86 signups is an 8px nub sitting alone on its own full-width track row. The encoding is
   truthful and nearly useless — a whole row of vertical space spent on a mark the eye can
   barely find. Honesty won; the presentation lost.

5. Two different kinds of number share one gold voice. Daily conversion (a rate) and
   signup counts are both set in bright gold; only the % sign separates them. Skimmed
   fast, "4.0%" and "57" sit in adjacent bands wearing the same uniform.

6. Act II spends two track rows per source when the relationship between the two series is
   a subset — signups are visits. The structure hides that truth; the reader must infer it
   from the legend and arithmetic.

PRESERVED DELIBERATELY

The waterline chart with daily conversion at the surface, the shared three-item legend,
the overture tiles, both curtains, the emblem, the palette, printed values on every mark,
and the minimal copy all stay. The masthead (3) is left alone: changing typography now
would be churn that risks the page's restraint without a clarity gain, and the available
system serif cannot honestly impersonate condensed poster type.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 0-3-3.html

A. Nested subset bars in Act II (addresses 1, 4, 6): each source is now one track — the
   gold signups bar sits inside the teal visits bar, on the same scale. The subset
   relation is encoded directly, conversion is visible as the filled fraction of each
   bar's mouth, Social's weakness reads at a glance, and the act loses a row of bulk per
   source. Both values are printed, stacked and color-keyed beside each bar, so no detail
   from v2 is lost.

B. Rates set in italic (addresses 5): every conversion value — the legend chip, the
   percentages at the waterline, the per-source percentages — is now italic gold, while
   counts stay roman. One small typographic rule separates rate from count everywhere,
   and italic numerals are native playbill vocabulary.

C. An entr'acte, not a rubber stamp (addresses 2): the divider between the acts becomes a
   waterline wave flanked by the ruled lines, quoting Act I's surface; the first divider
   keeps its tentacle curls. Variation where opera would vary, repetition removed.

D. Nothing else is touched. Act II's palette and structure now carry the sea through the
   subset metaphor itself (the converted fraction at the mouth of each current), which
   develops the ingredient without adding a word of copy or a drop of decoration.
