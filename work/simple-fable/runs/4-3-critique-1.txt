CRITIQUE OF 4-3-1.html

ORIGINALITY
1. The fusion is costume-deep. Remove the two checker ribbons and the roman
   numerals and what remains is a competent but generic dark dashboard: stat
   row, bar chart, ranked table. Velvet gradient + gold + italic Didot is the
   first idea anyone has for "opera"; a checkered strip is the first idea
   anyone has for "motorsport". Both were applied at the page edges, where
   decoration lives, not inside the data displays, where an identity has to
   earn its keep.
2. The two ingredients never meet in a single element. Opera owns the header;
   motorsport owns the ribbons and the condensed numerals. The one genuinely
   fused invention - finishing positions set as opera-act roman numerals - is
   underplayed and left ambiguous (ranked by what?).
3. "Performance" (opera performance / website performance) is the strongest
   card and it is played silently; nothing else sustains the playbill fiction.
4. Motorsport's own data language - finish lines, fastest-lap highlights,
   gaps to the leader - maps directly onto weekly metrics and was ignored in
   favor of a flag ribbon. Racing red appears exactly once, decoratively,
   under the title; a one-use accent is not a system.

CLARITY
5. The week's actual story - conversion climbs steadily from 4.0 on Monday to
   6.0 on Sunday, so Sunday leads on quality as well as volume - is nowhere on
   the page. Visits and signups rise in parallel; without the ratio, the two
   charts just say "up".
6. In Sources, the velvet bar is unlabeled beneath three numeric columns; a
   reader can take it to encode signups or conversion rather than visits. The
   rank behind I/II/III is likewise implied, never stated.
7. Craft bug: the conversion column is a fixed 6.2rem, narrower than its own
   letter-spaced header at phone width; the header can overflow its cell.
8. Nit: the letter-spaced date line is optically off-center by half a
   letter-space (trailing tracking).
9. The 58px signups track compresses 57-vs-74-scale differences, though exact
   values are printed above each bar.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 4-3-2.html
- Red made systemic instead of decorative: it now marks the week's best -
  Sunday's bar caps in both daily charts, Sunday's conversion figure, and
  Direct's 6.0% - a motorsport fastest-lap highlight that the red stripe under
  the title now belongs to.
- Added a third daily row, "Conversion %" (4.0 4.5 5.0 4.5 5.0 5.4 6.0),
  derived only from the supplied figures; it carries the week's story.
- Each source bar now runs toward a checkered finish line at the end of its
  track: the flag moves from pure decoration into the display. Direct touches
  the line; the gap behind it shows each rival's deficit.
- Sources heading now reads "Sources - ranked by visits", resolving both the
  numeral ambiguity and the bar's meaning for the cost of three words.
- Classification columns auto-size (fixes the mobile header overflow); stat
  numerals set italic for the number-board lean while charts stay upright;
  date-line optical centering fixed; signups track raised to 64px.

KEPT
Structure, palette, playbill header, checker ribbons as page frame, the
two-scale daily layout with printed values, roman-numeral finishing order,
and every supplied figure unchanged.
