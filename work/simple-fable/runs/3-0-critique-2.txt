CRITIQUE OF 3-0-2.html

ORIGINALITY
- The first revision bolted tech on instead of fusing it. A barcode that is
  aria-hidden and encodes nothing, an "EOF" tag, a second monospace face --
  these are badges pinned to an IRS costume, not a synthesis. Remove them
  and the page loses nothing, which is the definition of a prop.
- The typewriter-vs-terminal type duality (Courier vs Consolas) is so close
  that no reader perceives it. A private joke is not an identity.
- The segmented dot-matrix bars were the one genuine fusion move on the
  page. Everything else tech-flavored is sticker-deep.
- The background grid remains subliminal: too faint to signify, still
  rendering on every paint.

CLARITY
- The legend says "week high / week low", but in Part II the same markers
  flag the best and worst SOURCE. One legend, and it misdescribes half of
  its uses.
- Three textures stack on every ledger row: dotted leaders, bar tracks,
  segment stripes. The leaders run underneath the bars and collide with
  them; row tracking is doing its job twice, badly.
- The barcode caption repeats 12,480 and 624 -- the third appearance of
  both figures above the fold -- while crowding the mobile masthead into a
  title + barcode + stamp pile.
- The peak-row tint (#eef1e4) is a near-invisible green on cream, and the
  bullet marker is CSS-generated content that assistive tech never hears.
- Part II at 375px sits within a few pixels of horizontal overflow; wide
  header letter-spacing is spent on decoration at the exact breakpoint
  where it risks breaking the no-scroll rule.

KEPT (strong decisions)
- Form frame and masthead, numbered summary boxes, black part bands,
  merged labeled bar columns with printed values, segmented bar fills,
  glyph markers so color is not the only signal, the synthetic-data stamp,
  totals alignment, scope attributes, all derived figures.

CHANGES APPLIED
1. The sheet itself now fuses the two ingredients: tractor-feed sprocket
   strips down both edges and green-bar row striping make the page
   continuous-form computer paper -- the native habitat of a 1980s
   administrative ledger. Tech becomes the medium instead of a badge.
2. Green-bar stripes replace the dotted leaders: one row-tracking device
   instead of two competing textures, and the bars sit on clean rows.
3. The decorative barcode is removed with its duplicate caption; the
   masthead decongests, especially on mobile. The faint body grid is
   dropped for a flat desk tone that lets the sheet read as a sheet.
4. Legend corrected to "highest conv. / lowest conv.", true in both
   tables; peak day keeps its own entry.
5. Peak row re-tinted blue -- distinct from the green bars, visibly
   highlighted, consistent with its marker -- and the row label now
   carries a visually-hidden "(peak)" for assistive tech.
6. Mobile: narrower sprocket strips, tighter header letter-spacing and
   smaller bar minimums remove the overflow risk. Revision counter -> 3.
Purpose, figures, tables, derived values and structure unchanged.
