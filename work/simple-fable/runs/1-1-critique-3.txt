CRITIQUE OF 1-1-3.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh
- The identity is top-heavy. Three rounds in, everything below the "Sources"
  heading is an untouched admin table: the colonnade stops at the section
  break, and neither ingredient ever reaches the bottom third of the page.
- The KPI row is four generic rounded rectangles that any dashboard ships
  with. The page's first data moment has nothing Greek or disco about it.
- Same metric, two costumes: conversion is a glowing spot of light in the
  daily chart but a flat length bar in the table. The page invented one
  genuinely original device in round 2 and then didn't trust it enough to make
  it the system. That is the mark of decoration, not identity.
- The surviving ornaments (rays, sparkles, meander strip and colophon) are now
  doing the right amount of work; no further development is justified there.

CLARITY — harsh
- Encoding the same quantity two different ways forces the reader to relearn
  it mid-page. Worse, the asymmetry is informative in the wrong direction: the
  table's conversion bars carry a site-average tick, the daily dots do not, so
  "above or below average?" is answerable for sources but not for days — and
  the daily trend is where that question first arises.
- The tick's caption sits under the table, far from the chart where a reader
  first wants it; shared method should be stated once, at first use.
- The chart containers rely on aria-label on bare divs, which is not reliably
  exposed without a role.
- The dot track at 88% column width gives away precision room on phones,
  where the seven dots are already only a few pixels apart.
- The stylobate line still runs under the label column, faintly implying an
  eighth data column (carried from v2; tolerable, unchanged).

KEPT — strong decisions preserved
- Every figure printed; stated ranges on every track; the colonnade bars,
  laurel, stage-light pool, mirror-ball mark, meander strip and colophon; the
  real table with Share kept; the synthetic-data badge; "dip Thu"; no filler
  copy; no motion. The visits bars in the table stay length bars — length is
  the right encoding for a count, and restraint beats a matching gimmick.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 1-1-4.html
1. One device for conversion everywhere: the table's conversion bars are
   replaced by the same glowing dot-on-track (0–6%) used for days, and the
   site-average tick (5.0%) now sits on every conversion track on the page,
   daily chart included — same mapping, same scale, same light.
2. The single caption explaining the tick and the 0–6% span moves to first
   use, directly under the daily chart; the table's separate note is removed
   (one stated method, two uses).
3. The KPI cards become plinths: a solid gold base edge under each, hairline
   elsewhere, so the week's totals stand on pedestals — Greek weight on a
   previously generic zone, with zero text or layout change.
4. role="group" added to the labelled chart container; dot tracks widened to
   94% on phones for precision.

Purpose, figures, layout order and all data values are unchanged.
