Critique of 2-4-1.html (originality, then clarity), followed by the changes applied in 2-4-2.html.

ORIGINALITY — harsh

1. The sky-as-conversion encoding is the single genuine invention, and everything
   else coasts on it. Remove the glyphs and what remains is template luxury:
   ivory ground, letterspaced capitals, a double hairline frame. Nothing is
   specifically *hotel* — no tariff-board structure, no concierge-card
   conventions. The crest is an anonymous ring any spa brochure could wear.
2. The cloud is the stock Feather icon path, so the most original element is
   drawn with the least original marks. The glyphs read as flat UI icons, not
   printed engravings: pure outline, no ink, no wash.
3. The weekend lift — the week's actual shape, and the defining rhythm of a
   hotel — is left entirely to inference from day labels. The chart knows it
   and says nothing.

CLARITY

4. In Sources the column is headed "Sky" but the load-bearing value is the
   unlabeled percentage beside the glyph; the reader must carry the legend's
   definition over from the previous card to know what 3.1% is.
5. The Visits and Signups bar rows sit on separate scales and nothing says so
   explicitly. Mitigated in practice: each row's tallest bar touches its own
   printed maximum and every bar carries its value, so magnitudes cannot be
   misread — but it is an implicit convention, not a stated one.
6. Glyphs carrying aria-labels lacked role="img"; some screen readers will
   skip them.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 2-4-2.html (and only these)

- Sources header "Sky" renamed "Conversion". The glyph remains the sky mark;
  the number is now named where it appears. (Fixes 4.)
- A faint gold wash behind the Sat–Sun columns of the week board makes the
  weekend pattern a stated fact of the chart rather than an inference, with
  no words added. The signups row was rebuilt with padding instead of margin
  so the band runs unbroken. (Addresses 3.)
- Engraving pass on the glyphs: translucent gold wash inside the sun, warm
  ink-wash fill on the clouds. The marks now read as printed, not icon-font;
  the cloud geometry is kept because it is legible at 20px and redrawing it
  risks clarity for novelty's sake. (Addresses 2, partially concedes it.)
- The stat strip is framed above and below by hairlines, a tariff board — the
  hotel ingredient doing structural work instead of ambient styling.
  (Addresses 1 within the no-added-copy constraint.)
- role="img" added to labeled glyphs. (Fixes 6.)

DECLINED, DELIBERATELY

- No High/Low line and no forecast prose: both would duplicate values already
  printed on the bars. No caption renames — "Outlook" would misdescribe a
  retrospective report.
- No explicit separate-scale annotation (5): every bar is value-labeled and
  each row self-labels its maximum, so an axis note would restate numbers
  already on the page.
- Purpose, data, figures, layout and section order are unchanged.
