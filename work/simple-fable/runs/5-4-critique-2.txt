Critique of runs/5-4-2.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

1. The one original idea on the page — light means conversion — is left half built.
   Rates glow in the KPI row and in the sources table, but the chart, the largest
   surface, contains no rate at all. The signups band shows raw counts whose rise
   mostly restates the visits rise; the actual story of the week (conversion improves
   toward the weekend, 4.0% Monday to 6.0% Sunday) is nowhere on the page. The owner
   must do mental division to find the most decision-relevant fact the data holds.
2. The header ornament is still a stock diamond between rules — the generic luxury
   glyph every template reaches for, dimmed in the last pass but not rethought. The
   two ingredients never actually touch anywhere on the page: gold frames things,
   cyan fills things, and no element fuses them.
3. Structure remains the dashboard triptych. Defensible (it answers the owner's
   question directly), but it means identity still lives entirely in surface
   treatment. The only cure that does not tax clarity is deepening the one motif the
   page owns — see 1.

CLARITY — harsh read

4. Mobile defect: the sources header keeps its empty .c-bar span, whose grid-area
   "bar" does not exist in the narrow grid template. The span lands in an implicit
   extra column, stealing a gap's width from the name column, so the right-aligned
   Visits / Signups / Rate headers sit about 10px left of the numbers they label.
   Header-to-column misalignment is a direct clarity failure.
5. Sources bars are scaled to the largest source (Direct = full width), but nothing
   says so; a reader could take them for share of total. The adjacent exact numbers
   keep this from misleading, and an axis or scale note would cost more than it buys
   on a three-row list — flagged, accepted.
6. The chart's aria-label describes visits and signups but, once a rate row exists,
   must carry the rate story too, or screen-reader users get less than sighted ones.

STRONG DECISIONS — preserved

- Direct band captions in the chart (VISITS / SIGNUPS) instead of a detached legend.
- One page-wide color rule: visits pale cyan, signups gold, derived rates luminous.
- Exact value labels on every bar; separated bands rather than a dual-scale overlay.
- Minimal copy, visible synthetic-data caveat, double-hairline luxury panels, abyss
  depth gradient.

CHANGES APPLIED (one pass, data and purpose unchanged)

a. Daily signup-rate row added beneath the signups band, direct-labeled like the
   other bands and set in the luminous rate treatment: 4.0 / 4.5 / 5.0 / 4.5 / 5.0 /
   5.4 / 6.0 percent. The light-means-conversion motif now runs through every
   section, and the week's real finding is on the page instead of left as mental
   arithmetic. All values derived from supplied figures only. Fixes 1.
b. Ornament rebuilt as a fused identity mark: the gold hairlines now meet a small
   glowing cyan point — luxury rule and bioluminescent light in one element,
   replacing the stock diamond. Fixes 2.
c. Sources header's placeholder bar cell hidden in the narrow layout, so header
   labels align with their columns again. Fixes 4.
d. Chart aria-label extended with the rate trend. Fixes 6.

Not changed: the triptych structure (see 3), the relative-to-max source bars (see
5), and the div-based sources rows noted in the previous critique — same reasoning
holds.
