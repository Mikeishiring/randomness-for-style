Critique of 4-5-3.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

1. The identity has stopped developing and started settling. Round two
   added the board and the beetles; round three added nothing to the
   vocabulary, only bookkeeping. That was the right call then, but the
   honest reading now is that the two ingredients are developed exactly as
   far as they will go without hurting the report: the remaining
   conventional surfaces (bar chart, table) are conventional because
   convention is what keeps them instantly readable. Harsh version: this
   design's ceiling is cohesion, not invention, and v3 has not fully
   cashed in the cohesion.
2. Concrete cohesion failure: the page numbers three plates but frames only
   two. Plates I and II sit in bordered specimen mounts; "Plate III" is a
   bare table floating on the case bottom. The system the design invented,
   it applies inconsistently.

CLARITY — harsh read

3. Share of visits is the one figure a reader must still estimate. The 10%
   ticks were a half-measure: a ruler where a number belongs. 42.0 / 35.9 /
   22.1 are knowable to one decimal; making the reader interpolate ticks is
   false economy, especially when every other figure on the page is printed.
4. Pixel-level sloppiness: in both plates the checkered best-day marker is
   placed at x=330 while the 12px "Sun" label centred at x=320 extends to
   roughly x=330.5 — the chip can clip the final glyph. Small, real, and
   exactly the kind of finishing error a specimen label would never carry.
5. The totals row's cream background will lose contrast once the table
   gains the plate's tinted backing; it needs a visibly distinct tone or
   the reconciliation row stops reading as a rule-off.

CHANGES MADE IN 4-5-4.html

A. Sources section now sits in the same plate frame as Plates I and II —
   heading, plate number and table inside one bordered mount. The plate
   system is applied consistently (addresses 2).
B. Each share track is direct-labelled with its percentage: 42.0%, 35.9%,
   22.1%, and 100% on the totals row. Ticks stay as the ruler; the number
   does the precise work (addresses 3). Table minimum width raised to
   fit the labels without squeezing.
C. Best-day chips in both plates nudged from x=330 to x=334, clearing the
   "Sun" label (addresses 4).
D. Totals row backing set to the darker track tone so it still reads as a
   rule-off inside the tinted plate (addresses 5).

CONSIDERED AND REJECTED

- Engraved hatch fills on the visits bars (Victorian woodcut texture):
  texture noise on the page's simplest, most legible element; solid ink
  wins.
- Rebuilding the pit board as a semantic table or definition list: the
  current markup already linearises correctly for a screen reader
  ("Conversion by day, Mon 4.0%, ..."); churn without reader benefit.
- Any further copy, captions or trend annotations: everything sayable is
  already shown.

Purpose, data, every supplied and derived figure, plates, beetles, board,
plaques, header, palette — unchanged.
