Critique of 4-0-2.html (originality, then clarity), followed by the changes applied in 4-0-3.html.

ORIGINALITY — harsh read

1. The color system is half-built, which exposes the identity as surface
   treatment. v2 established red = visits, teal = signups, purple = session
   best — then broke its own rules everywhere channels mix: KPI deltas are
   teal even on the visits tile, the peak-day tile renders a visits figure
   and a signups figure in the same teal, and the sources table colors the
   SIGNUPS column but leaves VISITS white. A telemetry system that
   channel-codes inconsistently is a theme, not a system.
2. Conversion numbers in the sources table are red-tinted for no semantic
   reason. On this page red is the visits channel; borrowing it as a generic
   accent is exactly the coat-of-paint habit v1 was criticized for.
3. The motorsport flag vocabulary was adopted halfway. Purple-best is in, but
   motorsport pairs it with yellow for caution, and v2's emphasis system can
   only celebrate. The one actionable finding in the data — Social converts
   at half Direct's rate (3.1% vs 6.0%) — receives no emphasis at all.
4. The checkered strip remains ornament. It is the page's most generic
   signifier and still does no informational work.
5. The tech ingredient still rests mostly on the mono font and grid; it is
   carried by the telemetry structure rather than developed further.

CLARITY — harsh read

6. Both lanes label 1.0k/2.0k and 50/100 gridlines but not the zero
   baseline, leaving the zero-based scale implicit.
7. Emphasis that only marks winners misleads by omission: a reader scanning
   for problems finds nothing marked, yet the 2x source conversion gap is
   the week's main story after the upward trend.
8. In the KPI row, teal "+48%" under VISITS invites a false association with
   the signups channel defined one card below.

WORTH KEEPING (strong decisions preserved)

- Lane-per-channel traces, every value labelled, zero-based fills, in-lane
  channel labels, the CONV % sector row, the purple peak marking.
- The checkered strip stays despite (4): it is the single compact at-a-glance
  identity marker, and replacing it with a subtler device would trade
  recognition for taste with no clarity gain.
- Layout order, copy, KPIs, plate, notched livery corners, mobile-safe
  sources grid.

CHANGES APPLIED IN 4-0-3.html

a. Channel coding completed wherever channels mix: sources VISITS column and
   header in channel red, SIGNUPS header in channel teal; KPI deltas take
   their tile's channel color; the peak-day tile colors 2,100 red and 127
   teal (1, 8). Within a single-channel lane, value labels stay white — the
   trace identifies the channel and white maximizes contrast.
b. Unmotivated red removed from conversion figures; conversion is derived,
   so it reads neutral white (2).
c. The flag palette is completed: amber caution marks Social's 3.1%
   conversion, opposite purple best on Direct's 6.0% — emphasis now points
   at the finding as well as the win (3, 7).
d. Zero labels added to both lane baselines (6).
e. No further tech-ingredient additions (5): every candidate considered
   (HUD brackets, status readout line, scanlines) was decoration that would
   add noise without information, and the completed channel semantics are
   themselves the instrumentation idea carried further.
f. Data, purpose, structure and all other styling unchanged.
