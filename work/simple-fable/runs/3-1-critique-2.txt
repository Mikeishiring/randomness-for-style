CRITIQUE OF 3-1-2.html

ORIGINALITY — harsh

1. The fusion is real now but thin in places. The chrome-horizon lettering is
   a one-off: it appears on the title and nowhere else, so the strongest
   disco signature on the page reads as a logo treatment rather than a
   system. (Extending chrome fills to the §1 figures was considered and
   rejected: 36px chrome digits trade legibility for flourish, and the
   entry rule under each figure already carries the gradient. The device
   stays singular by choice, not oversight — noted so the next pass does not
   "fix" it.)
2. The mirror ball is still pure garnish. It earns its place as an instant
   genre cue, but nothing on the page would miss it.
3. The section strips are bare title bars. Real forms annotate their boxes —
   marginal notes, definitions, "see box 2c" — and the page uses none of
   that vocabulary even where a definition is actually needed (see 5).

CLARITY — harsh

4. Moving to one shared day axis fixed the duplicate row but created a new
   fault: the signup-rate row now sits BELOW the signups chart, roughly
   120px and two captions away from the Mon–Sun labels it depends on. The
   reader maps 5.4% to Sat by counting columns across an intervening chart.
   Worse than v1 on this one point.
5. The rate row's meaning rests entirely on its caption; the % values are
   visually identical in weight to the data labels above them.
6. The lone pink 6.0% is unexplained emphasis. Nothing on the page says why
   one cell is pink; the reader must connect it to the §1 peak-day entry on
   their own.
7. §3 is half-encoded: visits get a comparison bar, signups are bare
   numbers. The week's main story — Social converts at half the rate of
   Direct — exists only as digits in the last column. The panel spends its
   ink on the dimension with the least spread.
8. With v2's removal of legend swatches (correct for §2's single-series
   charts), §3 has no mechanism left to identify a second series if one is
   added — and point 7 requires adding one.

REVISION APPLIED

- Daily signup rates moved INTO the day-axis band: each cell now reads
  "Mon / 4.0%", with the rate in pink mono directly between the visits bar
  above and the signups bar below — physically seated between the two
  figures it is computed from. The separate rate block, its caption and its
  extra rule are deleted; §2 loses a full detached row and gains locality.
- The §2 section strip now carries a form-style marginal definition on its
  right end — "% = signups ÷ visits" — so the pink figures are defined at
  the moment they are first seen, in bureaucratic register rather than a
  caption paragraph.
- The lone Sunday highlight is retired: all rates share the pink ink, the
  maximum is self-evident in a seven-cell row, and §1 already names the
  peak day. Unexplained one-cell emphasis was noise, not information.
- §3 now encodes both series: each source row carries a violet-pink visits
  bar (scaled to max visits) over a thin gold signups bar (scaled to max
  signups). Social's gold bar collapsing to a quarter while its visits bar
  holds half makes the conversion story visible before the 3.1% is read.
- Small functional swatches added to the §3 VISITS and SIGNUPS column
  headers to bind numbers to bars — legitimate here (two series, one panel),
  unlike the single-series swatches removed in v2.

PRESERVED

Chrome-horizon title, dance-floor divider, faceted mirror-tile bars, filled
entry rules in §1, the diverging shared-axis chart, stamp, punched holes,
margin rule, mirror ball, § numbering, every supplied figure and every
derived figure (totals, per-day rates, per-source rates), printed values on
all bars. Purpose and data unchanged; page remains fully self-contained.
