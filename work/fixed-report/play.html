About the experiment
What we call AI slop is often recognition of familiar design patterns. This experiment explores how randomness can help break them: the same brief and data, two unexpected ingredients, then three refinements with the same prompt.
These are saved examples, not live generations. The stronger sequence restarts from the same one-shot in a fresh context. More refinement does not guarantee a better result.
