Originality: The chart now has a recognizable instrument character, but a dark background and dotted grid are not a breakthrough. It is still a conventional dashboard wearing weather-station styling. That is acceptable where the convention helps comparison; adding radar circles, animated clouds, or forecast language would make it less honest and less legible. Preserve the identity in the weather mark and observation chart instead of escalating the theme.

Clarity: The revised headline is useful, but “A strong finish” repeats it and “Sunday led visits and signups” repeats the same finding yet again. The mobile chart shrinks seven point labels, axis labels, and day labels into a narrow viewport; keeping every annotation visible is false completeness when it becomes too small to read. Tiny footer and table-header type also undermines the promise of simplicity. The previous refinement promoted the absence of prior-week data into a whole extra explanatory paragraph, which is disproportionate to this short report. “Weekly reading” overstates a panel that only discusses sources.

One refinement: Keep the existing layout, zero-based chart, totals, complete daily table, source bars, and restrained weather-station palette. Replace the repeated status with the exact Sunday peak values. On narrow screens, enlarge day and endpoint annotations and omit intermediate point annotations; the complete daily values remain directly below in the table. Give endpoint labels inward alignment to avoid clipping. Increase table-header, source-detail, and footer legibility. Shorten the chart's secondary label and name the source commentary accurately. Move the concise prior-week limitation to the readable consolidated footer. These are bounded clarity improvements rather than a cosmetic redesign.
