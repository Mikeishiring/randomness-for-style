The design is readable but its identity stops at the palette. Dark teal, a glowing dot and a serif wordmark could belong to almost any premium analytics template. The luxury-hotel ingredient is faint; the deep-sea ingredient is essentially one box shadow. “Your week, in view” also wastes the most prominent text on a phrase that does not identify the report. The invented property name gets greater contextual prominence than website performance.

The daily table is the strongest decision: all original figures remain visible, conversion is comparable and the quiet visit bars help scanning. The weekly metrics and two short findings deserve to stay. There is no justification for replacing these with an immersive ocean scene or interactive chart.

Refinement: replace the generic title with Website performance, retain the restrained property-like masthead, and turn its dot into a small original luminous medallion with concentric contours. Add a restrained contour ornament in the header margin, connecting bathymetric rings with the engraved detailing of hotel stationery. A warm fine rule gives the masthead a more deliberate hospitality character. Keep these ornaments away from the data. Explicitly identify the source bars as visits, since their widths currently rely on inference. Preserve every figure, rate, finding and responsive layout.
