The last revision made the page more identifiable, but the ornament now does more identity work than the information layout. Adding more rings or more glow would make that weakness worse. The restrained medallion, engraving and warm rule are sufficient; they should stay subordinate.

Clarity still has avoidable friction. “Daily rhythm” is an atmospheric heading where “Daily performance” would be more informative. The separate visits/signups legend repeats the table headers, takes scarce width and can imply that two series are plotted even though only visits have bars. “Conv.” saves a few characters at the expense of a less familiar reader. The day column consumes 43% of the table, forcing three numerical columns into the remaining space on phones, with no explicit cell separation.

Refine the data presentation as a quiet hotel ledger: clear column labels, generous numerical alignment, and a subtle warm baseline after the header. Put the existing semantic colors on the relevant column labels and remove the redundant legend. Give narrow screens a real numerical column layout, reduce the tiny bars before sacrificing any numbers, and keep conversion fully spelled out. Retain the luminous bars, engraved masthead, all source data, summary metrics, findings and methodology. No additional decorative scene or explanatory copy is justified.
