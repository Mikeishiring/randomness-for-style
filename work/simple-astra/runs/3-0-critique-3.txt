The design is still conservative. Ruled sections, monospace numbers and green ink are familiar visual language, and the page is not an unprecedented interpretation of Bureaucracy and Tech. The small geometric mark contributes little; the strongest identity comes from the form fields A, B and B/A, the numbered record sections and the precise, switchable chart. Those elements earn their place through structure and calculation rather than costume.

Clarity now has a coherent hierarchy: totals, a short evidence-based finding, daily volume, then source and daily detail. The chart has exact labels and explicit zero-based scales. The source comparison marks sit under the signup counts they encode and share a fixed track width. The complete tables retain every supplied observation and the calculated signup rates. The controls wrap and have usable targets; row headers clarify table navigation. The recurring green refers consistently to activity and emphasis, rather than pretending to represent different source categories.

There are possible stylistic alternatives, but no further change is justified by this brief. More bureaucratic ornament would consume attention without improving comprehension. More dashboard controls would imply data or capabilities the supplied record does not contain. Removing either table would discard useful detail. Replacing the modest identity with a theatrical one would sacrifice the original instruction to keep the report simple. The correct refinement at this point is restraint: preserve the current result unchanged. This is a judgment about the saved HTML, not a claim of browser-tested perfection.
