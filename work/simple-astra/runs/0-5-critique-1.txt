The first design is clear, numerically sound and unusually restrained. Its paper, dark green ink, serif typography and pinned winged tentacle specimen are strong decisions worth keeping. The complete daily and source tables, zero-based chart, explicit synthetic label, weighted weekly rate and rounding note all serve the owner.

Originality is weaker than the brief deserves. “The Observatory” and “weekly field record” could introduce almost any antique-themed dashboard. The two requested ingredients live almost entirely in a small decorative insect. The refinement develops that same specimen into a framed, cross-marked cabinet engraving, with finer wing hatching and a fictional taxonomic label, and names the collection “The Abyssal Cabinet.” The plain “Website performance” label keeps the purpose immediately legible. The identity remains confined to the header and restrained material treatment.

Clarity suffers from repetition: “Daily visits” immediately repeats as “Visits, by day”; Sunday’s success is narrated twice; source bars beneath text have no declared scale and are less informative than the adjacent exact counts. Remove those redundancies. Bring Sunday’s highest signup rate into the opening finding, retain the meaningful source-rate comparison, and highlight Direct in the source table using the existing Sunday treatment. All supplied daily and source figures, rates and total rows remain.

The original header keeps three blocks on one line even on narrow screens. The refinement gives mobile layouts a two-column header with the period below the title, preserving the specimen without squeezing the title or pushing content sideways. Numerical typography and table structure remain intact. No external assets, invented performance data, extra controls or unsupported comparisons are added.
