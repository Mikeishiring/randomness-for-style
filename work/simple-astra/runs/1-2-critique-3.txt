Originality: This is still recognizably a conventional performance report, not a radically new visual language. The serif headings and boxed metrics are familiar, and a Greek meander alone could belong to any Greek restaurant. The identity succeeds because the Olympus name and thunderbolt piercing a burger make the two ingredients specific; the drive-in sign and meander reinforce that emblem. They do not need to colonize the charts. More gods, menu terminology, temple framing, or food-shaped marks would be decorative escalation rather than an improvement to this report.

Clarity: The current version has direct section names, prominent totals, exact chart labels, an explicit conversion formula, complete daily and source figures, semantic row headings, and a coherent Sunday highlight. It identifies the dataset as synthetic and keeps the rounding note unobtrusive. The observations are supported by the supplied values and do not invent prior-week comparisons or causal explanations. The daily chart duplicates visits in the detail table, but that repetition serves two distinct needs: seeing the weekly pattern and checking exact daily performance across all three measures. Removing either would discard useful detail or slow interpretation.

Decision: No additional design change is justified in this pass. The remaining conventional qualities support the stated purpose; replacing them solely to appear more original would make the report harder to scan. The strongest identity decisions already occupy the branding rather than the data encoding. Preserve the current balance and all useful detail. The revised HTML is therefore an exact copy of the preceding result, not a cosmetic variation presented as progress.
