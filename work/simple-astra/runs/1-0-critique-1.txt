The original is legible, accurate, and usefully restrained. Its identity is less original than it claims: ORACLE plus a generic temple establishes ancient Greece, but the tech half is largely a monospace font. Clarity has three smaller weaknesses. The repeated “Across all sources” lines add no information. Traffic-source bar meaning is explained far from the bars, in the footer. Daily gridlines have no ticks or scale labels, and Sunday alone changes color without a legend or a necessary distinction. Exact labels make the daily chart readable, but these details add ambiguity rather than insight.
