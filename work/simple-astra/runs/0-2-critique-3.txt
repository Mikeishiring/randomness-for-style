The current design is not radically original: beneath the branding it is a conventional totals–chart–sources–table dashboard. That conventional structure is also what makes the report immediately useful. Trying to make the information layout itself resemble a tentacled creature or a fast-food menu would sacrifice the stronger part of the design for novelty.

The combination is now specific enough to stand on its own. A winged, tentacled burger mascot establishes the Cthulhu/fast-food collision; the deep-green storefront fascia, mustard accent and perforated receipt carry that identity through the page. Its weakness is that the horror ingredient remains playful rather than ominous. Making it darker, more ornate or more invasive would be a tonal preference, not a demonstrated improvement to this weekly performance report.

Clarity is stronger than novelty, appropriately. The headline identifies the subject, totals lead, daily figures remain readable without relying on an axis, the toggle explicitly names its metrics, source rows preserve both volume and signup efficiency, and the table preserves every supplied daily figure. Sunday and Direct are highlighted for defensible reasons. The synthetic scope and rate calculation remain stated. No previous-period trend is invented.

I find no further justified change in this pass. Additional brand treatment would repeat an established idea; cutting the table or source rates would remove useful detail; adding more findings would restate existing figures. The earlier fixes already addressed the concrete chart-height and narrow-table issues. The saved HTML is therefore an exact copy of the previous result, with no modification. This is a deliberate stopping point rather than a cosmetic revision to manufacture progress.
