The ruled record is now coherent, but coherence is not originality by itself. Its strongest idea is the form-field relationship A, B and B/A; the section rules and digital numbers support it without requiring more decoration. Adding seals, terminal ornaments or faux approval stamps would make the report worse. Preserve the structure, restrained palette, full tables, exact daily values and honest axes.

The source section has become visually inert: three rows of numbers ask the reader to perform every comparison. The earlier unlabeled source bars deserved removal, but removing visual comparison altogether was an overcorrection. Restore a small, accurately scaled signup bar directly beneath each signup count, inside the explicitly labelled Signups column. This makes Direct’s contribution immediately visible without a new legend or extra prose. Use a common fixed track width, with lengths proportional to 314, 224 and 86, so a width cap cannot distort the ratios.

Two remaining layout details weaken clarity: the chart heading retains a bottom margin inside a flex row, and the small-screen rule forces the heading and buttons onto one line even when there is insufficient room. Reset the margin, allow the row to wrap, and give the metric buttons a 44px target. Mark table row labels as row headers while preserving their typography. These are targeted improvements; no data, findings, calculations or chart scales change.
