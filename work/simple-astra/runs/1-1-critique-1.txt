The first result is clear and numerically sound, but its originality is shallow. A temple, a laurel wreath and a disco ball illustrate “ancient Greece plus disco”; none makes the Greek-myth ingredient specific. The visuals sit beside an otherwise conventional dashboard. “Your week in the spotlight” and “The daily rhythm” spend the strongest headings on atmosphere instead of orientation, while “End of the set” contributes nothing. The Sunday highlight changes the visit color without explaining that exception. On narrow screens, shrinking seven five-digit labels into very narrow bars sacrifices legibility.

The refinement keeps the restrained plum-and-gold palette, serif typography, accurate zero-based visit chart, weekly totals, daily signups, source table and useful observations. It develops Apollo’s lyre into a disco-ball frame, connecting a specific Greek mythological figure with music in one compact emblem. The identity stays in the masthead; headings name the information directly. The main heading becomes “Website performance,” the period includes the synthetic-data disclosure, and the footer retains only the rounding note. All visit bars use the same color. A horizontally scrollable daily chart preserves readable labels on small screens, with a visible scrollbar and keyboard focus. These are targeted changes rather than a redesign: no figures, reporting scope or useful detail change.
