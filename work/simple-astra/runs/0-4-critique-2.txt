The refined seal is now doing real design work: its hotel arch, wings and tentacles combine the ingredients in one recognizable object. The restrained green-and-brass ledger is still appropriate. The remaining weakness is editorial overstatement, not a shortage of decoration. “Hotel & abyss” is a cute explanation of the concept that makes the property feel less convincing. The header repeats the reporting period, and the footer repeats it again. Neither repetition helps the owner understand performance.

The clearest structural flaw is that Sunday's daily finding lives under Traffic sources. Its placement breaks the relationship between the evidence and its interpretation. The fixed title row and large total also have avoidable narrow-screen pressure. These are more valuable fixes than adding further occult ornament.

Retain the arched Cthulhu seal, typography, colors, all source and daily observations, calculated rates, source bars, zero-based daily bars and plain reporting headings. Remove the explanatory brand tagline and redundant header metadata; keep Week 01 beside the page title and consolidate synthetic attribution in the footer. Place the Sunday finding immediately after the daily table. Align the hotel masthead with the report at wide widths. Allow the title row to wrap and scale totals modestly on narrow screens. The source finding keeps its useful whole-week comparison. No new data, interactions or ornamental sections are warranted; the two ingredients are stronger when the seal and hotel name carry them without commentary.
