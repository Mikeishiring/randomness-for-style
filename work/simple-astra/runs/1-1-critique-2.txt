The revised page is much easier to identify, and Apollo’s lyre gives the theme a real point of view. It should not acquire more themed headings or decorative sections. Two weaknesses remain. The source bars live beside source names, disconnected from the numeric column they encode; the reader must infer that they represent visits rather than signups or conversion. The disco ball is still mostly a gridded gold sphere: its uniform fill reads as an ornament more than a mirrored surface. There is also an accessibility gap in daily visits: the value comes before its day in the reading order, without an explicit association, unlike the signup row.

Move each source bar into its Visits cell, immediately below its count, and retain that compact comparison on mobile. Add a few clipped, contrasting mirror tiles to the existing ball, using the existing gold, paper and plum palette; preserve the lyre silhouette and chart restraint. Give each daily visit group an explicit day-and-count accessible label. Preserve all figures, totals, calculations, observations and the readable scrollable daily chart. No new metrics, thematic prose or interactions are needed. These limited refinements address actual ambiguity and make the existing identity more specific without turning the report into a poster.
