The report is now clear, and another dramatic redesign would mostly spend clarity to advertise the theme. The remaining originality problem is specific: the mounted beetle is tilted like a scrapbook sticker, whereas the rest of the page suggests orderly cataloguing. The chart trays are a useful expression of a collecting cabinet and should stay. More insect illustrations, stamps, or invented catalogue vocabulary would compete with the actual report.

Two clarity defects justify a focused revision. The source bars sit beneath source names even though their lengths encode visits; a reader must infer the measure. Put those bars directly beneath their visit counts. The daily rate strip uses different horizontal padding and gaps from its corresponding day labels, weakening the intended column alignment. Give it precisely the same seven-column geometry. The day labels should also sit immediately beside the rate row; move the rate heading above the day labels instead of between days and values.

Refine the existing identity by straightening the specimen mount and giving each chart a restrained ruled catalogue label, using the existing metric colors. Keep the charts aligned and all existing observations, counts, rates, definitions, and synthetic scope. Preserve the restrained layout, accessible summaries, row headings, and mobile stacking. Change neither the purpose nor the supplied data. This pass makes only these local improvements; it does not add controls, comparisons, or decorative copy.
