The original is legible, numerically sound and restrained, but its identity is mostly costume: muted green, monospace and an invented “Performance office” pasted onto a conventional dashboard. The repeated weekly identifier and generic metric footnotes waste space. Boxed section numbers look like badges rather than parts of a working record.

The chart is the clearest substantive weakness. Unlabelled gridlines imply a scale without communicating one, and switching metrics silently changes that scale. Exact values help, but should not have to repair an ambiguous axis. The tiny source bars have no stated measure; their percentage widths and maximum-width cap can also distort the intended proportions. Remove them rather than add explanatory copy. “Rate” is unnecessarily vague in the tables.

Preserve the correct totals, complete daily and source tables, exact bar labels, restrained green, useful Sunday/Direct finding, and accessible metric buttons. Refine the bureaucracy/tech combination into a ruled performance record: one compact document header, numbered form fields, continuous section rules and tabular monospaced figures. Add explicit zero-based chart axes with round domains (2,500 visits; 150 signups), and update the axis alongside the selected metric. Retain all supplied figures and calculated rates. Consolidate calculation guidance once. No external data or invented comparisons are introduced.
