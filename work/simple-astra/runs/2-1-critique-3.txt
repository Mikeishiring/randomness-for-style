The remaining weakness is originality: this is still a conventional editorial dashboard, with Weather and Disco concentrated in a custom cloud/mirror-ball illustration, a tiled masthead mark and faceted bars. The typography and section structure are familiar. It would not win originality through layout alone.

That is not, by itself, a useful reason to change it. The requested purpose is understanding a week of website performance. The current result has a literal finding, all three weekly measures, exact daily values, a clearly selected visits/signups control, a real zero-based scale, comparable source bars and a full daily table. The decorative identity does not imply an extra metric. Its strongest fusion is now a recognizable cloud crossing a mirrored sun, echoed in the bars. More motifs, weather labels or disco effects would consume attention without improving understanding.

Clarity is already sufficient for this dataset. Source counts and signup rates remain together; the chart interpretation follows the selected measure; the rounded rates and synthetic origin are disclosed once. The small-screen suppression of the large illustration is a reasonable tradeoff for reading space. The stylesheet contains obsolete overrides, but that is implementation debt rather than a demonstrated defect in the visual report. Rewriting it during an aesthetic refinement would not substantiate a better design.

No further change is justified by this critique. Preserve the current complete HTML byte for byte rather than manufacture another revision for novelty. The purpose, useful detail, data and strong decisions remain unchanged.
