The design has reached a useful visual limit. Its filed header, ruled metric fields, mounted beetle, and paired specimen trays express both ingredients without requiring the reader to learn a fictional institution. It is not radically original, but forcing more originality now would add props rather than improve the report. The plain title, direct source headings, exact counts, restrained findings, and accessible summaries are stronger than any additional thematic language would be.

One regression deserves correction: the signup-rate heading now separates the signup bars from their day labels. That weakens the primary chart and puts its weekday baseline lower than the visits chart. Restore the weekday labels immediately below both charts, with identical padding. Place the daily signup-rate heading after those labels and before the percentage strip. The heading's small gap is less harmful than disconnecting the chart's axis labels. Keep the rate strip's corrected horizontal alignment.

No other visual change is justified. Preserve all figures, formulas, source bars beneath visit counts, responsive rules, synthetic scope, observations, and the existing identity. This is a narrowly scoped refinement, not another stylistic reset.
