Originality: the result is still a restrained performance dashboard, not a radically new visual format. Its serif typography and generous dark surfaces are familiar luxury cues. The identity becomes specific through their combination with pale luminous data bars, the irregular glowing medallion and the faint engraved depth contours. Those details are small, but appropriately so: a website owner needs to read the week, not decipher an underwater setting. More literal hotel or ocean imagery would consume attention without improving the report.

Clarity: the previous revision removed the most defensible weaknesses. The title identifies the subject, the daily heading is literal, the complete conversion label replaces an abbreviation, and colored table headers make the redundant legend unnecessary. The numerical columns now have explicit space. Daily figures, source figures, aggregate totals, calculated conversion rates and the two supported findings remain available without interactions. Synthetic status and the calculation rule are consolidated.

Remaining limitations are acceptable tradeoffs. Visit bars are supporting comparisons rather than fully labeled charts; the adjacent exact counts supply their meaning. The two sections have different row structures because seven daily observations and three acquisition sources serve different comparisons. A single unified table would flatten that distinction. The identity is deliberately restrained rather than theatrical.

No further change is justified in this pass. Additional decoration would compete with the information; additional copy would repeat visible data; reducing the detail would weaken the owner's understanding.
