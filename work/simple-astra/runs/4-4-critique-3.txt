The design now has a coherent identity, but its polish is uneven. The source ledger aligns values while leaving its Visits heading offset from those values. The daily ledger has a similar mismatch on phones because its heading retains desktop padding. Tiny 10px mobile headings prioritize fitting the layout over reading it. The daily row labels also lack the semantic headers already present in the source table. These defects undermine the precision the motorsport ingredient is meant to express.

Originality has reached a sensible limit for this report: the arched monogram and serif folio treatment supply the hotel character; the timing-board totals, numbered sections and checkered period mark supply motorsport. It is still a restrained dashboard, not a radically original composition, but further themed decoration would compete with the data. Preserve those decisions rather than add another motif.

Refine alignment, legibility and structure. Match both Visits headings to their numeric columns, raise table headings to 12px on larger screens and 11px on phones, stack the two ledgers sooner to protect their column widths, and give every day a semantic row header while preserving its existing appearance and Sunday's emphasis. All totals, daily figures, source figures, share bars, conversion rates, findings and caveats remain unchanged. No new facts or controls are introduced.
