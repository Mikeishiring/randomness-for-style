The previous refinement is coherent, but it should not claim radical originality. Its palette and serif remain familiar editorial choices. The identity now comes from the relationship between an operatic playbill title and an administrative return: double rules, numbered sections, a boxed reporting period, and restrained tables. More stage metaphors, seals or curtain decoration would turn that useful contrast into costume. The current level is enough for a report whose first job is comprehension.

One concrete clarity failure remains in the layout code. Between the smallest breakpoint and the wider mobile layouts, the long title, fixed-width period box and gap can exceed the available width. The title row needs to wrap rather than depend on the 400px breakpoint. There is also an accessibility gap: day and source names are ordinary table cells, so the relationship between each row label and its figures is not encoded. Finally, 'From zero' is less precise than the original 'Baseline: 0'; the earlier wording change weakened a useful chart label.

This revision is deliberately small. The title row can wrap, preserving the playbill hierarchy and boxed period without forcing horizontal overflow. Day, source and total labels become semantic row headers, with explicit styles retaining the existing visual hierarchy. The chart baseline label is restored. The strong decisions remain intact: all figures, full daily and source tables, exact bar labels, visibly paired source bars and visit counts, totals, findings and calculation method. No additional theatrical ornament or explanatory prose is justified. These are source-level refinements; no rendered-browser verification was performed.
