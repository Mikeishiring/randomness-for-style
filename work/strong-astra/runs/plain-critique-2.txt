The ledger idea is only half implemented. The page still follows a standard KPI strip / bar chart / table dashboard, with printer-like rules pasted over it. The daily chart duplicates a neighboring table and makes the owner translate between horizontal day labels and vertical rows. Source bars use a different reading direction again. The wordmark remains ordinary UI typography, while oversized totals spend more space asserting importance than helping comparison. This is tidy, but the visual concept has not earned its place.

The revision makes the ledger structural. Each day now occupies one ruled row, with its selected metric drawn directly beside its complete numerical record. The same left-to-right reading order continues into traffic sources. All daily visits, signups and rates remain visible; the Visits/Signups control changes the comparison bars and their explicit zero-based scale. Removing the duplicate chart and weekly-total footer makes room for a quieter, denser page. A compact serif masthead and monospaced numerical columns distinguish the weekly record without adding ornamental labels. Sunday receives the same pale-blue emphasis as the finding because it leads both selectable measures. The underlying figures, weekly totals, rate precision and source scope remain unchanged.
