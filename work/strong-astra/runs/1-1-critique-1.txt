The original is a competent generic analytics page with a temple icon and a disco ball attached. Remove those two illustrations and almost nothing about its layout, typography or charts suggests either ingredient. “Daily rhythm” and “spotlight” do more thematic work than the design. The separate orb consumes the most expressive space while the information stays in standard dashboard components. Two lavender callouts repeat facts already visible in the chart and table. The tiny source bars lack an explicit scale, and the differently colored Sunday bar introduces a second visit color without a legend explanation.

The revision commits to one idea: an Olympus disco record sleeve whose central chart is a seven-column temple. The classical display lettering, a shallow architectural pediment and shared baseline provide the Greek structure; mirrored rectangular cells inside the proportional visit columns and the bold condensed uppercase masthead supply disco's reflective, rhythmic character. These treatments replace the detached icons, decorative footer and generic callout boxes. The daily chart remains a zero-based, directly labeled bar chart, with signups aligned below each day; the table preserves every source figure and conversion. All visits use the same color. Weekly totals remain prominent and plainly labeled. At narrow widths, the chart has a deliberate horizontal scroll area so labels remain readable instead of shrinking to tiny text. The synthetic-data notice and rounding convention are consolidated next to the period. No external assets or new data are introduced.
