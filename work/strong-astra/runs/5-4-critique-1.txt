The original is a conventional dark dashboard wearing a sea-green palette. The circular logo, generic motivational headline, boxed table and familiar progress bars could belong to almost any premium analytics product. Luxury hotel is reduced to Georgia and a gold accent; bioluminescence is reduced to a glow on otherwise ordinary bars. The legend is particularly weak: it implies two plotted series, but only visits are plotted. The asymmetric boxed/unboxed sections lack a compositional reason.

Replace that arrangement with one clear idea: a hotel folio opening onto a luminous deep-water ledger. A warm paper summary column provides the restrained typography, rules and deliberate hierarchy of hotel stationery. The adjacent midnight field contains the actual activity, with seven luminous visit columns rising from one baseline and precise figures aligned directly beneath them. Light now represents measured visits rather than decorative branding. Signups and conversion retain explicit labels and exact values, without a misleading series legend. Sources use the same visit encoding in a compact ruled ledger. Sunday is visibly strongest; all original daily and source detail remains available.

The revision removes the invented brand, generic headline, logo and dashboard card. It retains the weekly totals, every supplied count, all displayed conversion rates and both useful observations. The small-screen view turns the daily columns into labeled table rows, retaining the same information without horizontal scrolling. No motion or remote assets are needed.
