The previous revision is clearer, but the source tray still falls back into three conventional dashboard cards. Equal widths alone do not make three separately positioned bars easy to compare: their starting points are scattered across the page. The collecting metaphor should impose a disciplined register, not merely put cream labels inside teal boxes. Mobile daily records have the opposite problem: the visit marks are squeezed into 45–74px slots while the paper labels still shrink. That sacrifices both the luminous field and numerical legibility to preserve a desktop composition.

The revision replaces the source cards with three aligned specimen rows. Names and counts occupy one fixed catalogue column, luminous visit bars share a vertical zero line, and signups and rates sit in aligned paper cells on the right. This makes the differences immediately comparable and gives the drawer one consistent registration system. On mobile, each daily specimen now gets its own full-width luminous measurement beneath its paper record, instead of a token sliver beside it. The labels retain readable space and the visits retain their proportional scale. Source rows similarly reflow into a label-and-record pair above a full-width bar. The identity is already coherent enough that adding more ornament would weaken it; this pass strengthens its rules of arrangement rather than adding another motif. Figures, totals, rates and interpretations are preserved.
