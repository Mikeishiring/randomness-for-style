The previous revision gives the opera-programme idea a structural foothold, but its supposedly purposeful margin is still mostly empty space holding Roman numerals. That is a decorative tax on the information. The findings sit below the tables like ordinary dashboard captions, while the margin offers almost no help reading the page. Arial throughout most of the ledger weakens the administrative register, and the sheet shadow adds generic card styling. The opening section points to a nonexistent heading ID. At intermediate widths, chart scale copy remains after the bars disappear.

This pass makes the programme margin carry the actual reading: Sunday and Direct receive compact italic lead statements, followed by the precise scope of each finding. The daily and source ledgers occupy the facing column. Opera contributes the relationship between a lead voice and supporting ensemble; bureaucracy contributes ruled columns, numerical alignment, monospaced entries and double-rule totals. The empty act gutter and repeated below-table captions are replaced, not supplemented. The sheet shadow is removed. All supplied counts, calculated rates, metric labels and interpretive facts remain present; the duplicate Sunday numbers in prose are unnecessary beside the exact row. The mobile layout puts each finding directly before its ledger, retains all numeric detail and removes scale text whenever its bars are hidden. The broken heading reference is corrected. This is a focused revision of the existing programme-return idea, not an additional layer of ornament.
