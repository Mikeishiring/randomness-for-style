The first revision still overclaims its transformation. A green line chart is not a weather record merely because its title says “readings.” Three little insect icons beside modern progress bars remain an illustrated dashboard, and the invented catalogue numbers add no useful information. The visual vocabulary is coherent but the two ingredients are not yet doing enough work.

This pass turns the central chart into two aligned recording strips on finely ruled instrument paper. Visits and signups now receive separate, explicitly labelled zero-based scales and share the same daily positions. That gives the weather-recorder structure a practical purpose: the owner can compare traffic and signup patterns without a misleading dual-axis overlay. All exact values and conversion rates remain in the register below. Brass identifies the signup trace while green identifies visits; the supplied totals, rates and source shares are unchanged.

The source area becomes a shallow specimen cabinet containing three inset, pinned labels rather than one flat stack of generic dashboard rows. The insects remain small specimens; the ruled divider binds each to its source measurements. Uninformative catalogue numbers are removed. The theme comes from recording and collecting structures rather than another decorative illustration or more themed prose. Mobile layouts keep complete labels and figures. No new observed data or historical comparison is introduced.
