The original is a conventional dark dashboard with an opera name, Roman numerals and a small tentacle drawing attached. Its split columns, metric strip and green rectangles could belong to almost any analytics product. The curtain does little structural work. Opera survives mostly in “closing act” and “cast”; Cthulhu mostly survives in the logo. Two separate advice blocks add bulk and imply a diagnosis the supplied data cannot establish. Repeated weekly totals and metric helper copy weaken the hierarchy.

The revision commits to an opera programme from an abyssal theatre: a warm paper sheet with a tall arch, a central tentacled seal, wine-colored serif titles and numbered programme sections. A dark arched stage contains the daily chart; its rounded rising columns echo the seal’s tentacles while preserving a shared zero baseline and exact labels. The full daily ledger sits alongside it, and the sources form a three-part billing across the programme below. This replaces the generic two-column dashboard composition rather than adding decorative cards. The chart remains readable without interpreting the theme.

Every supplied daily and source figure remains, as do weekly totals, explicit metric names and derived rates. Sunday’s highlight has a stated reason. The footer retains the synthetic-data scope, rounding convention and missing prior-week caveat. Repeated totals and generic advice were replaced with concise observations supported by these figures. Small screens stack the chart and ledger and then the source entries. No external sources, fonts, scripts or assets were used. This is one refinement, saved without subsequent editing; no browser rendering was performed.
