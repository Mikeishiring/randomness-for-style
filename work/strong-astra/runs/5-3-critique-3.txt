The submerged-score idea is now coherent enough that another wholesale theme change would be churn. Its weakest remaining choices are smaller but consequential. The title is still a strained performance pun. Oversized Roman numerals compete with the plain-language headings. The source ledger's upright row labels feel like a separate generic table. Most seriously, the connecting line and the luminous points do not share the same horizontal geometry: CSS gaps shift the point centers while the SVG assumes seven equal, gapless slots. That makes the supposedly expressive score look imprecise. The five-rule signup scale also produces fractional count ticks, an unnecessary cost of the metaphor.

The revision keeps the score and repairs those weaknesses. The title becomes “Weekly performance.” Section numbers recede; italic source names echo the programme masthead without adding ornament. The source ledger uses fixed numeric columns. Chart slots are now gapless so every SVG vertex and luminous point align at every width. The five rules use count-friendly scales of 0–2,400 for visits and 0–160 for signups, with integer intervals of 600 and 40. These are axis bounds, not additional observations; every supplied value and existing derived rate is unchanged. Small-screen title and control rows can wrap. No new decorative layer, additional data, source, or interaction is needed. The visual idea remains an operatic score made of bioluminescent data, rather than a chart surrounded by literal scenery.
