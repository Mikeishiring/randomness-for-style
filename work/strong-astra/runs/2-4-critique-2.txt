Keep the observation-board idea and make it the page's organizing structure. Replace the tall summary sidebar with a compact horizontal weekly register, giving the daily board the full page width. Align day headings, observations and readings into continuous ruled columns. Use a fine recording grid and an explicitly labelled Visits scale, and remove the area fill. Hotel character comes from the centered serif masthead, ivory register and brass-framed board, not extra symbols. Remove the repeated Sunday figures from the prose; those values already occupy the highlighted Sunday column. Preserve the complete source ledger and every daily metric. On narrow screens only, retain a keyboard-scrollable board with a short scroll instruction. This is a targeted structural refinement, not another layer of themed decoration.
