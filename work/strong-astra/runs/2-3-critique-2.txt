The previous revision overclaimed its synthesis. A blue bar chart under a rounded roof is still a dashboard chart, and the roof is a costume: it does not help explain the week. On narrow screens it also puts a scrolling rectangle inside a clipped arch. The weather identity remains mostly a colour choice. Meanwhile, the opera programme is expressed through small Roman numerals while the large billing column behaves like three conventional KPI cards.

This pass replaces those two weak decisions. The weekly summary becomes a compact crimson playbill, with centered theatrical serif billing and totals arranged as programme entries. The chart becomes an observation trace: straight daily connections, open measurement points, vertical day rules and a numbered zero-based scale. This is the familiar visual grammar of a weather recording sheet, and it makes Thursday's interruption of the rising week legible without inventing a forecast or smoothing between observations. Monospaced measurement labels distinguish the observational record from the programme typography. The arbitrary rounded enclosure is removed, giving the chart a rectangular field that also scrolls cleanly on mobile. Theatre red stays in the editorial frame; weather blue stays with visits. The exact daily and source figures, conversion rates, useful interpretations and synthetic provenance are preserved. No decorative weather symbols, invented metrics or extra thematic sections are added.
