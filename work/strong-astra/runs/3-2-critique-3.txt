The counter-and-return idea now has a real structure, so another wholesale reskin would weaken it. The weakest remaining decisions are in the register. The daily and source tables choose different column widths, making the same three metrics jump sideways between sections. That is sloppy paperwork. The pale full-length tracks make visits look like completion percentages, even though the denominator is merely the busiest day. The long condensed heading is also too dependent on the installed font fitting the narrow red panel.

Refine the same idea rather than add props. Give both registers identical explicit column widths and a shared type hierarchy, with monospaced section headings and a single perforation-style division between the daily record and its source breakdown. Remove the progress-meter tracks, retain the zero origin, and expose the visits scale in the column heading so the bars read as quantities. Break the counter title deliberately onto two lines and reduce its desktop size. Switch to the compact layout earlier so the four-column register has enough room. Keep all figures, clear metric labels, useful observations and the consolidated method/source note unchanged. No stamps, fake approvals, food illustrations or extra themed copy are needed.
