The previous refinement still cheats on the requested combination. Its underwater order board is a conventional bordered dashboard with Impact headings. The huge full-width receipt is especially unconvincing: a receipt should have a narrow, sequential reading rhythm, not spread seven short rows across the entire page. The sea influence remains almost entirely in the logo. Solid mint rectangles feel like standard chart defaults, and the extra performance headline repeats the masthead. Numbered sections alone do not make this a fast-food menu.

This pass replaces the composition with one service-window idea: a luminous menu board beside a narrow printed receipt. The dark board stacks the useful overview, daily trend and source comparison; the right-hand receipt lists daily figures and checks out with the two supplied totals. On smaller screens the receipt follows the board at full usable width. Oversized condensed figures retain the menu-board hierarchy without enclosing each KPI in its own cell. The daily columns become slender luminous forms with concentrated light at their tips, combining the repeated upright rhythm of fries with light emerging from deep water. Their height remains a linear measure of visits against a shared zero baseline; light intensity carries no additional metric. The pale receipt uses restrained monospaced typography and dotted rules instead of an oversized display heading. Redundant masthead copy is removed. Counts, clear metric labels, rates, supported findings and provenance remain intact. No decorative ocean scene, invented figures or unnecessary interaction is added.
