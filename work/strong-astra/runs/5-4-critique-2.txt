The first revision establishes a stronger identity, but its full-height cream sidebar still makes the two ingredients feel assigned to separate compartments: hotel stationery on the left, ocean dashboard on the right. The summary column is too tall for three metrics, and its empty lower half contributes little. The luminous marks remain conventional thick bars. On a phone, the entire visual encoding disappears, leaving a plain numerical list. Those are the weakest decisions.

This refinement turns the page into one continuous folio. A compact paper header groups the week, title and three totals, followed by a full-width nocturnal ledger. The visual hierarchy now follows the owner's task: weekly result first, daily activity second, source detail third. It also gives the daily comparison more width instead of reserving a permanent sidebar for very little information.

The seven visit marks become slender luminous stems with small bright tips, using light as the measured endpoint rather than bathing rectangular chart bars in glow. Their common zero baseline and maximum remain explicit. The daily header and numerical rows use fixed alignment so the metric labels actually match their values. Source marks share the same fine luminous treatment. On narrow screens the daily stems become horizontal visit lines below each day, preserving quantitative comparison and exact labels; the legend changes to match that orientation.

All previous figures, conversion rates, source details, observations and the synthetic-data disclosure remain unchanged. No new metric or invented benchmark has been introduced. The revision replaces the previous layout and marks rather than adding decorative motifs.
