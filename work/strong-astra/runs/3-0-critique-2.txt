The previous revision is more disciplined, but its composition still belongs to a generic dashboard: a chart piled above a redundant table, next to a source panel that runs out of information halfway down. The bureaucratic identity is mostly typography and rules; the technological identity is mostly a toggle. The Sunday observation is even filed under traffic sources. A serif paragraph does not fix that structural mismatch.

This revision makes the administrative register itself the visualization. Each daily row contains its day, a proportional horizontal bar, visits, signups, and signup rate, so comparison and exact inspection happen in one place. The existing metric control changes the bar column, with explicitly labelled zero-based scales, while both numeric columns stay visible. Numbered left margins identify the two schedules; both use the same full-width ruled form rather than mismatched dashboard panels. On narrow screens those margins become compact section headings. Observations are placed with their own evidence. This removes the duplicated chart/table structure and the underfilled sidebar without removing any supplied figures or calculated rates. The restrained paper, serif headings, fixed-width numerals, and functional chart column now support one clear idea: an interactive performance return.
