The ledger now governs the layout, so another wholesale redesign would be arbitrary. Its weakest remaining decision is functional: the daily bars can show signups, but the source bars are locked to visits. That makes the page unusually polished at showing traffic while making its owner do more work to compare the actual signup outcome. The blue bars also have no visual connection to the corresponding numeric column, so the selected measure must be repeatedly inferred from the heading. On narrow screens, hiding the scale name compounds that weakness. The serif identity and ruled rows already do enough; adding further period styling would only costume the data.

This pass completes the existing visual system rather than replacing it. Both ledger sections receive their own Visits/Signups selector. In each, the active numerical column shares the bars' blue ink and a faint column tint, making the visual-to-exact-value relationship explicit. Each section keeps a stable descriptive heading while its scale clearly names the selected metric, including on mobile. Source signup bars use a labeled zero-to-400 range and the supplied 314, 224 and 86 counts. The broad Sunday highlight is reduced to a restrained row-name marker so it does not compete with the active metric column. All supplied figures and derived rates are preserved. No additional narrative, icons or decorative motifs are introduced.
