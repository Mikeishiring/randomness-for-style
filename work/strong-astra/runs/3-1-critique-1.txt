The previous result looks like a polite editorial dashboard wearing a disco accessory. A small mirror ball and a purple accent do nearly all the thematic work. The Georgia headline, floating week stamp, shadowed sheet and generous separate title band feel borrowed from familiar dashboard templates. Bureaucracy contributes labels but barely governs the composition. Worse, only Sunday's bar has a mirror-tile pattern: decoration singles out a data point without a legend or a consistent visual rule.

The revised idea is a weekly statistical record presented as an official disco record sleeve. The title uses tightly set, heavy, uppercase typography against a solid violet field; a single grooved record replaces the miniature mirror ball. The week becomes the masthead's filing identifier rather than a floating badge. Ruled, shared edges organize totals, the daily chart and the two ledgers as one document. The chart's seven ruled columns and every bar's consistent tiled construction carry the disco floor into the data presentation. Matching tiled source bars make that language systematic, not a special effect reserved for Sunday.

The information remains the priority: weekly totals, metric labels, selectable daily visits/signups/conversion, exact daily and source tables, findings, synthetic-data attribution and rounding method are retained. All bars remain zero-based and proportional; tile lines are decorative, not count units. The responsive version stacks the two ledgers and retains all values. No animation or additional explanatory panels were added. The metric selector now has an explicit accessible group role. This is one refinement based on the supplied HTML and figures; no browser rendering was performed.
