The first revision calls itself a record sleeve but still looks like a pale editorial dashboard. Impact is an arbitrary nightclub shorthand; the roof floats above the chart, so the temple never organizes the page. Worse, the theme forces a 590-pixel chart onto phones. That is a real usability regression: seven days should be comparable without panning. The little capital extensions also make the chart silhouette less precise. Source figures remain in an unrelated conventional table.

This revision makes the whole page one night-time Olympus facade. The pediment moves to the masthead, the three totals form its continuous frieze, the seven mirrored visit columns occupy the central hall, and the source table shares the same inset and horizontal rules beneath them. Deep plum, warm ivory and lavender replace the beige report styling. The title uses one classical serif voice, with spaced uppercase lettering and a restrained offset edge on Olympus to suggest a disco sleeve rather than a second unrelated display font. No new mythological ornaments are added.

The chart loses its protruding capitals and mandatory minimum width. On phones, seven narrow columns still share one zero baseline; abbreviated day names and exact visit labels stay visible, with signups in the aligned row below. A short Visits label replaces the redundant axis at that size, leaving the bar lengths and direct values to communicate the scale. All supplied figures, totals, conversion labels and concise interpretations remain unchanged. The synthetic scope and rounding note remain beside Week 01. Changes are confined to visual hierarchy and responsive presentation, without adding unsupported claims or data.
