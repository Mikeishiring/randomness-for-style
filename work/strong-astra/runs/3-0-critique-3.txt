The daily register finally expresses the idea, but the source section still falls back to an ordinary table. Its columns do not align with the daily schedule, and its large source-name column wastes the space where comparative evidence could live. This makes the form feel assembled from two unrelated components. The daily-only toggle compounds that inconsistency: the page offers interactive comparison for one dimension and plain arithmetic for the other.

The revision completes the existing idea rather than inventing another visual theme. Both schedules now use the same five-column register: row label, proportional bar, visits, signups, signup rate. Column edges align across the two sections. One explicitly labelled bar-metric control governs both sets of bars, with separately labelled zero-based scales appropriate to daily and source totals. Every exact count remains in place when the metric changes. Wider row-label columns accommodate source names on small screens. The administrative form now organizes the whole report, while the technology is an actual shared analytical operation rather than an isolated widget. The paper palette, serif titles, fixed-width figures, observations, synthetic disclosure, and all supplied figures are preserved. No additional decorative bureaucracy, invented status, or external evidence is added.
