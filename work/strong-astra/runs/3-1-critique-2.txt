The first refinement overcorrected the weak theme with a large violet poster. The giant headline and literal vinyl disc claim more attention than the weekly totals. The disc is still a detachable prop: removing it changes no information or structure. That is superficial theming even if the styling is more confident. The source bars also sit under source names, leaving their encoded metric implicit. Finally, a chart whose maximum changes when switching metrics should state its current range visibly.

The second refinement makes the record sleeve a compact reporting system. A violet typographic title cell sits in the same ruled strip as the three weekly totals. Heavy, tight display lettering supplies the disco character; the shared field boundaries and monospaced labels supply bureaucracy. The standalone record illustration is removed, and the useful figures take the space formerly occupied by the oversized title. On smaller screens the title becomes one compact line above the totals.

Tiled bars remain the visual link between the daily chart and source comparisons. Source bars move into the Visits column directly beneath the counts they encode. The daily chart now names the selected metric and its zero-to-maximum range, updating with the selector. All supplied values, exact daily and source detail, conversion labels, findings and synthetic-data attribution are retained. This pass replaces the weakest decisions instead of adding more themed accessories. No browser rendering was performed.
