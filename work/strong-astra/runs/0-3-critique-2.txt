The first refinement overclaims its Cthulhu identity. Rounded chart bars are not tentacles; they are ordinary dashboard styling with an explanation attached. The oversized arched paper and the second arch around the chart compete without establishing a coherent theatre. The enlarged logo still does nearly all the literal thematic work. It reads as a tasteful heritage programme, but could be for any venue. The page also spends too much vertical space building up to the totals.

This pass replaces those weaker choices with one explicit idea: a Cthulhu proscenium enclosing the weekly performance. An integrated head and paired tentacles form the theatre opening, with the tentacles tracing its architectural sides. The headline and totals occupy the stage; space reserved at either edge keeps the frame away from the data. The compact printed masthead, serif performance headline, wine section headings and programme-like daily ledger retain Opera's typographic and editorial structure. The separate seal, outer paper arch and chart arch are removed. Bars return to square ends so the decorative metaphor does not compete with quantitative marks.

The result retains every supplied figure, the complete daily table, source shares and signup rates, the supported observations and synthetic-data caveats. The mobile frame becomes quieter with narrower gutters, while the existing chart/table stacking and compact source layout remain. The chart accessibility description no longer incorrectly assumes the table is always below it. No new data, external material, dependencies or interaction were introduced. This revision was saved once without post-save edits; it was not browser-rendered.
