The original is a generic dark analytics dashboard with a racing sticker on it. The skewed mark, “At pace” headline and footer stripes do almost all the thematic work; remove them and the composition is indistinguishable from any SaaS template. Tech is reduced to cyan and monospace. Three boxed totals, a large interchangeable bar chart, then two more boxed tables create a repetitive hierarchy. The chart and daily table duplicate the same data while the toggle hides one of the two useful measures. Unlabelled grid lines also suggest a scale without explaining it.

Replace that collection of cards with one pit-wall timing board: a compact session header, continuous numerical readout, and seven aligned daily rows. Motorsport now determines the dense timing-sheet layout, condensed italic session typography, numbered row rail and clearly marked Sunday best result. Tech determines the fixed-width numerical columns and paired telemetry tracks with explicit, stable zero-based scales. Both visits and signups remain visible alongside conversion; no interaction is required to recover useful detail. A narrower source board sits beside the daily board and uses the same column logic. Lime consistently means visits and cyan consistently means signups; conversion remains neutral. Remove the slogan, logo slashes, footer ornament and redundant explanations instead of adding more racing decoration.

All supplied daily and source figures remain unchanged, as do the totals and rounded conversion rates. The Sunday and source findings remain, condensed and placed beside their relevant data. The page identifies Week 01 and synthetic data once, defines conversion once, uses semantic tables, and reflows into a single column on small screens. No external assets or dependencies are needed.
