The original is readable and numerically sound, but its identity lives almost entirely in a small monster icon, dark teal and a slogan. Remove those and it is a standard SaaS dashboard: three boxed KPIs, interchangeable panels and Arial throughout. The rounded segmented control and soft bar corners reinforce that generic treatment. The chart's unlabeled grid adds visual structure without quantitative meaning; the source tracks need a separate sentence to explain their denominator. The footer slogan adds atmosphere without helping the owner.

The revision commits to a recovered deep-sea telemetry instrument. An elongated, tentacled circuit sigil occupies a narrow vertical spine; branching traces connect the three metrics. Engraved serif headings and compact monospaced readings make the occult and technical ingredients shape the typographic hierarchy. Rules and shared alignment replace boxed cards. One pale sea-green carries visit readings, violet distinguishes signup readings, and the dark background stays quiet.

The daily plot becomes seven aligned pairs of horizontal readings, with both visits and signups visible at once. Each metric has its own explicitly labeled zero-to-maximum scale; no cross-metric length comparison is implied. This removes the toggle and exposes all supplied daily counts without interaction. Traffic sources become a compact comparison ledger with visits, signups, rates and explicitly labeled visit shares. The daily rate table remains, preserving precise comparisons and the calculation note. A narrow-screen layout stacks the source columns without hiding data. All counts remain unchanged; percentages are calculated only from the supplied figures. No prior-week comparison or causal claim is introduced.
