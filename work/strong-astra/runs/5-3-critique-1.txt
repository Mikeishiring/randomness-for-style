The original is a standard analytics dashboard in a dark teal skin. “Pelagic Opera,” Roman numerals, Georgia, and “Fin.” name the concept without organizing the page around it. The rounded gradient bars suggest generic wellness software; the traffic-source microbars repeat numbers without making the comparison much easier. The two boxed findings compete with the actual data, and the theatrical farewell is filler. The chart also mixes a percentage-height bar with a padded flex container, making its relationship to the grid unnecessarily fragile.

The revision commits to an illuminated opera programme: a formal, centered typographic bill opens into an arched stage of daily performance, with the source ledger as a contrasting programme column. Deep-sea darkness and a restrained luminous mint carry the plotted quantities; warm paper typography and fine brass rules carry the operatic structure. The arch replaces the generic chart panel rather than adding a separate decorative motif. Daily bars have flat, bright caps and a shared zero baseline, with their heights tied directly to the same explicit plot area as the grid. The headline figures remain immediately readable, and every supplied daily and source figure remains available. Findings become short unboxed observations. A native disclosure supplies the full daily table for comparison and non-JavaScript access. Controls retain clear labels, visible focus and pressed states; the columns stack on narrow screens. No external assets or additional data are introduced.
