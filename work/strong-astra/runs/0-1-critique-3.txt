The current composition already commits to one clear idea. The symmetrical portal and creature organize the masthead; the serif wordmark and large serif totals share its character; and the daily data columns carry the mirror-tile treatment. Plain source rows deliberately keep exact visits, signups and rates easy to compare. Replacing them with an illustrated visualization would not recover missing information or improve an identified reading problem. Increasing their decoration would undermine the instruction to replace weak choices rather than accumulate decoration.
