The daily timing ledger is finally doing real work, so another wholesale theme change would be gratuitous. Its useful structural idea should survive. The source panel is the weak holdout: it still consists of three familiar dashboard widgets with little progress tracks and repeated labels. Calling it a receipt does not make it one. Its ragged signups and conversion labels also make comparison slower than the daily ledger. The narrow, dark-red daily meters are too low-contrast to earn their visual space, and the conversion formula is repeated in both the KPI strip and footer.

Refine the same pit-stop counter board instead of inventing another concept. Replace the source widgets with an actual four-column till ledger: source, visits, signups and conversion, with all numerical values aligned and their labels supplied once. Preserve the visits bars within the visits column, on the same stated zero-to-6,000 scale. The light ledger now complements the dark motorsport timing board through matching tabular organization, while the large menu-price totals and italic counter sign keep the fast-food ingredient structural. Do not add flags, food illustrations, badges, faux prices or metaphorical metric names.

Keep the full data and existing metric labels. Increase the daily meters from three to five pixels and use a lighter red on the dark board so the encoded visit lengths remain legible. Remove the duplicate conversion formula from the totals; retain its definition and rounding method once below the board. Preserve the chronological daily rows, visible conversion figures, responsive stacking and concise findings. The result is an incremental correction to the weakest remaining presentation, with no new interaction burden or decorative layer.
