The previous page has a competent dashboard skeleton, but its identity is almost entirely cosmetic: an unexplained ring mark, warm paper, green bars and rounded cards. None changes how the owner reads the week. The oversized generic headline delays the useful information; the repeated bordered panels make totals, trends and supporting detail feel equally weighted. Thin source bars under names are especially weak: their metric is unstated, and their tiny size adds little to the numbers already present. The daily chart has gridlines without a readable scale. Findings repeat values rather than exploiting the layout to make comparisons easier.

The revision commits to a weekly traffic ledger: a compact numbered masthead, strong typographic totals, ruled sections, and tabular numerical detail. One cobalt ink identifies the active chart and its quantities. The daily chart and full daily table sit together, so the trend can be checked against precise figures without searching another card. Explicit zero-based ticks make the metric switch legible. The source table becomes a full-width comparison with visits bars inside the visits column, where their meaning is clear. Rounded cards, the decorative mark, the generic hero headline and redundant source commentary are removed. All supplied visits and signups, the existing rounded rates and weekly totals are retained; no historical comparison or causal claim is introduced.
