The original is a conventional analytics dashboard wearing a burger-and-lightning logo. Cream, red, serif headings and playful section names could belong to almost any restaurant. Greek myth contributes almost nothing beyond the word Olympus. Three boxed metrics, two interchangeable panels and a full-width table make the layout feel assembled from default dashboard components. The tiny source meters have an unexplained scale; the footer slogan and repeated metric helper lines add atmosphere without meaning.

The revision commits to an Olympian roadside menu board: a temple pediment crowns one large red performance board, three architectural bays hold the metrics, and a contrasting order receipt carries the complete daily ledger. Inscription-like serif capitals and numbered menu rows combine the Greek monument with the fast-food counter. The daily chart and source table share the board instead of becoming separate cards. Clear metric labels, all supplied values, calculated rates and the two useful findings remain intact. Decorative source meters, repeated totals and filler copy are removed. The design uses a consistent cream/red palette, a zero-baseline visits chart with direct values, and a stacked mobile layout; daily details remain visible rather than hidden behind an interaction.
