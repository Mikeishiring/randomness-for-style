The first page wears its theme as a removable skin. OLYMPUS, a generic arch and an ornamental leaf do almost all the work; replace the name and it could be any boutique analytics template. “Where guests arrive” blurs a precise analytics concept without making the information more useful. The generic three-card summary, unrelated dark conclusion banner and repeated divider lines do not form a coherent visual idea. The chart's unlabeled grid suggests a scale it never explains, while the gold Sunday bar creates an unexplained exception. The source mini-bars add a second, underspecified comparison to an already complete table. Supporting copy repeats obvious scope beneath totals and provenance in the footer.

The revision commits to a single idea: the weekly folio of a hotel on Mount Olympus. A shallow pediment and an architectural seven-column chart make the Greek setting structural; serif display numerals, ruled ledger rows and a folio margin supply the hotel register. The daily display uses the same ink for every day and a visible zero-based scale. Visits and Signups remain selectable, with all seven pairs also available in a compact daily ledger. Source metrics keep literal labels and full supplied values. One source finding replaces the detached promotional-looking banner. The house emblem uses a small lightning motif for Zeus rather than a generic leaf. All data, totals, rounding and purpose remain unchanged; the theme does not rename metrics or invent commercial context.
