The original identity is mostly costume: a checkered logo, red hero and racing stripe could be removed without changing the dashboard. Opera is reduced to a serif headline and “final act”; motorsport contributes decoration rather than the timing-sheet discipline that would help compare results. The oversized banner delays the numbers, the chart duplicates a separate daily ledger, and the source bars sit in arbitrarily narrow name cells. Its unlabelled grid suggests precision without supplying a readable scale. Flex-shrinking bars can also compromise the intended height proportions.

Replace that arrangement with one idea: a race timing sheet typeset as an opera programme. A compact programme masthead, oversized editorial serif totals, numbered acts, strict monospaced result columns and a burgundy performance rail create the identity through structure. Daily figures and the selectable chart become one aligned running-order table: every day's visits, signups and conversion stay visible, while bars use an explicit zero baseline and maximum scale. Sunday receives a restrained finish-line treatment. Sources become a second, matching results table with full-width comparative bars. The dramatic serif is reserved for the title, totals and section names; small upright labels keep navigation literal. Remove the generic hero, skewed stripe, checkered logo and redundant metric notes. Preserve all supplied data, derived rates, the Sunday and Direct findings, synthetic-data disclosure, keyboard-operable metric controls and a compact mobile layout.
