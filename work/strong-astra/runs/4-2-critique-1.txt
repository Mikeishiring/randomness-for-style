The current page is a generic analytics dashboard wearing a racing flag and diner colors. Remove the flag and “served fast,” and almost nothing distinguishes it. The three boxed KPIs, side-by-side panels, conventional bars and repeated insight callouts follow a dashboard template; the ingredients decorate the template instead of determining it. Motorsport appears mainly in the checkerboard and “daily run”; fast food is almost entirely verbal. The oversized slogan gives brand copy more authority than the week’s results. The tiny footer also makes the source-bar denominator needlessly distant from the bars.

Commit to a pit-lane order board: a compact diner masthead, a large numbered week, three menu-board totals, a timing-board daily chart, and a receipt-like source ledger. Condensed, italic display lettering carries the pit-stop identity; tabular monospace makes the figures read like timing results and till totals. Red, cream and black have distinct jobs, with yellow restricted to the overall conversion and the chart metric selection. Replace floating dashboard cards and decorative checkerboards with one ruled board and an attached perforated ledger. Keep all metric labels literal and retain every daily and source figure, including conversion rates and the expandable daily table.

The chart uses a zero baseline, explicit scale endpoints, and consistent bar color across days. Source bars now encode visits against a clearly labeled zero-to-6,000 scale, alongside their exact visit and signup counts and conversion rates. Sunday’s peak and Direct’s strongest source conversion are stated briefly next to the relevant data. The layout stacks on small screens; controls retain visible focus, selected states and full accessible names. This is one refinement, not an additional decorative layer.
