The first revision solved the dashboard hierarchy but understated its central visual idea. Its “illuminated dance-floor cells” were actually one-dimensional stripes: closer to a generic audio meter than a disco floor. The uniformly slanted condensed masthead belonged almost entirely to motorsport. Pink alone was doing too much work for disco. Source bars were thinner than daily bars for no informational reason, weakening the board's consistency. Sunday's counts were repeated immediately beneath their table row, adding copy without insight.

This pass makes the existing timing-board idea physically coherent instead of adding another motif. Visits now fill a shared field of square light cells, four cells high on desktop, giving the comparison a dance-floor geometry while preserving exact proportional widths and explicit scales. Daily and source rows use the same light-field treatment. The masthead pairs broad outlined NIGHT lettering with compact, forward-slanted CIRCUIT lettering: nightclub marquee and race typography share one line. The warm ink, plum background and single pink data color remain restrained. The redundant Sunday values are removed from the finding; the table retains every count and conversion. No figures, metric labels or source ordering change. The layout remains static, self-contained and responsive, with numeric values in semantic tables and a forced-color fallback for the outlined title and bars.
