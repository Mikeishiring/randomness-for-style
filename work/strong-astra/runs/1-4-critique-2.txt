The first refinement stopped halfway. It introduced a pediment and fluted bars, then squeezed them back into the familiar dashboard split: graph on the left, table and tinted insight card on the right. That arrangement made the proposed colonnade incidental. The tiny stripes were a cosmetic treatment, while the composition still belonged to an analytics starter template. The slogan was generic hospitality copy; duplicated period, purpose and provenance labels weakened the supposed restraint. The shaded source note remained an unrelated component with no reason to exist as a card.

This revision makes the entire sheet one architectural hotel folio. The centered Olympus masthead and Zeus lightning pediment sit above a ruled metric frieze; all seven daily bars now share the full width as a legible colonnade. The source register forms the broad base below, with larger serif numerals, aligned numeric columns and plain source names. Observations are short ledger notes rather than boxed content. “The gods’ retreat” identifies the mythic hospitality idea without renaming any performance metric. Repeated masthead descriptions and footer provenance are removed; synthetic status remains in the masthead. Mobile keeps the same sequence and readable controls. All figures, zero-based chart scales, accessible chart summaries, metric switching and the daily data disclosure are preserved. No decorative assets or external dependencies are added.
