The original is a conventional analytics dashboard wearing two props: a checkerboard and a disco ball. Remove those and neither ingredient survives. The pastel bar chart, boxed KPI strip and two-column tables could belong to any SaaS product. “Your week. Up to speed.” occupies the strongest typographic position without saying what the page measures. The daily chart also makes the owner switch metrics and then consult a separate table to understand conversion. The source bars have no explicit scale, and their dots do no work.

The revision commits to a single idea: a late-night circuit timing board built from illuminated dance-floor cells. A condensed, slanted masthead sets the motorsport rhythm; a tall monospaced results table makes the timing-board structure useful. Daily visits become accurately scaled horizontal light strips within the table, so visits, signups and conversion remain visible together. The equalizer-like repetition and restrained hot-pink light give disco a structural role rather than another illustration. A shared zero-to-2,500 scale makes the daily bars comparable. Traffic sources use the same construction with a clearly stated zero-to-6,000 scale. Weekly totals run directly across the board, and Sunday is identified with a text label as well as emphasis. Decorative orb, checkerboards, generic hero copy, duplicate daily table and metric toggle are removed. All supplied counts, weekly totals and one-decimal conversion figures remain. The page explicitly identifies the dataset as synthetic and retains the conversion definition and absence of prior-week data.
