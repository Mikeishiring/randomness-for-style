The prior result is a competent generic analytics dashboard with an Ariadne logo attached. The maze in the masthead and gold thread in the closing banner supply nearly all of the mythology; neither affects how the owner reads the information. The luxury-hotel ingredient amounts to a serif font and cream background. The large KPI strip, toggle chart, sidebar table and dark takeaway banner could belong to almost any premium SaaS template. The banner also gives the weakest source more visual weight than the week's actual totals.

The weakest decision is the split between a visits-or-signups chart and a separate source ledger. It hides useful daily figures behind a toggle, then repeats conclusions in multiple prose blocks. The source micro-bars are too short to add much beyond the adjacent counts. The oversized week number consumes space without helping interpretation.

Replace that composition with one idea: Ariadne's weekly folio, a hotel register whose continuous thread leads from totals through daily entries to source entries. The thread is the section structure, not a second decorative illustration. A restrained monogram-like maze begins that thread; numbered folio entries, a narrow left rail, fine rules, aligned figures and book typography carry the hotel identity into the layout. One centered register replaces the chart/sidebar/banner arrangement.

Daily visits and signups are now visible together in a ruled ledger. In-cell bars preserve at-a-glance daily shape, each metric uses its own zero-based maximum, and explicit column labels and exact values prevent cross-metric scale ambiguity. Daily rates are derived directly from the supplied values. Source entries use the same aligned columns so the owner does not learn a second presentation. Sunday and Direct lead their respective sections; short notes identify their significance without a large callout. The Social comparison remains available in the source note. Totals, all supplied figures, source rates and synthetic-data disclosure are preserved. No historical comparisons or causal explanations are invented.

This revision was applied once. The HTML is self-contained, uses no external assets, has semantic tables, responsive spacing and a single synthetic-data disclosure. No browser rendering was performed in this constrained experiment.
