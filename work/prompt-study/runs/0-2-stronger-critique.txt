The previous page is a competent generic analytics dashboard with a themed logo. Removing the mascot would leave almost no evidence of Cthulhu or fast food. The yellow KPI strip, neutral chart area and ordinary heading could belong to any small-business dashboard. The receipt is the strongest existing idea, but it arrives as an isolated decorative treatment. The small mascot does too much of the thematic work. The red Sunday bar also introduces a second data color without a consistent informational role.

I replaced that assortment with one setting: an abyssal takeaway counter. The upper dashboard becomes a single deep-green menu board, with a rounded crown, restrained yellow double rule, heavy price-like monospace numerals and dotted source-name leaders. The existing winged, tentacled burger mascot is enlarged into the counter sign. Heavy antique serif headings and elongated rounded chart bars echo its strange silhouette while preserving a shared zero baseline and proportional bar heights. The cream daily breakdown becomes the counter's narrower itemized receipt; its type and totals follow that role rather than generic table styling. The Cthulhu ingredient now informs the sign, silhouette, dark field and typography; the fast-food ingredient determines the board-to-receipt hierarchy and presentation of numbers.

The changes replace the earlier panel treatments rather than adding ornaments, invented metrics or themed metric names. Visits, Signups and Signup rate remain literal labels. All supplied counts, daily rates, source rates and the metric-switch interaction are retained. Sunday is identified by its actual figures and a shorter observation, eliminating the arbitrary red bar. Synthetic-data attribution and rate methodology remain consolidated. Responsive rules collapse the board to one column, keep the receipt readable, and preserve visible keyboard focus. No external assets, sources or skills were used. This is one refinement pass; the revised HTML was saved once and was not subsequently edited.
